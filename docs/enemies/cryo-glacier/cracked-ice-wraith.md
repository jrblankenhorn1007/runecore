## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 48% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -31%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 32 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 38 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the ice wraith as deceptively patient for a frost beast. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
