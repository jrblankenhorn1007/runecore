## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 70% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 21 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 23 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Toxic Waste Tunnels describe the gas wretch as deceptively patient for a toxic mutant. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
