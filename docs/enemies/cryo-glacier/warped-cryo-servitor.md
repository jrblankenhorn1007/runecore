## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 52% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -25%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 18 frost damage to the first target struck.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 40 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The cryo servitor did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Frost Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
