## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 55% and 11 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 19 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 22 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The rime hound did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
