## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 55% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 81 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 115 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The swamp serpent did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
