- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 60% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -39%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 42 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the blizzard harpy has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
