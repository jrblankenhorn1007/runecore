## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 26% and 19 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 29 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the coral golem as deceptively patient for a pressure horror. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near coral deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
