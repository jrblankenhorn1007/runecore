## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 57% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 47 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the contaminated rat-king before anything else. As a corroded construct, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
