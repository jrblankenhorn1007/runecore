## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 21 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The cave bat is a cave beast native to Subterranean Caverns, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
