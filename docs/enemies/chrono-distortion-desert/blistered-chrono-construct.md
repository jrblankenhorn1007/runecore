## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 143 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 89 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The chrono construct is a temporal wraith native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
