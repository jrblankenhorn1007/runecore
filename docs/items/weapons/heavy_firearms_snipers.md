# Heavy Firearms, Snipers & High-Caliber Ordnance Catalog

This document specifies the heavy machine guns, rotary gatling cannons, anti-materiel sniper rifles, rocket launchers, and grenade launchers in Untitled RPG. These firearms offer devastating firepower, extreme range, or suppressive crowd shredding at the cost of high recoil, slow movement, and heavy ammunition consumption.

---

## 1. Firearm Mechanics & Properties
- **Ammunition Subtypes**: Utilizes Heavy Rifle Rounds (7.62mm / .50 BMG), 12-Gauge Slugs, 20mm Autocannon Shells, and 40mm Explosive Grenades.
- **Handling & Recoil**: Heavy weapons reduce movement speed while firing by 25–40% unless stabilized by Juggernaut/Gunslinger passive skills or bionic recoil dampeners.
- **Weakpoint Multipliers**: Sniper rifles feature massive base weakpoint multipliers (3.0x to 4.5x critical damage on headshots).
