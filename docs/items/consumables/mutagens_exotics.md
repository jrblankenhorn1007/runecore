# Mutagenic Organs, Bio-Essences & Exotic Ingestibles Catalog

This document specifies the bizarre, volatile, monstrous, and non-standard biological items players can consume, ingest, or absorb in Untitled RPG ("the weird stuff you eat for temporary stats") (~80 exotic consumable definitions).

---

## 1. Mechanics of Exotic Ingestion & Biological Mutation
- **Non-Culinary Ingestibles**: Raw monster glands, glowing radioactive sludge, petrified eyeballs, mutant chrysalises, void ichor, and alien brain matter that bypass the standard cooking stove.
- **Volatile Trade-Offs**: Consuming raw exotic biological matter grants massive, bizarre temporary or permanent stat mutations, often accompanied by visceral side effects (visual hallucinations, acidic reflux, involuntary shock discharges, temporary blindness, or increased hunger).
- **Synergies**: Berserkers and Symbiotes possess digestive tracts adapted to raw mutation, amplifying positive stat yields by $+50\%$ and negating nausea side effects via the *Iron Stomach* passive.
