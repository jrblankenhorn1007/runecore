## Abilities

2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the timber wolf before anything else. As a cyborg hound, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 23% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
