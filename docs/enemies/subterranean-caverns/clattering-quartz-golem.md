## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 24% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 31 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 24 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 31 damage.

## Lore
The quartz golem is a cave beast native to Subterranean Caverns, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 20% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
