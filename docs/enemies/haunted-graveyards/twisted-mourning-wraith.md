## Abilities

2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 46 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 66 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The mourning wraith is a cursed spirit native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Death Curse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
