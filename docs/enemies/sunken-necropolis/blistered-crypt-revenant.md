## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 28 damage at range.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 16 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the crypt revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This cursed guardian hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Whirl is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
