## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 26% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 43 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the metro horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
