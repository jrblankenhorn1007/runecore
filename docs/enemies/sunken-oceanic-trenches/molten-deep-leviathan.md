## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 16% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -36%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 57 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 91 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Oceanic Trenches warn newcomers about the deep leviathan before anything else. As a coral construct, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 81% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
