## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 27% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 37 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 33 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the silver adder ranks among its defining hazards. This blind predator favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
