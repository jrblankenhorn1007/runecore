## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 28% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 54 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 94 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the null serpent as deceptively patient for a solar wraith. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
