# Biome Dossier: Precursor Orbital Tether & Derelict Space Station

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Precursor Orbital Tether & Derelict Space Station (Biome 21)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-21`, Tier 6 High Orbit / Space Frontier.
- **Location & Altitude**: High orbit reached by ascending the Precursor Space Elevator cable ($Y = +1500$ to $Y = +3000$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Hard Vacuum & Decompression**: Zero atmosphere; players outside pressurized compartments deplete oxygen instantly and take 25 decompression damage/s without an EVA Space Suit or Cybernetic Life-Support Core.
  - **Zero Gravity Physics**: Character physics shift to pure Newtonian momentum: jumping propels you indefinitely until firing micro-thrusters, grabbing walls, or using magnetic grapple boots.
  - **Solar Radiation Flares & Micrometeorites**: Periodic bursts of raw solar radiation deal 50 thermal/rad damage unless hiding behind micrometeorite hull plating.
- **Ambient Temperature**: -150°C (Deep space shadow) / +120°C (Direct unshielded sunlight).

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Spacecraft Hull Armor Plating, Pressurized Airlock Bulkheads, Radiation-Shielded Glass, Solar Array Panels, Carbon-Nanotube Tether Cables.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Nanotube Fibers, Titanium Hull Scrap, Silicon Wafers.
  - **Moderate**: Aether-Infused Starmetal, Neodymium Magnets, Lithium Cells.
  - **Rare**: Neutronium Micro-Plating, Pure Antimatter Fuel, Tachyon Telemetry Relays.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Zero-G Hydroponic Mutants**: Floating orbital algae cultured in precursor greenhouses; rich in concentrated proteins.
  - **Cosmic-Ray Fungus**: Black mold growing on exterior satellite dishes that absorbs interstellar radiation.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Derelict Defense Array "Olympus-Zero"
- **Dungeon Structure**: Massive wheel-shaped orbital space station spinning slowly against the curvature of the planet below. Features decompression airlocks, zero-g maintenance tubes, central solar power distribution hubs, and high-altitude defensive battery bridges.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Repressurizing damaged compartment sectors by sealing airlock hull breaches using structural foam dispensers and routing auxiliary reactor power.
- **Dungeon Boss: Aegis-Omni "The Orbital Core AI" (Level 62 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: The spherical main reactor and primary communications array floating in zero gravity.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Hovers in the center of the zero-g sphere; rotates 4 massive solar reflector wings, firing continuous solar laser beams in intersecting patterns while releasing waves of automated drone swarms.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Activates station self-defense lockdown; vents atmosphere from the arena (zero-g eva combat), fires orbital kinetic bombardment railguns, and summons graviton fields that violently pull the player into rotating turbine blades.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Olympus-Zero Particle Beam Annihilator (T6 Energy Heavy Weapon), Zero-G Micro-Thruster Boots (Leg Augment), Neutronium Plating Ingot, 4x Precursor Datacubes, Access Key to the Infinite Void Rift.
