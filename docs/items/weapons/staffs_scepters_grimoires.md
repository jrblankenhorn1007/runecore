# Arcane Staffs, Scepters & Spell Grimoires Catalog

This document specifies the magical spellcasting implements in Untitled RPG: two-handed channeling staffs, one-handed divine/elemental scepters, enchanted spellbooks, and dark grimoires. These weapons allow direct casting of elemental spells, summoning familiars, and amplifying magic affinities.

---

## 1. Magic Implement Mechanics & Properties
- **Spell Channeling & Inherent Spells**: Each staff and grimoire possesses an innate primary spell attack that costs Mana ($MP$) to cast, as well as passive spell damage amplification affixes.
- **Offhand Synergy**: Scepters and wands are one-handed, enabling dual-casting (dual wands) or pairing with shields, spell foci, or grimoires.
- **Spell Critical & Scaling**: Spells scale with Intelligence ($\text{INT}$) for elemental/arcane damage and Wisdom ($\text{WIS}$) for healing/holy/nature damage.
