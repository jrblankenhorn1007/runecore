## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 55% and 39 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 75 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 78 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 150 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the charged wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near neodymium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
