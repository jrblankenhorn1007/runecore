## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 73 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 216 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 231 damage at range.
4. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 236 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The mire leech did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Toxin Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
