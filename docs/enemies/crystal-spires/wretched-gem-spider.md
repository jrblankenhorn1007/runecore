## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 43% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 42 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The gem spider did not always haunt Crystal Spires & Geodes; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
