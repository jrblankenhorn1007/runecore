## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 30 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 14 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the trapper ranks among its defining hazards. This beast breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
