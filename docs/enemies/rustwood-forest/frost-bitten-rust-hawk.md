## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 13% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -27%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 7 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 8 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the rust hawk as deceptively patient for a bandit raider. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
