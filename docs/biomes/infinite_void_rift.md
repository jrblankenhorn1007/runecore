# Biome Dossier: The Infinite Void Rift & Deep Abyss

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for **The Infinite Void Rift & Deep Abyss (Biome 22)** in Untitled RPG. This biome represents the ultimate endgame frontier, where the fabric of reality disintegrates into entropy, black holes, and cosmic horrors.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-22`, Tier 7 Endgame / Cosmic Horror.
- **Location & Depths**: The bottomless tears in reality found beneath the mantle ($Y < -1000$) or accessed via Void Gateways.
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Zero Reality / Cosmic Decay**: Ambient entropy constantly disintegrates matter; drains 10 HP/s and 5 Bionic Power/s unless protected by Void-Touched Obsidian, Singularity Plating, or Archon Aegis.
  - **Micro-Singularities**: Wandering miniature black holes that pull in players, blocks, and projectiles, crushing entities that touch their event horizon for 500 damage.
  - **Reversed Gravity Sectors**: Sudden gravity shifts where falling occurs upward into endless starry abysses.
- **Ambient Temperature**: Absolute Zero (-273.15°C).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Void-Touched Obsidian, Dark Matter Strata, Singularity Residue, Distorted Space-Time Shards, Pure Void Essence.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Dark Matter Flakes, Void Obsidian Shards.
  - **Moderate**: Singularity Filaments, Tachyon Resonance Crystals.
  - **Rare**: Pure Antimatter Capsules, Neutronium Micro-Plating, Genesis Bio-Catalysts.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Entropic Black Lotus**: Absorbs all ambient light; blooms with raw cosmic antimatter.
  - **Void-Tendril Weeds**: Dimensional tears that mimic plant life, lashing out at living warmth.
  - **Singularity Spores**: Fungal growths that float against the pull of gravity.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Event Horizon Citadel
- **Dungeon Structure**: Non-Euclidean citadel constructed from floating black monoliths hovering over the event horizon of a supermassive black hole. Rooms fold into one another with portal thresholds that defy 2D spatial logic.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Aligning gravitational deflection rings to stabilize space-time bridges across the accretion disk.
- **Dungeon Boss: Ouroboros the World-Eater (Level 75 Final Campaign Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Shifting platforms orbiting the swirling maw of the central singularity.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–60% HP)**: Colossal cosmic serpent coiling around the citadel; breathes black hole beams that rip through blocks, summons waves of void specters, and inverts room gravity.
  - **Phase 2 (60%–20% HP)**: Tears the arena apart into floating debris chunks; enters hyper-dimensional phase, striking from 4 directions at once while gravitational vortexes pull player toward the center.
  - **Phase 3 (20%–0% HP)**: Enters Supernova Collapse; the screen darkens to pitch black with only player and boss glowing; boss attacks with relativistic antimatter swipes; player must burst down the boss before the singularity reaches critical mass (90-second DPS check).
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Ouroboros Singularity Bow (T7 Mythic Weapon), Crown of the Void Sovereign, Pure Antimatter Core (Slot 5 Heart Augment), 5x Precursor Datacubes, Key to the Endless Endgame Crucible.
