## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 52% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -29%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 29 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the vent crawler before anything else. As a forge golem, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
