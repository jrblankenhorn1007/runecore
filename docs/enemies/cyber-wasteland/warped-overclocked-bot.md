## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 47% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -12%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 93 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 87 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers warn newcomers about the overclocked bot before anything else. As a combat drone, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Whirl is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
