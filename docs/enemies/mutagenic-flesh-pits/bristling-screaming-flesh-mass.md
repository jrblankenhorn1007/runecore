## Lore
Few who study Mutagenic Flesh-Pits dispute that the screaming flesh mass ranks among its defining hazards. This eldritch spawn guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its eldritch flesh scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
