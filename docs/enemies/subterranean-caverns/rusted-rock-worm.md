## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 42 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 21 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the rock worm ranks among its defining hazards. This crystal crawler guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
