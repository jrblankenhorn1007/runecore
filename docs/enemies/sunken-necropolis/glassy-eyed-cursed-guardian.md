## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 43% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 76 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 45 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Necropolis warn newcomers about the cursed guardian before anything else. As a undead revenant, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
