## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 19% and 116 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 311 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 148 damage at range.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 328 damage.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 126 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Chrono-Distortion Desert dispute that the dune serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This glass elemental patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its glass sand scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 77% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
