## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 9% and 2 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 12 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 6 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the rifleman as deceptively patient for a cyborg hound. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
