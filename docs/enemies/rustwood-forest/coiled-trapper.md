## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 12 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 11 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The trapper did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
