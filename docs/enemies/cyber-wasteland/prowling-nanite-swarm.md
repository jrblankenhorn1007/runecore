## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 54% and 56 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 132 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 156 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the nanite swarm as deceptively patient for a rogue ai construct. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
