## Lore
The sludge hound did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
