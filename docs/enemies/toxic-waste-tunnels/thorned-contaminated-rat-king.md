- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 66% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 91 toxin damage to the first target struck.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 84 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the contaminated rat-king before anything else. As a waste crawler, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
