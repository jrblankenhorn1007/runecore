## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 66 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -22%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 142 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The station wisp did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 36% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
