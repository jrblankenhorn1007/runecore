## Lore
The anglerfin horror is a pressure horror native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near coral deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
