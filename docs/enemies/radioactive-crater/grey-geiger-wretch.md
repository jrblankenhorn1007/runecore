## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 76 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 49 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The geiger wretch is a gamma horror native to Radioactive Crater Basin, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Radioactive Crater Basin has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
