## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the fried circuit horror as deceptively patient for a combat drone. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
