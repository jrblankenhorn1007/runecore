## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, the gestation horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near eldritch flesh deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
