## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the cathedral sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This ghost/wraith ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near peat deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
