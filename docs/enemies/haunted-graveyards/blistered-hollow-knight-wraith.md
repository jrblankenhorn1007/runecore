## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 47% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 39 damage at range.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the hollow knight wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
