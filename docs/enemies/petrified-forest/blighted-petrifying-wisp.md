## Lore
The petrifying wisp is a gorgon spawn native to Petrified Ancient Forest, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 33% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
