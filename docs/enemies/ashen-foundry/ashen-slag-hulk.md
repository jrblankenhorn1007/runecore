## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 65% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -37%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 46 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 36 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Few who study Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps dispute that the slag hulk ranks among its defining hazards. This fire elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its basalt scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
