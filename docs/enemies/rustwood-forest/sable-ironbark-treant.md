## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 15% and 21 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 44 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 57 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
4. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 37 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the ironbark treant as deceptively patient for a bandit raider. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 58% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
