## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 51% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -36%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 31 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the icicle crawler as deceptively patient for a frozen revenant. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
