- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 18% and 32 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 84 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Oceanic Trenches warn newcomers about the barnacle horror before anything else. As a coral construct, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 82% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
