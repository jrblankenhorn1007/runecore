## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 66% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -40%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 98 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 52 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 99 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the molten golem before anything else. As a ember wraith, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 22% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
