## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 74 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 49 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The rot zombie did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
