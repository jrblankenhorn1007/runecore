- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 43% and 24 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 46 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Radioactive Crater Basin dispute that the rad-storm wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This fallout beast guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its uranium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
