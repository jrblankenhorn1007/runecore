## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 37% and 95 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 375 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
4. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 150 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Local guides in Precursor Orbital Tether warn newcomers about the void sentinel before anything else. As a solar wraith, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
