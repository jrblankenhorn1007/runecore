## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 70% and 69 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 109 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 137 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Infinite Void Rift, the oblivion wretch has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
