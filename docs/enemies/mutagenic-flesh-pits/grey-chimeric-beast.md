## Lore
Few who study Mutagenic Flesh-Pits dispute that the chimeric beast ranks among its defining hazards. This enzyme ooze lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its eldritch flesh scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
