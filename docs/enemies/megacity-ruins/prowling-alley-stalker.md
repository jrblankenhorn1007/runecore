## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the alley stalker ranks among its defining hazards. This security construct favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 74% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
