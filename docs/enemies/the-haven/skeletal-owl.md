## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 11% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -10%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 27 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 25 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the owl has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
