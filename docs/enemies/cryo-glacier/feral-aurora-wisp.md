## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 65% and 37 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -32%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 58 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 125 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks warn newcomers about the aurora wisp before anything else. As a cryo wraith, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
