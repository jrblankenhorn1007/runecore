## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 56 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 62 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in The Infinite Void Rift warn newcomers about the dark matter wraith before anything else. As a abyssal titan, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 34% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
