## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 52 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 91 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
The weeping widow spirit did not always haunt Haunted Graveyards & Mire; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
