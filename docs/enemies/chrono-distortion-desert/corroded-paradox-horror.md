## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 50 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Few who study Chrono-Distortion Desert dispute that the paradox horror ranks among its defining hazards. This glass elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its glass sand scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
