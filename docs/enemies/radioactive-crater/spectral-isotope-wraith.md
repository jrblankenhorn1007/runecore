## Lore
Few who study Radioactive Crater Basin dispute that the isotope wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This mutant ghoul swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its uranium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
