## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the gaze horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its toxin weakness rewards elemental prep.
