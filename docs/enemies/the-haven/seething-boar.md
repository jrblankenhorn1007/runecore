## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 10% and 4 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 12 damage.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 9 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in The Haven (Hub Valley) warn newcomers about the boar before anything else. As a feral livestock, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 32% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
