## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 7% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The badger is a scavenger vermin native to The Haven (Hub Valley), guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Haven has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
