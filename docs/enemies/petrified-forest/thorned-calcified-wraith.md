## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 125 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 106 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 66 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the calcified wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This basilisk kin lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
