## Lore
The rift shark did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near coral deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
