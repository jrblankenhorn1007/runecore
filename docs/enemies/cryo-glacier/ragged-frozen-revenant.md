## Abilities

2. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 29 frost damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the frozen revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This ice elemental guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
