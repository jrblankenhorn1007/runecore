## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 64 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the sky whale calf as deceptively patient for a sky predator. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Void Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
