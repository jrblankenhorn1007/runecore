## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 34% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 56 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 49 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the light wisp as deceptively patient for a shard beast. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 15% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
