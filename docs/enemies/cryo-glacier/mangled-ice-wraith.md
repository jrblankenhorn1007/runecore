## Lore
The ice wraith is a frozen revenant native to Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cryo-Glacier has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
