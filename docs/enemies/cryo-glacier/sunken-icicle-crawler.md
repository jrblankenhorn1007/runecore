## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 64% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -33%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 61 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 37 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the icicle crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This frozen revenant circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
