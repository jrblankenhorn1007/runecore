## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 26% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 63 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 100 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The rot zombie is a undead revenant native to Sunken Necropolis, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
