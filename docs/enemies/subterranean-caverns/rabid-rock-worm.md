## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 34 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 77 damage at range.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the rock worm ranks among its defining hazards. This crystal crawler guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 64% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
