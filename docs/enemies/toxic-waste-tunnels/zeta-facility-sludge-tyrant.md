## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 67% and 73 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 159 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 91 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 93 damage and applying a stagger.
4. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 138 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the contaminated rat-king ranks among its defining hazards. This sludge ooze patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 51% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
