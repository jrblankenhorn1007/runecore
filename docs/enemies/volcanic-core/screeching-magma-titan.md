## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The magma titan is a magma elemental native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near obsidian deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
