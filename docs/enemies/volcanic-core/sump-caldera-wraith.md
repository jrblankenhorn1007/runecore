## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the caldera wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This molten construct hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
