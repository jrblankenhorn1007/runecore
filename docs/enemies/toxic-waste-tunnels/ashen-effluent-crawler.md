## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 62% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.

## Lore
The effluent crawler is a waste crawler native to Toxic Waste Tunnels, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Toxic Waste Tunnels has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
