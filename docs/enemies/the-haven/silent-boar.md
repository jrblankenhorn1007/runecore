## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 14% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 7 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 7 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The boar did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
