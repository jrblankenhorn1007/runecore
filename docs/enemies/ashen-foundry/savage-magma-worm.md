## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 64% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -28%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 55 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the magma worm has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
