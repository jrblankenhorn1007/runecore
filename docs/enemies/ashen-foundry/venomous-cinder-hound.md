## Lore
The cinder hound is a magma construct native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
