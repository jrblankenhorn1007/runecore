## Lore
The oblivion wretch is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 59% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
