## Defenses & Weaknesses

- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -44%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 73 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 100 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 77 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The cinder behemoth did not always haunt Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
