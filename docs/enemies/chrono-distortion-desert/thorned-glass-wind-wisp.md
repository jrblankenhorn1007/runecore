## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 20% and 43 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 123 damage.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 62 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the glass wind wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
