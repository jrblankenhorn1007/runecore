## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 45 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The charged wisp is a lightning wraith native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 21% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
