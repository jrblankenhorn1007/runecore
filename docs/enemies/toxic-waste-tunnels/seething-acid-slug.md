## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -21%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 22 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 17 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the acid slug has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
