## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 74% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 74 damage.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 61 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Toxic Waste Tunnels describe the acid slug as deceptively patient for a gas beast. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
