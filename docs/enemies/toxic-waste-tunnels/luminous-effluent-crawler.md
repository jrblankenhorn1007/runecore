## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the effluent crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This waste crawler ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
