## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 116 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the metro horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
