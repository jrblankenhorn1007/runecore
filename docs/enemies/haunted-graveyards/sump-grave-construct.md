## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 31% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 72 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 97 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 108 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the grave construct has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
