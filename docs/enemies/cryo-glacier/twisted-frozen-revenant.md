## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 54% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -25%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 40 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 32 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 19 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the frozen revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This cryo wraith ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
