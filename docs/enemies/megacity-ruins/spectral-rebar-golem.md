## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 25% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 77 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 95 damage in a 6-tile radius.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 75 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the rebar golem has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
