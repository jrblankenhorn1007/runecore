## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 68% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -59%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 160 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 153 damage at range.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 64 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the cinder behemoth before anything else. As a magma elemental, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 16% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
