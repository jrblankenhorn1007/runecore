## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 50% and 73 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 129 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 135 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Haunted Graveyards & Mire describe the hollow knight wraith as deceptively patient for a cursed spirit. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 52% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
