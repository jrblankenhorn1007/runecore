## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 36 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the glass elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
