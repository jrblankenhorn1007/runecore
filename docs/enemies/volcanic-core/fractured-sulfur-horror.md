## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the sulfur horror as deceptively patient for a molten construct. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 82% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
