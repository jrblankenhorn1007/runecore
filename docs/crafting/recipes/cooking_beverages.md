# Cooking & Beverage Recipes: Meals, Rations, Teas & Fermented Brews

This document specifies all 70 cooking, baking, butchery, and beverage recipes crafted at the Field Stove, Open Campfire, Cookpot, and Fermenter Crock in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Objects**: Field Kitchen Stove & Fermenter Crock (crafted from stone and copper pipes).
- **Proximity Requirement**: Player must be within 4 tiles of the stove or campfire.
- **Heat Source**: Requires fuel (Wood, Charcoal, Coal) to boil, bake, and roast meals.
