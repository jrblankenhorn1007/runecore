## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 18 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 12 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Rustwood Forest, the rifleman has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
