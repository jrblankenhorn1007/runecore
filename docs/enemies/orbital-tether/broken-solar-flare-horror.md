## Lore
The solar flare horror did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
