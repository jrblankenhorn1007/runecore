## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the static horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
