# Biome Dossier: Haunted Graveyards & The Cursed Mire

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for **Haunted Graveyards & The Cursed Mire (Biome 14)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-14`, Tier 3 Surface / Wetland.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Expansive foggy swamplands and derelict cemetery hills located beyond Rustwood Forest ($X = 2500$ to $X = 4000$, Surface).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Ethereal Fog**: Thick ghostly fog limits visibility to 6 tiles; player radar and minimap are scrambled while wandering through deep fog.
  - **Quicksand & Bog Sinks**: Muddy swamp pits that pull players downward; sinking past waist drains stamina and prevents jumping without a Grapple.
  - **Will-o'-the-Wisps**: Deceptive floating blue flames that lead players toward hidden pitfall traps and elite banshee ambushes.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 9°C (Damp, bone-chilling mist).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Peat Moss Soil, Bog Mud, Weathered Gravestones, Black Iron Cemetery Gates, Decaying Elm Wood.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Peat Bricks, Bog Iron, Rusted Scrap Metal.
  - **Moderate**: Lead Slabs, Silver Bullion, Quartz Shards.
  - **Rare**: Mourning Opal, Cursed Soul-Gems, Dark Aether Resin.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Witch's Hair Moss**: Hangs from dead gallows trees; used in alchemy for silencing draughts.
  - **Grave-Lilies**: White flowers blooming only over buried coffins; restores sanity and mana.
  - **Ghost-Orchids**: Rare nocturnal flowers used in potent invisibility and phase potions.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Sunken Cathedral of St. Jude
- **Dungeon Structure**: Half-submerged Gothic cathedral leaning precariously into black mire waters. Features crumbling bell towers, flooded crypts, stained-glass clerestories, and an overgrown labyrinthine graveyard.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Tolling church bells in the correct harmonic sequence to dispel protective spirit wards and open the flooded crypt vaults.
- **Dungeon Boss: Lady Vivienne the Weeping Bride (Level 34 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Flooded cathedral nave lit by ghostly candlelight, with rotten pews floating in shallow water.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Hovers over the water singing a sorrowful dirge that drains mana and slows player; flings volleys of razor-sharp spirit daggers and teleports into mirrors.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Transforms into a grotesque banshee shrieker; flies through the air tearing the roof apart, summoning a torrential rainstorm that floods the room with rising water, and dive-bombing with sonic shrieks.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Vivienne's Weeping Veil (Head Augment/Helmet), Spirit-Lash Monomolecular Whip (T3 Rogue Weapon), Mourning Soul Gem, 2x Precursor Datacubes.
