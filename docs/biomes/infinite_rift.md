# The Infinite Rift (Endgame Dimension)

The Infinite Rift is the limitless, scaling post-game dimension in Untitled RPG, accessible exclusively through the stone portal in the deepest chamber of the Haven once **The Architect** has been defeated in the Sunken Orbital Core.

---

## 1. Dimension Environment & Non-Euclidean Physics

- **Biome Type**: Extra-Dimensional Reality Tear / Boundless Endgame Dungeon.
- **Dimensional Aesthetics**: Shifting geometric terrain constructed from floating iridescent crystal slabs, floating cathedral arches, exposed starry voids, and undulating gravity vectors.
- **Infinite Floor Scaling**: Depth 1, Depth 2, Depth 3 ... $\infty$.
  - Each floor is procedurally generated using an expanded BSP leaf partitioning algorithm.
  - Monster health, damage, armor, and movement speed scale exponentially:
    $$\text{Enemy Scaling Factor} = 1.0 + 0.08 \times (\text{Rift Depth} - 1)^{1.25}$$
  - Loot drops gain elevated quality modifiers and unlock exclusive **Prismatic Tier 9+ Gear**.

---

## 2. Randomized Floor Mutators (Affixes)

Every floor of the Infinite Rift randomly rolls 2 to 4 simultaneous mutators that dramatically alter physics, combat rules, and survival dynamics:

| Mutator Name | Environmental & Combat Effect | Counter-Strategy / Synergy |
|---|---|---|
| **Solar Overdrive** | Ambient temp $= +60^\circ\text{C}$; all enemies explode in fire on death; fire damage dealt and taken $+100\%$. | Wear Molten Slag armor; deploy ice weapons. |
| **Absolute Vacuum** | Zero atmosphere: continuous breath drain unless wearing sealed helmet; ranged bullet velocity $+100\%$. | Equip deep-diver lungs or hazmat helmet. |
| **Null-Magic Singularity** | All spell mana costs doubled; energy weapons consume zero power; magic shields disabled. | Switch to ballistic firearms and bionic weapons. |
| **Vampiric Swarm** | All enemies heal for $20\%$ of all damage dealt; enemy movement speed $+25\%$. | Maintain distance; utilize high-knockback weapons. |
| **Low-Gravity Anomaly** | Jump height increased by $+150\%$; fall speed reduced by $60\%$; aerial attacks deal $+50\%$ damage. | Utilize rocket boots and mid-air hover palms. |
| **Temporal Distortion** | Reality pulses every 10 seconds, slowing game speed by $50\%$ for 3 seconds. | Exploit time slow for precision sniper weakpoints. |
| **Volatile Shock Lattice** | Ground blocks periodically conduct electricity; electric attacks chain infinitely. | Equip rubber sabatons or maintain flight. |
| **Chimeric Frenzy** | All enemies possess chimeric beast grafts (bear arms, mantis leap); melee attack reach $+50\%$. | Parrying with shields stuns frenzied attackers. |
| **Glass Cannon Realm** | Player and enemies take $+200\%$ damage, but all critical strike multipliers are doubled. | High-stealth and sniper precision builds thrive. |
| **Acidic Atmospheric Fog** | Continuous corrosive mist that degrades armor durability twice as fast. | Nanite repair hives and self-repair armor. |

## 3. Enemy Populations & Prismatic Boss Encounters

### 3.1 Scaling Rift Horrors
- **Rift Phantom**: Phasing entity that teleports behind player on every strike; deals combined void and shock damage.
- **Void-Warped Commando**: Elite precursor cyber-soldier armed with antimatter beam rifles and tracking micro-missiles.
- **Apex Chimera Avatar**: Mutated abomination possessing bear claws, dragon lungs, and chitin plating; charges relentlessly.
- **Archon Inquisitor**: Hard-light celestial guardian that deploys rotating laser walls and reflects projectile attacks.

### 3.2 Rift Guardian Bosses (Every 5th Floor)
At every 5th depth (Depth 5, 10, 15, 20...), the floor culminates in a locked confrontation against an empowered **Rift Guardian**:
- **Empowered Campaign Bosses**: Overclocked variants of campaign bosses (e.g. *Chrono-Excavator MK-Omega*, *Grafted Void-Mother*, *Singularity Vulcan*), possessing expanded attack patterns, double phase transitions, and additional elemental affinities.
- **Victory Rewards**: Defeating a Rift Guardian guarantees a drop of at least 1 **Prismatic Weapon or Armor piece**, along with *Stasis Seals*, *Pure Precursor Cores*, and *Chrono-Dust* for masterwork item crafting.

## 4. Exclusive Prismatic Tier 9+ Gear Drops

Prismatic items represent the absolute apex of power in Untitled RPG:
- **Visuals**: Iridescent rainbow borders (`#E040FB`) with dynamic particle shaders.
- **Affix Capacity**: **5 Full Affixes + 2 Unique Legendary Perks**.
- **God-Tier Synergies**:
  - *The Architect's Singularity Edge*: Greatsword that tears permanent black hole fissures on heavy swings.
  - *God-Slayer Prismatic Cuirass*: Heavy chestplate granting a dynamic energy barrier that absorbs 500 damage and reflects all hostile projectiles.
  - *Loop of Infinite Time*: Ring that automatically rewinds the player's health, stamina, and spatial coordinates by 3.0 seconds upon suffering fatal damage (90s cooldown).
