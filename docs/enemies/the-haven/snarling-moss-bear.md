## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 12% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 18 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 14 damage.

## Lore
The moss bear is a feral livestock native to The Haven (Hub Valley), patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Haven has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 20% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
