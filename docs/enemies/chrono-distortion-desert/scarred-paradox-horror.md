## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 18% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 183 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 107 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The paradox horror did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
