## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 17% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -33%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 8 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 7 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 10 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the ironbark treant ranks among its defining hazards. This feral predator favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
