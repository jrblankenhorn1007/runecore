## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 67% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 73 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The arc hound did not always haunt Magnetic Thunder-Crags; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 66% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
