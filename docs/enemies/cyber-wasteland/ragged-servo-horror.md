## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 65% and 44 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 115 damage.
2. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 74 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers warn newcomers about the servo horror before anything else. As a rogue ai construct, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Shock Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
