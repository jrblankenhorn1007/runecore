## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the cloud stalker ranks among its defining hazards. This storm elemental charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
