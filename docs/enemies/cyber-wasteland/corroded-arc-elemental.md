## Lore
Local guides in Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers warn newcomers about the arc elemental before anything else. As a arc elemental, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Caustic Spray is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
