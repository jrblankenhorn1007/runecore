## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 52% and 50 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 160 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 77 damage.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 182 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The contaminated construct did not always haunt Radioactive Crater Basin; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
