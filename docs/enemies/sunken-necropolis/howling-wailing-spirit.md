## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 43% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 115 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 89 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
4. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 163 damage at range.

## Lore
The wailing spirit did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its sound detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
