## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 28% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 30 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 32 damage at range.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the turret wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
