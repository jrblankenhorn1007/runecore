## Abilities

## Lore
Local guides in Ancient Ruined Megacity warn newcomers about the battery leech before anything else. As a security construct, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 61% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
