## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 173 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 216 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the oblivion wretch ranks among its defining hazards. This reality wraith swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
