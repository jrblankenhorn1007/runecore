## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 20% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
The glass elemental did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glass sand deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
