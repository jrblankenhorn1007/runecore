## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -4%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 115 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the cloud stalker as deceptively patient for a sky predator. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
