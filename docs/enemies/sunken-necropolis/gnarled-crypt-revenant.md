## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 94 damage.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 90 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Necropolis warn newcomers about the crypt revenant before anything else. As a cursed guardian, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 80% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
