## Abilities
1. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 50 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 97 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 82 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the bone construct has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
