## Lore
The bog toad is a glowing swarm native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
