## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 25% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 96 damage in a 4-tile radius.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 89 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 68 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The ossuary crawler did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Death Curse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
