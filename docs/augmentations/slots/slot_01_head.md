# Slot 1: Head Augmentations Catalog (24 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 24 head augmentations (Neural processors, targeting HUDs, telepathic crowns, and hive minds) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Head Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: 1 Head Augmentation installed at the Cyber-Clinic or Bio-Vat.
- **Disciplines**:
  - *Bionics*: Neural accelerators, targeting HUDs, sensory decoders. Deducts Humanity from base 100.
  - *Magitech*: Telepathic crowns, mana capacitors, mind-shielding runic plates. Grants max mana or spell reduction.
  - *Chimeric Grafts*: Swarm nodes, beast skull plates, antenna arrays. Requires stability/mana upkeep.
