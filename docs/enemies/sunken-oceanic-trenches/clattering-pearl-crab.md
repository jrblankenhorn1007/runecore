## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 18% and 69 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -39%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 97 damage in a 6-tile radius.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 157 damage.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
4. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 162 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Oceanic Trenches warn newcomers about the pearl crab before anything else. As a pressure horror, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
