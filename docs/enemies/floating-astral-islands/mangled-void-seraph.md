## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 134 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 82 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 136 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the void seraph ranks among its defining hazards. This storm elemental lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
