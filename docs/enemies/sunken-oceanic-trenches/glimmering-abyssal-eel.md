## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 154 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 170 frost damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The abyssal eel did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
