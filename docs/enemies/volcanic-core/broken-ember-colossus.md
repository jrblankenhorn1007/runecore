## Lore
The ember colossus is a magma elemental native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
