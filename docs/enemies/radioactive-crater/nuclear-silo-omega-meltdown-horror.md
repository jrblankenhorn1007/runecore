## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 51% and 116 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 136 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
4. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 243 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
The half-life spawn did not always haunt Radioactive Crater Basin; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near uranium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
