# Biome Dossier: Chrono-Distortion Desert & Glass Dunes

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Chrono-Distortion Desert & Glass Dunes (Biome 17)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-17`, Tier 5 Anomalous / Desert.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Arid scorched glass desert on the western continent ($X = -4000$ to $X = -6000$, Surface), ground to vitrified green glass by an ancient orbital antimatter beam.
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Temporal Anomalies & Time Warps**: Intermittent localized time distortions; stepping into temporal rifts randomly accelerates or decelerates game time by 300%, or rewinds player position 4 seconds into the past.
  - **Blistering Glass Dust Sandstorms**: Fierce glass particle winds rip across the surface; deals 15 damage per second and shreds exposed cloth unless wearing Full Sealed Armor or Wind-Goggles.
  - **Scorching Daylight**: Daytime temperatures exceed 60°C; severe dehydration tick rate.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 55°C (Day) / -10°C (Night).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Vitrified Green Glass Sand, Trinitite Obsidian Blocks, Fulgurite Lightning Spindles, Sun-Baked Sandstone, Ancient Ruin Rubble.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Vitrified Glass Shards, Pure Quartz Sand, Calcite.
  - **Moderate**: Topaz Crystals, Titanium Ore, Fulgurite Spires.
  - **Rare**: Tachyon Resonance Shards, Chrono-Dust, Precursor Chrono-Alloy.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Chrono-Cactus**: Spines point backwards in time; harvesting needles yields time-dilation darts.
  - **Glass-Bloom Flowers**: Rigid crystalline flowers that only open during midnight glass storms.
  - **Sun-Stalker Roots**: Deep subterranean roots containing concentrated hydration fluid.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Hourglass Ziggurat of Chronos
- **Dungeon Structure**: Massive inverted sandstone pyramid whose chambers cycle through different historical eras. Rooms shift between pristine high-tech precursor facilities and crumbling ancient ruins in real time.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Manipulating temporal rewinding dials to reverse collapsed bridges and broken doors back into their intact states.
- **Dungeon Boss: Chronos the Temporal Architect (Level 56 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Enormous clockwork arena with giant rotating gear pendulums and shifting hourglass sand floors.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Fires tachyon beams that trap player in localized time-stasis bubbles; rewinds his own health if player fails to disrupt his time-anchor pylons.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Shatters the arena clock; summons past and future echoes of himself to fight simultaneously; warps player controls and speeds up projectile hazards to supersonic rates.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Chrono-Driver Tachyon Railgun (T5 Energy Weapon), Stasis Pocket Watch (Relic), Precursor Chrono-Alloy Ingot, 3x Precursor Datacubes.
