## Lore
The swamp serpent is a spore beast native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
