## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 15% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -31%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 18 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 9 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 18 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the boar before anything else. As a rogue drone, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
