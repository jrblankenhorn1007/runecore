## Lore
The gaze horror is a basilisk kin native to Petrified Ancient Forest, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
