## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 15% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 14 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
The bandit did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
