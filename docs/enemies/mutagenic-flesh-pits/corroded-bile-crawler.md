## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 63% and 84 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 131 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 303 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 270 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
4. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 130 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The bile crawler is a chimeric beast native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near eldritch flesh deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
