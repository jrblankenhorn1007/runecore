## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 1% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 22 damage.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 19 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 17 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the bramble hound ranks among its defining hazards. This scavenger vermin guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
