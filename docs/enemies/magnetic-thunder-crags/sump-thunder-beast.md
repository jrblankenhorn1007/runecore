## Abilities
1. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 101 shock damage to the first target struck.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 95 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The thunder beast is a storm elemental native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
