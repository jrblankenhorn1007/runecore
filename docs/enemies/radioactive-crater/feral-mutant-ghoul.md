## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 49% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 67 damage.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 57 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The mutant ghoul did not always haunt Radioactive Crater Basin; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near uranium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
