## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the boar ranks among its defining hazards. This territorial predator lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
