## Lore
The ember wraith is a fire elemental native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
