## Lore
The bioluminescent jelly is a coral construct native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 72% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
