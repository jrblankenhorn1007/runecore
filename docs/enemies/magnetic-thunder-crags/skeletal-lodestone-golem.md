2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the lodestone golem before anything else. As a thunder beast, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 45% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
