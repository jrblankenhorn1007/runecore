## Lore
The void horror is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
