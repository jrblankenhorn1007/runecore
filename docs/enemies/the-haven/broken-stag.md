## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 4 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -30%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 14 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the stag has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
