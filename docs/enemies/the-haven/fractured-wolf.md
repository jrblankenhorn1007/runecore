## Lore
The wolf did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
