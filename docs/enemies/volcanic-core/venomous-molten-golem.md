## Abilities
1. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 112 fire damage to the first target struck.
2. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 96 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
The molten golem did not always haunt Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Fire Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
