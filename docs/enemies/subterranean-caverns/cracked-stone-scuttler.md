## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 58 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the stone scuttler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
