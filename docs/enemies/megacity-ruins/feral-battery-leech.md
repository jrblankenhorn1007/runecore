## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the battery leech ranks among its defining hazards. This salvage scavenger favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
