# Slot 9: Right Arm Augmentations Catalog (26 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 26 right arm augmentations (Arm-mounted laser cannons, hydraulic smashers, arcane casting conduits, and rotary gun rigs) for Untitled RPG.

## 1. Mechanics of the Right Arm Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Right Arm Augmentation installed at the shoulder/elbow.
- **Role**: Operates primarily as the main offensive weapon actuator, high-energy heavy weapon arm, or primary spellcasting conduit.
