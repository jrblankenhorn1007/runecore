## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 46% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -42%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 20 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 7 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the ash wretch has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
