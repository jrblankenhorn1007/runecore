## Abilities

2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 153 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 135 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the caldera wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This lava wyrm ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
