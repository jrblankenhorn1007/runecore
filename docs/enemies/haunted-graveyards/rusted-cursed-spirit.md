## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 44% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -10%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 185 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 101 damage in a 3-tile radius.
4. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 129 damage at range.

## Lore
Local guides in Haunted Graveyards & Mire warn newcomers about the cursed spirit before anything else. As a ghost/wraith, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
