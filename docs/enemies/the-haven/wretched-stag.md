## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the stag ranks among its defining hazards. This bramble beast ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
