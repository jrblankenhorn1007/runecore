## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 83 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 118 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
4. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 139 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The soul parasite is a soul parasite native to Sunken Necropolis, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

Commonly found near bone slate deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
