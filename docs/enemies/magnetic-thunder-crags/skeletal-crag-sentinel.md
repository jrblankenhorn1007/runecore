## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 56% and 53 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 173 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the crag sentinel before anything else. As a static horror, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 29% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
