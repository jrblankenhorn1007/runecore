## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 7% and 6 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -22%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 13 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 21 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the copper-fang cur has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 23% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
