## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 82 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 65 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the cloud stalker as deceptively patient for a void seraph. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
