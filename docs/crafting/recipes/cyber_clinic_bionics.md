# Cyber-Clinic Recipes: Bionic Implants, Power Cells & Electronics

This document specifies all 85 recipes crafted and assembled at the Cyber-Clinic and High-Tech Electronics Bench in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Object**: Cyber-Clinic Surgical Table & High-Tech Electronics Bench (assembled in Haven clinic).
- **Proximity Requirement**: Player must be within 4 tiles of the Cyber-Clinic bench.
- **Power Requirement**: Station must be connected to an active generator via copper power wires.
