## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 55% and 116 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 242 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 375 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
4. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, the flesh horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Grapple chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
