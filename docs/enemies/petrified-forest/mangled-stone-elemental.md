## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 60% and 33 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the stone elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
