## Lore
Few who study Mutagenic Flesh-Pits dispute that the sinew golem ranks among its defining hazards. This flesh horror guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its eldritch flesh scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
