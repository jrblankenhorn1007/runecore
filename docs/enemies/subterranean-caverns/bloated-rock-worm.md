## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 12 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 24 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 38 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the rock worm before anything else. As a cave beast, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
