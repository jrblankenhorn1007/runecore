## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the fried circuit horror as deceptively patient for a arc elemental. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
