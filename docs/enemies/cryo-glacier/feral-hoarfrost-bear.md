## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 56% and 34 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 110 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 127 damage in a 6-tile radius.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 60 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the hoarfrost bear ranks among its defining hazards. This cryo wraith ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
