## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 73% and 26 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 63 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 65 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the thunder beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Shock Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
