## Abilities

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the slag beast before anything else. As a fire elemental, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
