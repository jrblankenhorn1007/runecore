## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 72 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 92 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 159 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the glass elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
