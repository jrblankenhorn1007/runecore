## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 35% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 25 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 12 damage.

## Lore
The deep spider did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near granite deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
