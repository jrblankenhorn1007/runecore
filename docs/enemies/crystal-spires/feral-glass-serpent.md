## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 43% and 19 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 37 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The glass serpent did not always haunt Crystal Spires & Geodes; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
