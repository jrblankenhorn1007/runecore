## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the hollow knight wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This cursed spirit guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
