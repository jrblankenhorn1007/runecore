## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the storm wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its physical weakness rewards elemental prep.
