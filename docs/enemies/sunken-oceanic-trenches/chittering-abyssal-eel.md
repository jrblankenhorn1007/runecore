## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 26% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -30%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 47 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 62 frost damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the abyssal eel as deceptively patient for a aquatic predator. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
