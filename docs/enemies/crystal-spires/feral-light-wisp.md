## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 39 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 75 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 83 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the light wisp as deceptively patient for a crystal golem. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Grapple is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
