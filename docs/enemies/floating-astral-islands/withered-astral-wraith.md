## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 124 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the astral wraith as deceptively patient for a astral wraith. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
