## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 52 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 104 damage.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 120 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 167 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Few who study Chrono-Distortion Desert dispute that the glass elemental ranks among its defining hazards. This sand horror patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its glass sand scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its toxin weakness rewards elemental prep.
