## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 23 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 33 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 37 damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Subterranean Caverns describe the cavern bear as deceptively patient for a cave beast. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Whirl is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
