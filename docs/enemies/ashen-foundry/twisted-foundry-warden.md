## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 59% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -29%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 15 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 23 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
The foundry warden is a fire elemental native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
