## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the pressure wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This aquatic predator lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
