- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 28% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -3%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 69 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 72 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
The shroud wisp is a cursed guardian native to Sunken Necropolis, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 32% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
