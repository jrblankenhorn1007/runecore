## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the sun-touched sentinel as deceptively patient for a astral wraith. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
