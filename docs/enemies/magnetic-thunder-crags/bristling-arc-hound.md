## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 75% and 52 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 140 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 152 damage at range.

## Lore
The arc hound is a thunder beast native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
