## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 81 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
The bog toad did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
