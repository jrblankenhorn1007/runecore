## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the sunken sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This aquatic predator favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
