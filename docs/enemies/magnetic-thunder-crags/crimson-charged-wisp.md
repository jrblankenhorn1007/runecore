## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 61% and 84 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -10%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 180 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 147 damage.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 97 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
4. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 204 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Magnetic Thunder-Crags describe the charged wisp as deceptively patient for a thunder beast. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
