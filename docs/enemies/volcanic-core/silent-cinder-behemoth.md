## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 64% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -40%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 74 fire damage to the first target struck.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 83 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The cinder behemoth is a ember wraith native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
