## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 65 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 45 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Chrono-Distortion Desert dispute that the temporal wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This precursor sentinel swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its glass sand scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
