## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 101 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 59 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the gamma horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
