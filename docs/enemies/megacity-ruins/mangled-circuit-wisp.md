## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the circuit wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This salvage scavenger lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
