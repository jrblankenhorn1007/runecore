## Lore
The snow stalker is a frozen revenant native to Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cryo-Glacier has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
