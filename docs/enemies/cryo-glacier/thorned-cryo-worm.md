- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 65% and 17 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 61 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks warn newcomers about the cryo worm before anything else. As a cryo wraith, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 33% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
