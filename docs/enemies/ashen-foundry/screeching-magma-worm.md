## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 49% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -28%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 24 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 23 damage at range.

## Lore
Few who study Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps dispute that the magma worm ranks among its defining hazards. This industrial drone hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its basalt scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
