## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 12% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 20 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the copper-fang cur as deceptively patient for a wild avian. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
