## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 11% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 17 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 24 damage at range.
3. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 22 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The thorn lynx did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
