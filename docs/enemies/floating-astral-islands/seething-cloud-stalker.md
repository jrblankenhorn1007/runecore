## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the cloud stalker ranks among its defining hazards. This storm elemental lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its physical weakness rewards elemental prep.
