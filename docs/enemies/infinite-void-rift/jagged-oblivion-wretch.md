## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 104 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The oblivion wretch is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its sound detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
