## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the glacier golem as deceptively patient for a frost beast. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
