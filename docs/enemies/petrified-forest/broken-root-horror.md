## Abilities
1. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 125 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 75 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 116 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The root horror is a root horror native to Petrified Ancient Forest, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
