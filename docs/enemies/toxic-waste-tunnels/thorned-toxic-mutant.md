## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 70% and 19 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 28 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 32 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Toxic Waste Tunnels describe the toxic mutant as deceptively patient for a toxic mutant. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
