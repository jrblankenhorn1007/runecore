## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 67% and 12 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Toxic Waste Tunnels describe the fume wraith as deceptively patient for a sludge ooze. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
