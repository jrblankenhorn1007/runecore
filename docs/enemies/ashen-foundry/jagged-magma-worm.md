## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 41 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 42 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The magma worm is a industrial drone native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
