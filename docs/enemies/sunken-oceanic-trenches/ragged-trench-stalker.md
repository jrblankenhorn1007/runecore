## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 21% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -40%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 43 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The trench stalker did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
