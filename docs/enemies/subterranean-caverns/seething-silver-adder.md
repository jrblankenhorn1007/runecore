## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 53 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 43 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 53 damage in a 5-tile radius.

## Lore
The silver adder is a rock elemental native to Subterranean Caverns, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 60% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
