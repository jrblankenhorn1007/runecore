## Lore
Fieldnotes from Bioluminescent Fungal Bog describe the mire leech as deceptively patient for a glowing swarm. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glowing shrooms deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
