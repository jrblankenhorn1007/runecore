## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 8% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 16 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 11 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the owl as deceptively patient for a wild avian. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
