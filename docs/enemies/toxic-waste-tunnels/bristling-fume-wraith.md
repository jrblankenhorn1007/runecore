## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the fume wraith before anything else. As a corroded construct, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
