## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 64 damage.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the tempest guardian before anything else. As a thunder beast, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 20% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
