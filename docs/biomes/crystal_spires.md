# Biome Dossier: Crystal Spires & Prismatic Geodes

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Crystal Spires & Prismatic Geodes (Biome 13)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-13`, Tier 4 Subterranean / Crystalline.
- **Location & Depths**: Subterranean deep crust beneath mountain ranges ($Y = -300$ to $Y = -600$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Prismatic Resonance**: Crystalline walls hum at ultrasonic frequencies; casts of elemental spells cause nearby crystal clusters to violently resonate and explode into shrapnel.
  - **Reflective Facets**: Energy weapon beams, laser bolts, and magic projectiles ricochet off crystal surfaces up to 4 times, creating chaotic bullet hazards.
  - **Razor-Sharp Crystal Formations**: Walking on unmined crystal shard floors causes 20 piercing damage and severe bleed without Spiked Soles or Heavy Boots.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 18°C (Temperate, dry crystalline cave).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Prismatic Geode Blocks, Quartz Crystal Spires, Calcite Strata, Amethyst Formations, Shimmering Sand.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Quartz Crystals, Amethyst Clusters, Calcite.
  - **Moderate**: Sapphire Veins, Emerald Strata, Topaz Formations.
  - **Rare**: Prismatic Diamond Geodes, Tachyon Resonance Shards, Pure Astral Gems.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Crystalline Spore Flowers**: Petrified fungal growths that bloom with shimmering gem dust.
  - **Luminescent Crystal Stalks**: Naturally glowing quartz spires that emit radiant spectrum light.
  - **Silica Moss**: Clings to geode walls; used in high-purity glass and lens fabrication.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Hollow Geode Labyrinth
- **Dungeon Structure**: Massive hollow cavern inside a mountain-sized amethyst geode. Features shimmering crystal bridges spanning bottomless chasms, laser refraction puzzle halls, and vibrating resonance chambers.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Rotating massive optical crystal prisms to refract colored laser beams into matching color receptors to unlock ancient barrier seals.
- **Dungeon Boss: Lithos the Prismatic Sovereign (Level 40 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Huge hexagonal amphitheater lined with giant faceted mirror spires.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Fires intense light beams that refract through the mirror spires into an inescapable laser grid; spins into a diamond vortex to shred melee attackers.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Shatters his outer shell into hundreds of orbiting razor shards; gains immense attack speed and channels a room-wide Prismatic Overload beam.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Prismatic Resonance Staff (T4 Staff), Diamond-Tipped Monomolecular Claws, Prismatic Aegis Shield, 2x Precursor Datacubes.
