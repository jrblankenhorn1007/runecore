## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 24% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -29%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 51 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the salvage scavenger has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
