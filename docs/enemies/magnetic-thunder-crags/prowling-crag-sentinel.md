## Lore
The crag sentinel is a static horror native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Static Discharge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
