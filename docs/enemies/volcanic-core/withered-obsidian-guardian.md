## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 75% and 39 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -52%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 90 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the obsidian guardian ranks among its defining hazards. This molten construct favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
