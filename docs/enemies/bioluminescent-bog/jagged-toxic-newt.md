## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 53% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -8%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 55 damage.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 40 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The toxic newt is a toxic amphibian native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
