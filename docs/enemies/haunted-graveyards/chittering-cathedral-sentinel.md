## Lore
The cathedral sentinel is a grave construct native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
