## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 49% and 43 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -9%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 119 damage.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 94 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Mutagenic Flesh-Pits describe the tumor wraith as deceptively patient for a flesh horror. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
