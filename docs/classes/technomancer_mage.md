# Technomancer (The Mage / Arcane-Circuit Caster)

The Technomancer is the primary elemental spellcaster and arcane engineer of Untitled RPG, fusing ancient forgotten incantations with electromagnetic circuits, battery cells, and overclocked focusing wands.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Playstyle

### 1.1 The Archetype
Where traditional wizards study dusty tomes, the Technomancer treats magic as an arcane power current. By routing mana through superconducting wires and crystalline micro-reactors, they produce piercing plasma bolts, deploy autonomous Tesla coils, and warp localized space-time into black-hole singularities.

- **Primary Combat Role**: Ranged Elemental Magic DPS / Area-of-Effect Destruction / Battlefield Control.
- **Weapon Specialization**: Focus Wands, Spell Staffs, Arcane Grimoires, Energy Chimes, Plasma Scepters.
- **Survival Niche**: Ambient energy detection; charges electrical settlement batteries using mana; detects magical anomalies and crystal deposits; resistant to electromagnetic anomalies.

### 1.2 Baseline Attributes & Derived Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: 5 (Minimal physical carrying capacity).
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: 6 (Standard agility).
- **Vitality (VIT)**: 7 ($+70$ Max HP).
- **Intelligence (INT)**: 16 ($+32.0\%$ spell damage, $+80$ Max Mana, $+16\%$ crafting speed).
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: 10 ($+1.0\text{ MP/s}$ mana regen, $+10$ stability).
- **Cyber-Affinity (CYB)**: 6 ($+60$ Power Capacity).

**Starting Derived Pools**:
- Health: $170\text{ HP}$
- Stamina: $50\text{ Stamina}$
- Mana: $130\text{ Mana}$
- Power: $60\text{ Power}$
- Humanity: $100$
- Stability: $100$

### 1.3 Starting Equipment Kit
- **Main Hand**: Circuit-Etched Focus Wand (14 magic damage, 3.5 attacks/sec, 4 mana cost).
- **Off Hand**: Copper Conduit Battery (+15 Max Power, $+0.5\text{ MP/s}$ mana regen).
- **Armor**: Conduit Weave Robes ($+4$ Armor, $+25$ Max Mana, $+15$ shock/fire resist).
- **Consumables**: 3x Mana Battery Packs, 2x Teleport Scrolls.

## 2. Signature Class Mechanic: Spell Overclock

The Technomancer can route electrical battery power into their active spellcasting. Pressing Right Mouse Button while casting any wand attack or spell activates **Spell Overclock**:
- Consumes 10 Power per cast.
- Doubles projectile velocity and extends range by $+50\%$.
- Grants $100\%$ target penetration (pierces through all hit enemies).
- Triggers an electrical detonation upon final impact dealing $100\%$ bonus shock damage.

## 3. Class Skill Tree & Abilities

### 3.1 Active Skills (Bound to Hotkeys `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`)

#### Active Q — Arcane Plasma Bolt
- **Hotbar Slot**: `Q`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 2.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Fires a pulsating orb of superheated plasma that pierces through up to 3 enemies in a straight line, dealing $160\%$ spell damage as combined fire and arcane damage.

#### Active E — Tesla Shock Pylon
- **Hotbar Slot**: `E`
- **Resource Cost**: 25 Power or 30 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 12.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Deploys a stationary grounding coil on the floor for 10.0 seconds. The pylon zaps up to 3 nearby enemies within a 5-tile radius every 1.0 second with chain lightning, dealing $85\%$ electric damage.

#### Active R — Quantum Rift Barrier
- **Hotbar Slot**: `R`
- **Resource Cost**: 35 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 18.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Warps local space-time to project a distortion wall for 6.0 seconds. Hostile enemy projectiles passing through are slowed by $80\%$, while allied attacks passing through are amplified by $+30\%$ bonus damage.

#### Active F — Singularity Collapse
- **Hotbar Slot**: `F` (Ultimate)
- **Resource Cost**: 60 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 40.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Manifests a micro-black hole at the target coordinate for 3.0 seconds. Drags all surrounding enemies relentlessly toward its center before collapsing in a catastrophic spatial shockwave dealing $450\%$ spatial void damage.

### 3.2 Key Passive Specializations
1. *Arcane Flux*: Maximum Mana pool increased by $+15\%$.
2. *Overclocked Wands*: Wands and staffs fire $+20\%$ faster.
3. *Static Field*: Casting any spell charges the next melee or tool attack with $+25$ electric damage.
4. *Superconductor*: Chain lightning spells chain to $+2$ additional targets.
5. *Mana Shielding*: $25\%$ of incoming damage is subtracted from Mana instead of Health.
6. *Thermodynamic Transfer*: Hitting burned targets with frost spells triggers thermal shock, shattering them for $+35\%$ bonus damage.
7. *Cyber-Arcane Resonance*: Spell damage scales by an additional $+0.5\%$ per point of Cyber-Affinity.
8. *Essence Tap*: Defeating an enemy with a spell restores $4\%$ Maximum Mana and 5 Power.
9. *Plasma Core Infusion*: Energy weapon kills grant $+10\%$ spell damage for 8 seconds (stacks up to 3 times).
10. *Channeled Speed*: Channeled spells grant $+20\%$ movement speed while channeling.
11. *Archmage Circuitry*: Cooldowns of all active spells reduced by $15\%$.

## 4. Level 20 Free Signature Augmentation

### *Runic Micro-Reactor Core*
- **Body Slot**: Heart / Core
- **Augmentation Type**: Magitech Hybrid Power Cell
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 surgery credits, 0 Humanity penalty, 0 Stability upkeep).
- **Core Functionality**:
  - Replaces mortal heart with a runic fusion reactor.
  - Generates $20\text{ Power/s}$ continuously.
  - Automatically converts $50\%$ of all power generated into continuous Mana regeneration ($10\text{ MP/s}$).
  - Grants $+100$ Maximum Mana and $+50$ Maximum Power.
- **Cross-Class Exotic Surgery Trade-Off**:
  - Non-native installation penalty: $-20$ Humanity, $-20$ Stability, and $-20\%$ physical melee weapon damage unless invested heavily in Intelligence and Wisdom.
