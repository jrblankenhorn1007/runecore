## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 94 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the event-horizon sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This void horror charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 24% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
