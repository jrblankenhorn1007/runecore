## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the gorgon spawn ranks among its defining hazards. This petrified guardian patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
