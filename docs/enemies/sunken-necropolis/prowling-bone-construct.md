## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 26% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 67 damage.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 44 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The bone construct did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
