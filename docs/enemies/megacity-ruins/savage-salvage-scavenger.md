## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 21% and 19 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 45 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 22 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the salvage scavenger has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
