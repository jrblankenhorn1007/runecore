# Forge Recipes: Weapons, Ballistic Firearms & Ammunition Pressing

This document specifies all 85 weapon and ammunition recipes crafted at the Blast Forge, Smeltery, and Mechanical Ammo Press in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Object**: Blast Forge & Ammo Press (crafted from 16x Chiseled Stone and 10x Iron Ingots).
- **Proximity Requirement**: Player must be within 4 tiles of the Blast Forge.
- **Heat Requirement**: Requires active fuel in the fuel slot (Coal, Charcoal, Coke, or Magma Slag).
