## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 31% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 34 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Necropolis describe the grave wraith as deceptively patient for a cursed guardian. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Phase Step is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
