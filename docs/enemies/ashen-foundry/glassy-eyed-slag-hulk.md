## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 26 damage at range.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 31 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The slag hulk is a magma construct native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
