## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the marsh stalker ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic amphibian ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
