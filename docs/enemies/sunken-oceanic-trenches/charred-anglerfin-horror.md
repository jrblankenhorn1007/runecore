## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the anglerfin horror ranks among its defining hazards. This deep leviathan favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
