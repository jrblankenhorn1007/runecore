# The 9 Class Signature Augmentations Specification

This document specifies the 9 unique Class Signature Augmentations in Untitled RPG, detailing their native Level 20 award conditions, core mechanics, and exotic cross-class surgical acquisition rules.

---

## 1. Class Signature Augmentation System

### 1.1 Native Acquisition (Level 20 Milestone)
Every character class receives their designated signature augmentation upon reaching **Level 20**:
- **Free Installation**: Automatically installed into its designated body slot at the Haven Cyber-Clinic or Bio-Vat.
- **Zero Cost Penalty**: Native classes suffer **0 Humanity penalty**, **0 Power upkeep**, and **0 Stability upkeep**.

### 1.2 Cross-Class Exotic Surgery Rules
Non-native classes can acquire and install another class's signature augmentation (e.g. a Mage installing the Juggernaut's *Integrated Hydraulic Piston Core*, or a Berserker installing the Technomancer's *Runic Micro-Reactor Core*).
- **Prerequisites**: Must discover the rare schematic in Tier 5+ Vaults, pay 25,000 Credits, and provide 10x Titanium Ingots and 5x Biometric Adapters.
- **Incompatibility Penalty**: Non-native installations incur severe penalties:
  - Bionic signatures on casters: $-30$ Humanity, $+25\%$ spell mana cost, doubled heavy armor speed penalty.
  - Magitech/Chimeric signatures on warriors/gunners: $-30$ Stability, $2.5\text{ MP/s}$ continuous mana upkeep, and $-20\%$ physical damage unless invested in INT/WIS.

## 2. Complete Roster of 9 Signature Augmentations

### 1. Juggernaut: Integrated Hydraulic Piston Core
- **Body Slot**: Torso
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Heavy Bionic Internal Frame
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-30$ Humanity, $+25\%$ spell mana cost
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Doubles player base armor rating from all equipped armor pieces.
  - Grants permanent immunity to all physical knockback, knockdown, and stagger effects.
  - Adds $+150$ flat Maximum Health.

### 2. Berserker: Chimeric Beast Claws & Tendons
- **Body Slot**: Right Arm
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Chimeric Apex Beast Tissue
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Stability upkeep, 0 Mana upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-25$ Stability, $2.0\text{ MP/s}$ mana upkeep
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Replaces forearm with razor-sharp beast claws.
  - Adds $+25$ flat physical damage to all melee attacks.
  - Permanent $+8\%$ lifesteal on hit.
  - Melee strikes apply Deep Bleed, shredding target armor by $2\%$ per hit (up to $20\%$).

### 3. Gunslinger: Sub-Orbital Target Link
- **Body Slot**: Eyes
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Cybernetic Optical Array
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-20$ Humanity, 5 Power/s continuous upkeep
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Thermal and night vision; highlights enemies and hidden passages through walls.
  - Crosshair reveals enemy health numbers and weakpoint markers.
  - Ballistic bullets curve slightly to smart-track towards marked weakpoints.

### 4. Phantom: Nano-Wire Monomolecular Daggers
- **Body Slot**: Hands
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Subdermal Retractable Cyber-Weaponry
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-25$ Humanity, $-10\%$ physical armor rating
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Retractable monomolecular blades extend from between finger joints.
  - Attacks with these blades completely ignore all enemy physical armor.
  - Strikes executed from stealth or from behind deal $400\%$ ambush damage.

### 5. Technomancer: Runic Micro-Reactor Core
- **Body Slot**: Heart / Core
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Magitech Hybrid Power Cell
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Stability upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-20$ Humanity, $-20$ Stability
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Generates $20\text{ Power/s}$ continuously.
  - Converts $50\%$ of all power generated into continuous Mana regeneration ($10\text{ MP/s}$).
  - Grants $+100$ Maximum Mana and $+50$ Maximum Power.

### 6. Medic: Subdermal Cellular Replicator
- **Body Slot**: Skin / Dermis
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Advanced Bionic Nanite Mesh
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-25$ Humanity, 10 Power/s continuous upkeep
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Implants a dense mesh of dormant replication nanites beneath the epidermis.
  - Prevents fatal damage once every 300 seconds: upon taking lethal damage, instantly revives player to $50\%$ Max HP and emits a blinding flash that stuns nearby attackers for 3.0 seconds.

### 7. Symbiote: Symbiotic Chimeric Carapace
- **Body Slot**: Torso
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Living Chitin Beast Shell
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Stability upkeep, 0 Mana upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-30$ Stability, $2.5\text{ MP/s}$ mana upkeep
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Transforms skin into living, breathing chitinous plating.
  - Regenerates $2\%$ Maximum Health and $2\%$ Maximum Stamina every 3.0 seconds.
  - Grants $+30$ Armor and $+20$ Cold and Heat resistance.

### 8. Warden: Hard-Light Aegis Projector
- **Body Slot**: Left Arm
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Magitech Hard-Light Emitter
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-25$ Humanity, 15 Power per block
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Replaces left forearm with a deployable hard-light projector.
  - Activating ability creates a stationary 6-second dome shield blocking all hostile enemy projectiles while allowing allied attacks to shoot out freely (Cooldown: 45s).

### 9. Reanimator: Automated Minion Assembler Spine
- **Body Slot**: Nervous System
- **Augmentation Discipline**: Bionic 3D-Printing Neural Lattice
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep)
- **Non-Native Penalty**: $-30$ Humanity, 15 Power upkeep
- **Granted Abilities & Stats**:
  - Integrates automated assembly conduits along the spine.
  - Whenever an active minion (drone or thrall) is destroyed, it is automatically reassembled instantly without consuming Soul Fragments or scrap materials (Cooldown: 12s).
