## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 27% and 12 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 17 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the stone scuttler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
