## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 20% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -9%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 67 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 25 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Subterranean Caverns describe the silver adder as deceptively patient for a subterranean insect. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
