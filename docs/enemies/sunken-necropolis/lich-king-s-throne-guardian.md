## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 32% and 73 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 108 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 148 damage.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 218 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
4. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 124 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the ossuary crawler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near bone slate deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
