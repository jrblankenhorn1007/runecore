## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the quicksand horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
