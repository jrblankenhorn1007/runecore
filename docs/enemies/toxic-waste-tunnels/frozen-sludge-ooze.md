## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 40 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 37 damage at range.

## Lore
The sludge ooze did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 25% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
