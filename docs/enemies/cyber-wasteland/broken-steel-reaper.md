## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 62% and 46 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 135 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 134 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the steel reaper as deceptively patient for a arc elemental. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
