Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the runoff horror ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
