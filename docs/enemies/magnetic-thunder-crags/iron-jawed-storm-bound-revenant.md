## Lore
Few who study Magnetic Thunder-Crags dispute that the storm-bound revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This lightning wraith circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its neodymium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
