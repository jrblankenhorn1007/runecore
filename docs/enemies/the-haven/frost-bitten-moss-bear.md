## Lore
The moss bear is a bramble beast native to The Haven (Hub Valley), breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Haven has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near oak timber deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
