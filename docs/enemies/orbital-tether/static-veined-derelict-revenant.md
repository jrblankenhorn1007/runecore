## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the derelict revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This precursor guardian favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
