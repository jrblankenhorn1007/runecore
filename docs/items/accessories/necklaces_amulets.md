# Necklaces & Amulets Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, attribute enhancements, magical affinities, and unique procs for necklaces and amulets across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~100 accessory definitions). Characters can equip 1 Amulet/Necklace in their primary neck slot.

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Amulet/Necklace equipped in the Neck Equipment Slot.
- **Affix Bias**: Amulets roll higher attribute bonuses (+STR, +DEX, +INT, +VIT, +WIS, +CYB), elemental damage amplification percentages ($+10\%$ to $+50\%$), and triggerable cheat-death or resource shield procs.
