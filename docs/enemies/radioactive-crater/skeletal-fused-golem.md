## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 38% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 100 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The fused golem did not always haunt Radioactive Crater Basin; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 17% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
