## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 78% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -47%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 58 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the magma titan as deceptively patient for a molten construct. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
