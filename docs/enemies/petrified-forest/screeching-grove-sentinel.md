## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 60% and 66 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 183 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 136 physical damage to the first target struck.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
4. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 107 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The grove sentinel is a stone elemental native to Petrified Ancient Forest, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 34% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
