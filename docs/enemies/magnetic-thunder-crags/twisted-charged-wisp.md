## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 63% and 86 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 118 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 96 damage in a 4-tile radius.
4. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 183 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the charged wisp before anything else. As a thunder beast, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near neodymium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
