## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 61% and 52 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -32%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 97 damage.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 114 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 212 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The hoarfrost bear did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
