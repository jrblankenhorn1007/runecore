## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 51% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -45%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 17 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 34 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The aurora wisp did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 71% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
