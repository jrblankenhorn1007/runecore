## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 73% and 116 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -6%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 207 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 257 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the arc hound has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
