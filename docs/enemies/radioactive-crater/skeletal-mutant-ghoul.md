2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 190 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Radioactive Crater Basin describe the mutant ghoul as deceptively patient for a gamma horror. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 44% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
