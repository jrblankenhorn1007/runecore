## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 48% and 86 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 152 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 225 shock damage to the first target struck.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 166 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
4. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the arc elemental as deceptively patient for a arc elemental. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Shock Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
