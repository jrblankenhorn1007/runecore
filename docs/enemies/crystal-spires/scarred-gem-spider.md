## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 71 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 74 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the gem spider as deceptively patient for a shard beast. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
