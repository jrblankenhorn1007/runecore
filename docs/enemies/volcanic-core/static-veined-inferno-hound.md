## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 51 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 49 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The inferno hound is a magma elemental native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
