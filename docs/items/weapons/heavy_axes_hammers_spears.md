# Heavy Weapons & Melee Ordnance: Axes, Warhammers, Spears & Polearms

This document specifies the heavy two-handed axes, colossal warhammers, poleaxes, halberds, spears, and pikes in Untitled RPG. These weapons emphasize crushing physical armor, multi-target cleaves, long-range thrusts, and massive poise damage.

---

## 1. Weapon Mechanics & Scaling
- **Poise & Stagger**: Heavy weapons deal significant Poise Damage, breaking enemy super-armor and staggering bosses into vulnerable states.
- **Reach & Hitboxes**: Spears and polearms feature extended linear hitboxes with sweet-spot mechanics (dealing +25% extra damage when struck with the weapon's tip).
- **Primary Attributes**: Heavy Axes/Hammers scale with Strength ($\text{STR}$); Spears/Polearms scale with Strength and Dexterity ($\text{STR} + \text{DEX}$).
