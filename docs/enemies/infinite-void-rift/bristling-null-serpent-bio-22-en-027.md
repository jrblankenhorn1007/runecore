## Lore
The null serpent is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
