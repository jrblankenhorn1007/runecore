## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 26% and 52 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -21%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 151 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 202 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
4. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 120 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the mole horror ranks among its defining hazards. This rock elemental charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near granite deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
