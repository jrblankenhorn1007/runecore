## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 75 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 103 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the rebar golem has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
