## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 37% and 48 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 75 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 65 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the solar wraith as deceptively patient for a void sentinel. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 75% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
