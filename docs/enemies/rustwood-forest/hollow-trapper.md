## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 10 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 15 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The trapper did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
