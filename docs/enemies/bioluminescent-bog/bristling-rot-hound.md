- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 55% and 29 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 92 toxin damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Bioluminescent Fungal Bog warn newcomers about the rot hound before anything else. As a glowing swarm, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Piercing Shriek is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
