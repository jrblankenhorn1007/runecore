## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 13% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 10 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 20 damage at range.

## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the steam servitor ranks among its defining hazards. This rogue drone ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
