## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 145 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 88 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
The storm wisp is a storm elemental native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
