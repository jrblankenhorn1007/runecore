## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 59% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 86 toxin damage to the first target struck.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 176 damage at range.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the barrel horror ranks among its defining hazards. This gas beast breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
