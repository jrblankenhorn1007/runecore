## Lore
Few who study Magnetic Thunder-Crags dispute that the arc hound ranks among its defining hazards. This storm elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its neodymium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
