## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 77% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -48%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 141 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 127 damage at range.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the volcanic crab ranks among its defining hazards. This ember wraith lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
