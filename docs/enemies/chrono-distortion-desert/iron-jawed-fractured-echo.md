## Abilities

2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 89 damage.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 56 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The fractured echo is a precursor sentinel native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 42% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
