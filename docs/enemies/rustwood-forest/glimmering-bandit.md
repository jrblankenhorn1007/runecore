## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 15% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 29 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
3. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 23 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the bandit as deceptively patient for a cyborg hound. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
