## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the dune serpent has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
