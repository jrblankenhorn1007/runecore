## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 172 damage in a 5-tile radius.

## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the solar wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This precursor guardian ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 21% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
