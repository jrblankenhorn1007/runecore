## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 39% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 280 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 179 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
4. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 129 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The astral wraith is a void seraph native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Weakpoint Overload chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
