## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 30% and 35 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 50 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the current wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
