## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 28% and 35 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -32%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 92 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 80 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 62 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the anglerfin horror as deceptively patient for a coral construct. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
