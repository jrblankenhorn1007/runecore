- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 62% and 21 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 53 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 66 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The sulfur horror did not always haunt Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
