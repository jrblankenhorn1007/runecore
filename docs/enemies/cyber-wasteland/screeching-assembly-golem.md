## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 51% and 51 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 124 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 140 damage in a 6-tile radius.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 92 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The assembly golem did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Death Curse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
