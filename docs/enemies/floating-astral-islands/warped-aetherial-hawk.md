## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 49 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -2%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the aetherial hawk has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
