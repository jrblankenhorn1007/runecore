## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the quicksand horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
