## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the agate serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This stone elemental ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near petrified wood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
