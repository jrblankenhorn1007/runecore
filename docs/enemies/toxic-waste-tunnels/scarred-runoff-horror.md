## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 74% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the runoff horror ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
