## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 45% and 56 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the gamma horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Weakpoint Overload chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
