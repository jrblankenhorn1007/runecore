## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 61% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -49%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 42 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The fire elemental did not always haunt Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
