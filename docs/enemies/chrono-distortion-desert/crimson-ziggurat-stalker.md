## Lore
The ziggurat stalker did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
