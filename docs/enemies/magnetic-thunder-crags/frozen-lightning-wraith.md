## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 75% and 56 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 130 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 137 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the lightning wraith before anything else. As a storm elemental, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
