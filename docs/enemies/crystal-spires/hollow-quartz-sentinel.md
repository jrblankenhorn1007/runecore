## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 39% and 69 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 95 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 81 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 149 damage and applying a stagger.
4. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Crystal Spires & Geodes warn newcomers about the quartz sentinel before anything else. As a geode crawler, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
