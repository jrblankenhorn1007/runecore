## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 45% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -39%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 30 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 26 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 25 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the vent crawler before anything else. As a industrial drone, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
