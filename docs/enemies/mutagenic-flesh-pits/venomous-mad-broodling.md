## Lore
The mad broodling did not always haunt Mutagenic Flesh-Pits; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
