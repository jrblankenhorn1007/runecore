- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 53% and 43 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 130 damage in a 4-tile radius.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 86 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the gorgon spawn has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
