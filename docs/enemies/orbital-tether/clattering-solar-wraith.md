## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 81 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the solar wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This vacuum horror guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
