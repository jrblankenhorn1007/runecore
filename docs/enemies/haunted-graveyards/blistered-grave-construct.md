## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 36% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 89 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 90 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 50 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The grave construct is a grave construct native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
