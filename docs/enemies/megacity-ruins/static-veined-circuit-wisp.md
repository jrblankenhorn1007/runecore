## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 37% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 32 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 17 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the circuit wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
