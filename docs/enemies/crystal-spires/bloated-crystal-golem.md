## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 44% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 123 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the crystal golem as deceptively patient for a crystal golem. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
