## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the chimeric beast before anything else. As a eldritch spawn, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
