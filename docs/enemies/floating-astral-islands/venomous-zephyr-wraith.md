## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 134 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 64 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 74 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The zephyr wraith is a sky predator native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
