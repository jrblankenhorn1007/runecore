## Lore
The petrified guardian is a root horror native to Petrified Ancient Forest, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near petrified wood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
