## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the pressure wraith as deceptively patient for a aquatic predator. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
