## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 7% and 4 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -28%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 14 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 19 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 20 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The cyber-hound is a rogue drone native to Rustwood Forest, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 31% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
