## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 15% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 18 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The deep spider is a crystal crawler native to Subterranean Caverns, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
