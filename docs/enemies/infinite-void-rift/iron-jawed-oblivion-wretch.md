2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 144 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Infinite Void Rift, the oblivion wretch has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
