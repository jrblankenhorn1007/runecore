## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 56% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 32 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 65 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The charged wisp is a magnetic construct native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 21% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
