## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 29% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 52 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the circuit wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 88% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
