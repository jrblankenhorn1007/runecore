## Lore
The waste crawler is a toxic mutant native to Toxic Waste Tunnels, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Toxic Waste Tunnels has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
