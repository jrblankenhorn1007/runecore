- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 4% and 10 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 25 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 31 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The bramble hound did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 45% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
