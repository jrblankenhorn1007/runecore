## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 76% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -46%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 67 damage in a 3-tile radius.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 41 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, the fire elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 40% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
