## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 74% and 69 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -41%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 141 fire damage to the first target struck.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the cinder behemoth before anything else. As a molten construct, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near obsidian deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
