## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 48% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the rogue ai sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
