## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 39% and 63 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 138 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 103 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The solar wraith is a precursor guardian native to Precursor Orbital Tether, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Precursor Orbital Tether has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 84% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
