## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the void sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This zero-g construct guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
