# Reanimator Class Skill Tree: Necromancy, Bone Armor, Soul Harvest & Undead Armies

This document specifies all 52 class skills for the **Reanimator (Necromancer Archetype)** in Untitled RPG. The Reanimator manipulates death itself, harvesting souls from slain foes, raising skeletal warriors and cyber-corpses, forging splintering bone plate, and channeling dark entropic curses.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Resource Dynamics
- **Core Resource**: Soul Shards ($SLS$, 0–100 scale). Reaped from dying enemies or summoned via dark blood rituals. Consumed to reanimate dead matter, forge bone carapaces, and detonate corpses.
- **Army Limit & Minion Scaling**: Minion attributes scale dynamically based on the Reanimator's Intelligence, Wisdom, and Cybernetic Affinity.
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Bone Scythes, Scepters of Undeath, Grimoires of Decay, Daggers, Staffs.
