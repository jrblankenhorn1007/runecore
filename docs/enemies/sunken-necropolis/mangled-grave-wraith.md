## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 43% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 67 damage.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 82 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The grave wraith is a soul parasite native to Sunken Necropolis, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
