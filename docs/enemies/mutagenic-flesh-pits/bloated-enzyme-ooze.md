## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 94 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 75 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 71 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the enzyme ooze before anything else. As a chimeric beast, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 58% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
