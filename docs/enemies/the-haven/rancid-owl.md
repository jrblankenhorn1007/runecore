## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 11% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -27%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 10 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The owl did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
