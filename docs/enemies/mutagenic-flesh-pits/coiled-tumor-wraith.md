## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 45% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -7%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 42 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 53 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The tumor wraith did not always haunt Mutagenic Flesh-Pits; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its physical weakness rewards elemental prep.
