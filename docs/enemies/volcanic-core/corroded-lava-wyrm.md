## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 61% and 43 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -40%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 87 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 126 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 71 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the lava wyrm before anything else. As a ashen titan, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 31% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
