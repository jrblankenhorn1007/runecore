## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -21%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 128 damage at range.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 99 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The time-locked revenant did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
