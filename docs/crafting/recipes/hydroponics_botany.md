This document specifies all 40 agricultural, cloning, soil cultivation, and bio-nutrient recipes crafted at the Hydroponic Tray, Garden Plot, and Botany Synthesizer in Untitled RPG.

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Objects**: Soil Garden Troughs, Automated Hydroponic Growth Trays, Botany Synthesizer.
- **Watering & Light System**: Requires fresh water hookups (piped from rain collectors or water pumps) and grow-lights (electric UV lights or sunlight).
- **Growth Acceleration**: Automated hydroponics grow crops 2.5x faster than outdoor soil plots.
