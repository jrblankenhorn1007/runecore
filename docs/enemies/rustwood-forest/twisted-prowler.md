## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 21% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 21 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 22 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Rustwood Forest, the prowler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
