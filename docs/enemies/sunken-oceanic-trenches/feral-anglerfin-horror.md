- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 20% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -31%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 25 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the anglerfin horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Piercing Shriek is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
