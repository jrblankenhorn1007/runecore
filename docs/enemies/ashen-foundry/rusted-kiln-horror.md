## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 49% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -45%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 14 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the kiln horror as deceptively patient for a magma construct. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
