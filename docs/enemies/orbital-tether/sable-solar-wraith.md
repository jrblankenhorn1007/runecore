## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 43% and 95 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 304 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
4. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 311 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the solar wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This precursor guardian ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 41% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
