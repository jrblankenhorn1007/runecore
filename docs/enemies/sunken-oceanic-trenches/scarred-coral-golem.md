## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the coral golem ranks among its defining hazards. This deep leviathan swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
