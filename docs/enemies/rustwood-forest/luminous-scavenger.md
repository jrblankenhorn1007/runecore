## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -22%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 22 damage in a 4-tile radius.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 19 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 17 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the scavenger as deceptively patient for a cyborg hound. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 25% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
