## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 40% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 102 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 81 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The light wisp did not always haunt Crystal Spires & Geodes; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
