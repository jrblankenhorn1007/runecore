## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 30% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 98 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The turret wraith did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
