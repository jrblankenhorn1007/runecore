## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 61% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 67 damage.
2. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 69 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 38 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the toxic mutant has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
