2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The derelict revenant did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

Commonly found near hyper-alloy deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
