## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 22% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -28%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 52 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 73 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The glass wind wisp did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 22% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
