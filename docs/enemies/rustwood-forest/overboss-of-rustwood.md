- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 30 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 62 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 131 physical damage to the first target struck.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 123 damage in a 3-tile radius.
4. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the rifleman before anything else. As a rogue drone, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
