## Lore
The gloom bat did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
