## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 41% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 71 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Necropolis warn newcomers about the bone construct before anything else. As a undead revenant, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
