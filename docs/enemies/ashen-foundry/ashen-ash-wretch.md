## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 58% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -33%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 41 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 69 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the ash wretch has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
