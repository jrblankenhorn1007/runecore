## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 27% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -25%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 68 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 25 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The cavern bear did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 34% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
