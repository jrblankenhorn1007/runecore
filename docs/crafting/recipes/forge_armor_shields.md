# Forge Recipes: Metal Armor Sets, Heavy Plating & Reinforced Shields

This document specifies all 70 armor and defensive shield recipes crafted at the Blast Forge and Heavy Armorer's Anvil in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Object**: Blast Forge & Heavy Armorer's Anvil.
- **Proximity Requirement**: Player must be within 4 tiles of the Blast Forge.
- **Metalworking Mastery**: Unlocks tiered defense values, environmental cold/heat insulations, and movement speed mitigations.

---
