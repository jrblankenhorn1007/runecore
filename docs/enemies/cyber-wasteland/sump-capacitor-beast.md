## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the capacitor beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
