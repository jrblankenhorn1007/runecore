- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 71 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, the lichen beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
