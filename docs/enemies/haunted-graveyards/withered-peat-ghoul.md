- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 44% and 14 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 31 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 34 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
The peat ghoul did not always haunt Haunted Graveyards & Mire; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 18% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
