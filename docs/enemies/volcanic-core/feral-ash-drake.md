## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 64% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -47%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 51 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The ash drake is a ashen titan native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Corrosive Spit is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
