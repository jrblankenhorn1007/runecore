## Lore
The void sentinel is a vacuum horror native to Precursor Orbital Tether, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Precursor Orbital Tether has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 26% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
