# Slot 5: Heart & Core Augmentations Catalog (26 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 26 heart and power core augmentations (Micro-fission reactors, cold fusion arcs, ancient elemental mana crystals, behemoth chimera cores, and hybrid aether reactors) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Heart / Core Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Heart/Core Augmentation installed at the center of the chest.
- **The Engine of the Character**: The Heart slot supplies the primary energy economy:
  - *Bionic Cores*: Generate continuous electrical **Power Units** and increase maximum Power capacity, but deduct Humanity.
  - *Magitech Crystals*: Grant massive Maximum Mana and continuous Mana regeneration, requiring Stability.
  - *Chimeric Hearts*: Grant massive Maximum Health and biological regeneration, requiring Stability.
  - *Hybrid Cores*: Bridge both tracks, generating both Power and Mana for hybrid builds.
