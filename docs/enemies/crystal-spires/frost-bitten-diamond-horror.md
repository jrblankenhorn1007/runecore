## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 141 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the diamond horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
