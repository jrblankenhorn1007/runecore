## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the sludge hound before anything else. As a waste crawler, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
