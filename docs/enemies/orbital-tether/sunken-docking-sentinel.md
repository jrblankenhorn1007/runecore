## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Precursor Orbital Tether, the docking sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near hyper-alloy deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
