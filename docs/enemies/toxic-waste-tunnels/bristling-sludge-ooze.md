## Lore
The sludge ooze is a waste crawler native to Toxic Waste Tunnels, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Toxic Waste Tunnels has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Static Discharge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
