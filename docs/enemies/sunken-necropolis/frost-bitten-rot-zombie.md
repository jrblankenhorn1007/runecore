## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 50 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 79 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the rot zombie ranks among its defining hazards. This bone construct guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
