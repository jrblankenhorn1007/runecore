## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, the tumor wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near eldritch flesh deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
