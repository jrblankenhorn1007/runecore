# Biome Dossier: Radioactive Crater Basin & Nuclear Silo Omega

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Radioactive Crater Basin & Nuclear Silo Omega (Biome 19)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-19`, Tier 5 Hazardous / Nuclear Wasteland.
- **Location & Boundaries**: A massive 3-kilometer impact caldera formed by an ancient thermonuclear blast, situated beyond the Cyber-Wasteland ($X = -2000$ to $X = -3500$, Surface & Deep Silos).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Lethal Gamma Radiation**: Ambient radiation field measures over 500 mSv/h. Players without Lead-Lined Armor, Rad-Away Injections, or Radiation Filtration Lungs suffer Acute Radiation Sickness (maximum health continuously degraded, random genetic mutations, visual static HUD interference).
  - **Irradiated Slag Pools**: Glowing neon-green melt pools that deal 70 radiation and thermal damage per second.
  - **Nuclear Fallout Storms**: Radioactive dust winds that reduce visibility to 5 tiles and quadruple radiation accumulation rates.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 32°C (Scorched radioactive heat).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Irradiated Trinitite Glass, Lead-Contaminated Soil, Cracked Reactor Shielding Concrete, Uranium Slag Blocks, Fallout Ash.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Uranium Ore Pellets, Lead Slabs, Heavy Ash.
  - **Moderate**: Thorium Veins, Depleted Uranium Fragments, Cobalt Crystals.
  - **Rare**: Enriched Plutonium Pellets, Rad-Resistant Graphene, Metastable Isotopes.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Radiotrophic Black Mold**: Feeds directly on gamma radiation; synthesized into anti-radiation compounds.
  - **Glowing Fallout Ferns**: Emit an eerie green glow; harvestable for luminescent nuclear dyes.
  - **Mutated Giant Spores**: Pustules that burst into toxic radioactive clouds when disturbed.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Missile Silo Omega & Meltdown Reactor
- **Dungeon Structure**: Multi-level underground military missile bunker complex descending 12 levels beneath the crater floor. Features vertical missile silos with rusted gantries, blast-door airlocks, decontamination showers, control bunk rooms, and a cracked nuclear reactor chamber.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Finding and inserting lead control rods into the reactor core to lower radiation levels in flooded corridors, allowing safe access to the launch command vault.
- **Dungeon Boss: General Vance "The Meltdown Sovereign" (Level 52 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: The circular reactor chamber floor directly underneath an active, unshielded fission core surrounded by cooling ponds.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Pilots a heavily irradiated Precursor Powered Exosuit; fires dual miniguns chambered in depleted uranium rounds, fires radioactive cluster mortars, and deploys Cherenkov defense drones.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: The exosuit breaches, exposing a grotesque nuclear mutant inside; radiates a lethal heat wave across the room (forcing player to cycle cooling switches), charges across the arena with glowing molten fists, and fires sustained nuclear beam lances.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Heavy Depleted Uranium Autocannon (T5 Heavy Firearm), Lead Radiation Lining (Slot 07 Skin Augment), Nuclear Fuel Cell (Relic), 3x Precursor Datacubes.
