## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 10% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 9 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 11 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Local guides in The Haven (Hub Valley) warn newcomers about the rat-king before anything else. As a feral livestock, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
