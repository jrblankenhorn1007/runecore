## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the rot hound ranks among its defining hazards. This glowing swarm charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
