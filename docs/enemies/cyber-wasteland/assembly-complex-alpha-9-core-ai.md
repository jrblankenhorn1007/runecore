## Lore
The servo horror is a arc elemental native to Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
