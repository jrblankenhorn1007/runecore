## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 52 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -27%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 141 damage.

## Lore
The glass elemental did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
