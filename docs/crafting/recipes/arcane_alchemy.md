# Arcane & Alchemy Recipes: Chimeric Grafts, Spell Wands, Robes & Potions

This document specifies all 85 recipes crafted at the Arcane Loom, Chimeric Bio-Vat, and Alchemical Crucible in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Station Mechanics & Requirements
- **Station Object**: Arcane Loom & Bio-Vat Crucible (operated by Weaver Selene in Haven).
- **Proximity Requirement**: Player must be within 4 tiles of the Arcane Loom.
- **Mana Affinity**: Station utilizes surrounding mana resonance; does not require electrical power wires.
