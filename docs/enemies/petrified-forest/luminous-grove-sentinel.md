## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 47% and 66 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 98 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 129 damage and applying a short knockback.
4. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 205 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the grove sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This stone elemental charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near petrified wood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
