## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 48% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 20 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 13 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the bog toad ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic amphibian charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Toxin Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
