## Abilities

3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 161 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the crystal golem has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
