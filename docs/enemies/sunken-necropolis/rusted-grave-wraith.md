## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 97 damage.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the grave wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near bone slate deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
