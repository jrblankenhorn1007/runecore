## Lore
The steam servitor did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 24% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
