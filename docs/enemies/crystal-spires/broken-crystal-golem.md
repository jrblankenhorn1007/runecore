2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 51 damage at range.

## Lore
The crystal golem is a light wisp native to Crystal Spires & Geodes, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Crystal Spires has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 40% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
