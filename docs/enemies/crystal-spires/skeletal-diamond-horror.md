## Lore
Few who study Crystal Spires & Geodes dispute that the diamond horror ranks among its defining hazards. This crystal golem breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its gems scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
