## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 34% and 26 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -5%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 91 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The soul parasite did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near bone slate deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
