## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 66% and 44 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 77 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the charged wisp before anything else. As a magnetic construct, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
