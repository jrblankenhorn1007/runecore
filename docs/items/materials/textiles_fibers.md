# Textiles, Fibers & Leather Catalog

This document specifies the organic plant fibers, synthetic polymers, woven cloths, spider silks, and cured hides used for armor crafting, insulative linings, wiring insulation, and medical bandaging in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Textile & Fiber Processing
- **Procurement**: Harvested from wild cotton, flax, hemp, giant arachnid silk webs, animal pelts, and industrial nylon/kevlar scrap.
- **Spinning & Weaving**: Raw fibers are spun into threads on the Spinning Wheel, woven into cloth on the Loom, or tanned into durable armor leather at the Tanning Rack.
- **Functional Roles**: High thermal resistance (cold/heat protection), electrical wire insulation, soft armors, bowstrings, sails, and medical dressings.
