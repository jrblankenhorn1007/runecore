## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 62% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 35 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the waste crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
