## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 53% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 28 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 32 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the cryo worm as deceptively patient for a frost beast. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 34% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
