## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 50% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 28 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the gamma horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
