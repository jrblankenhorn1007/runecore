## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 49% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 85 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 148 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Radioactive Crater Basin warn newcomers about the crater crawler before anything else. As a radiation elemental, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
