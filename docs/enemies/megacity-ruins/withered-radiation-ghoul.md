## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 35% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -21%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 38 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 50 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the radiation ghoul ranks among its defining hazards. This feral mutant breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Phase Step is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
