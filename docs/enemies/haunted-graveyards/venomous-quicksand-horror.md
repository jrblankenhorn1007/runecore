## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 31% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 91 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
4. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the quicksand horror ranks among its defining hazards. This grave construct breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near peat deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
