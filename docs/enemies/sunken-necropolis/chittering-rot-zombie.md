2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 145 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 148 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the rot zombie has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
