## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 31% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 86 damage.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the cavern bear has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
