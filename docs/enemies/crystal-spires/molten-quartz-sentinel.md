## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 39% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 41 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the quartz sentinel as deceptively patient for a crystal golem. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 80% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
