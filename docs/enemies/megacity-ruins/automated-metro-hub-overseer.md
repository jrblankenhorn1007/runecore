## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 73 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 238 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
4. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 221 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ancient Ruined Megacity describe the scrap hound as deceptively patient for a feral mutant. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
