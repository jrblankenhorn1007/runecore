## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 13% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the rat-king has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
