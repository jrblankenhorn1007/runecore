## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 52% and 52 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -26%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 155 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
4. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 183 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the slag hulk has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 95% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
