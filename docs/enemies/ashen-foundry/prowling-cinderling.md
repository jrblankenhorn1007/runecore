## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 59% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -39%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 24 fire damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the cinderling has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
