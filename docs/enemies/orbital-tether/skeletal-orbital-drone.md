## Lore
Local guides in Precursor Orbital Tether warn newcomers about the orbital drone before anything else. As a precursor guardian, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
