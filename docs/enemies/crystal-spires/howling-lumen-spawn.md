## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the lumen spawn has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
