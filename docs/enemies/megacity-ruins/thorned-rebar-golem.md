3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 92 damage at range.

## Lore
The rebar golem is a radiation ghoul native to Ancient Ruined Megacity, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ancient Ruined Megacity has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Shock Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
