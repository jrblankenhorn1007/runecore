## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 55% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 80 damage.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 56 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the basilisk kin ranks among its defining hazards. This petrified guardian charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
