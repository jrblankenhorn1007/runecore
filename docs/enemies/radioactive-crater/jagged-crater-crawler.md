## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 40% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -3%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 116 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 105 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 128 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the crater crawler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
