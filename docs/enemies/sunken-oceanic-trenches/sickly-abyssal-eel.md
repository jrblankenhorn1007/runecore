## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 20% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -29%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 85 damage in a 3-tile radius.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the abyssal eel as deceptively patient for a deep leviathan. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
