## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the sarcophagus horror ranks among its defining hazards. This soul parasite lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 20% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
