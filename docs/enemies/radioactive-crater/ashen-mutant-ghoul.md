## Lore
Few who study Radioactive Crater Basin dispute that the mutant ghoul ranks among its defining hazards. This gamma horror hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its uranium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
