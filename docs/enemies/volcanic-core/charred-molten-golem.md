## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 167 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 161 damage.

## Lore
The molten golem is a magma elemental native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
