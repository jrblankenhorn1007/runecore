# Slot 4: Lung Augmentations Catalog (20 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 20 lung augmentations (Hazard filtration units, rebreathers, dragon-drake pyro glands, cryo-vents, and stamina boosters) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Lung Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: 1 Lung Augmentation installed at the Cyber-Clinic or Bio-Vat.
- **Role**: Governs environmental breath survival (toxic fog, underwater, smoke), breath attack weapons, and stamina aerobic capacity.
