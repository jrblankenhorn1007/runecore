## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 116 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The zephyr wraith is a void seraph native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
