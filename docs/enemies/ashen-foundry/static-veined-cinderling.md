## Lore
The cinderling is a forge golem native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
