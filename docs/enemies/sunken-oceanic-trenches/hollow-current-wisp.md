## Lore
The current wisp is a pressure horror native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
