## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 63% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -5%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 41 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The waste crawler is a gas beast native to Toxic Waste Tunnels, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Toxic Waste Tunnels has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
