## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 70% and 86 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 237 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 137 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 325 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
4. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the tempest guardian before anything else. As a thunder beast, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 46% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
