## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 46% and 95 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 214 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 158 damage in a 5-tile radius.
4. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the petrifying wisp before anything else. As a basilisk kin, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
