## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 46% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 15 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 19 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the mourning wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
