This document specifies all 25 left arm augmentations (Deployable riot shields, chimeric bear arms, grappling winches, flamethrower sub-assemblies, and arcane null-gauntlets) for Untitled RPG.

## 1. Mechanics of the Left Arm Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Left Arm Augmentation installed at the shoulder/elbow.
- **Role**: Operates primarily as an off-hand defensive, grappling, utility, or heavy two-handed support apparatus.
