- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 33% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -30%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ancient Ruined Megacity describe the battery leech as deceptively patient for a feral mutant. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
