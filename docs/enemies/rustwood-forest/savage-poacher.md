## Defenses & Weaknesses

- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 9 damage.

## Lore
The poacher is a cyborg hound native to Rustwood Forest, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 40% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
