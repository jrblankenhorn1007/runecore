## Lore
The static ghost is a security construct native to Ancient Ruined Megacity, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ancient Ruined Megacity has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
