# Gunslinger Class Skill Tree: Ballistics, Trick Shots, Weapon Mods & Ammunition

This document specifies all 52 class skills for the **Gunslinger (Ranger Archetype)** in Untitled RPG. The Gunslinger excels at rapid firearms manipulation, specialized ammunition synthesis, ricochet ballistics, dual revolvers, and long-range tactical snipes.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Resource Dynamics
- **Core Resource**: Focus & Ammo Reserves ($FOC$, 0–100 scale). Focus builds through uninterrupted aiming, evasion maneuvers, and landing precision weakpoint hits.
- **Reload & Ammo Mechanics**: Active reload minigame unlocks perfect reloads (instant chambering + bonus elemental damage for the magazine).
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Revolvers, Lever-Action Carbines, Shotguns, Sniper Rifles, Akimbo Handguns.

---

## 2. Complete Gunslinger Skill Catalog (52 Skills)

### 2.1 Branch 1: Deadeye Marksmanship (Precision & Sniping)
1. **GUN-01: Steady Aim (Passive, Lvl 1)**: Crouching or standing still reduces bullet spread by 50% and increases range by +20%.
2. **GUN-02: Precision Headshot (Passive, Lvl 2)**: Weakpoint hits deal +30% critical strike damage (+5% per rank).
3. **GUN-03: Sniper's Call (Active, 15 FOC, Lvl 5)**: Enters scoped focus mode; reveals enemy weakpoints through walls and guarantees a critical hit. (CD: 8s)
4. **GUN-04: Penetrating Round (Passive, Lvl 8)**: High-caliber rifle shots pierce through up to 2 additional enemies.
5. **GUN-05: Calibrated Optics (Passive, Lvl 12)**: Increases critical strike chance with rifles and carbines by +8%.
6. **GUN-06: Piercing Dart Round (Active, 20 FOC, Lvl 16)**: Fires a supersonic dart that pierces armor, ignoring 50% of target defense. (CD: 10s)
7. **GUN-07: Eagle Eye Vision (Passive, Lvl 20)**: Extends camera viewport distance while aiming ranged weapons by +30%.
8. **GUN-08: Target Painted (Active, 25 FOC, Lvl 25)**: Lasers an enemy for 10s; all ranged attacks against them gain +20% damage and 100% accuracy.
9. **GUN-09: Overpressure Cartridges (Passive, Lvl 30)**: Bullets travel 40% faster and cause a 0.5s stagger on hit.
10. **GUN-10: Decapitating Ballistics (Passive, Lvl 36)**: Killing an enemy with a weakpoint headshot explodes their skull, dealing AoE shrapnel damage.
11. **GUN-11: Kill Zone Protocol (Active, 45 FOC, Lvl 44)**: Marks a 10-tile radius; any enemy entering the zone triggers an instant automated counter-shot.
12. **GUN-12: Anti-Materiel Round (Active, 60 FOC, Lvl 52)**: Fires a devastating heavy round dealing 500% weapon damage and blowing through reinforced terrain.
13. **GUN-13: Apex Marksman (Passive, Lvl 60)**: Bullets that travel more than 15 tiles deal +50% amplified damage.
