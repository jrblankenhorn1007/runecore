## Lore
The void seraph is a storm elemental native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Void Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
