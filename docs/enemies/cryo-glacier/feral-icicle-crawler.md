## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 59% and 37 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -44%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 62 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 117 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
4. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The icicle crawler is a glacier construct native to Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cryo-Glacier has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glacial ice deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
