## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 27% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 61 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 56 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Floating Astral Sky Islands warn newcomers about the archon construct before anything else. As a void seraph, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Grapple is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
