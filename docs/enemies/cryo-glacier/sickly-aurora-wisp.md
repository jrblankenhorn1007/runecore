## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 50% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 31 frost damage to the first target struck.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 29 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the aurora wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This cryo wraith breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
