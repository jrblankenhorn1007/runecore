## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 69% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -9%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 70 damage at range.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 72 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the sludge hound before anything else. As a sludge ooze, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
