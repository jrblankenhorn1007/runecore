## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 65% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 54 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 44 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the firewall guardian has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 61% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
