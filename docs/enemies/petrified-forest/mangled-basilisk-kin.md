- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 42% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 68 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 64 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 108 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Petrified Ancient Forest describe the basilisk kin as deceptively patient for a stone elemental. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
