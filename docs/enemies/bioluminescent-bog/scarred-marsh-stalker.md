## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 58% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 86 damage.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 52 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 107 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Bioluminescent Fungal Bog describe the marsh stalker as deceptively patient for a fungal horror. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
