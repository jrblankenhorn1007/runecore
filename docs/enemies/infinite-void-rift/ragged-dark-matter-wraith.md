## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 85% and 71 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 204 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Infinite Void Rift describe the dark matter wraith as deceptively patient for a abyssal titan. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
