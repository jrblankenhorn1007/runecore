## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 31 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 62 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 34 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The grave-gold ghoul did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 44% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
