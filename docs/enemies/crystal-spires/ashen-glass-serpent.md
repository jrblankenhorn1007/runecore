## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 46 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 43 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the glass serpent has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Physical Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
