## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 6% and 18 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -29%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 85 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 39 damage.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
4. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the meadow serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This feral livestock favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
