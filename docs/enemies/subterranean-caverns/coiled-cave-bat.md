## Lore
The cave bat did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near granite deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
