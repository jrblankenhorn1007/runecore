# Symbiote (The Druid / Chimeric Shifter)

The Symbiote is the primal beast-shifter, wildlife channeler, and nature-grafted titan of Untitled RPG, utilizing living chimeric animal DNA to transform between powerful metamorphic forms and dominate rugged wilderness biomes.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Playstyle

### 1.1 The Archetype
Embodying the ancient druidic shapeshifter fused with mutagenic chimeric genetic grafts, the Symbiote adapts instantaneously to the demands of the battlefield. They shift seamlessly between an armored Ursine form for heavy crowd-control and an agile Lupine/Raptor form for rapid traversal, summoning thorned brambles and commanding tamed predators.

- **Primary Combat Role**: Shapeshifting Bruiser / Mobility Traversal / Nature Crowd-Control.
- **Weapon Specialization**: Grafted Claws, Bone Fangs, Primal Staves, Chitin Scythes, Thorn Whips.
- **Survival Niche**: Photosynthetic regeneration in sunlight; forage double wild berries and meat; tracks wild beasts across the map; resistant to environmental temperature extremes.

### 1.2 Baseline Attributes & Derived Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: 12 ($+18.0\%$ melee damage, $+24\text{ kg}$ carry weight).
- **Vitality (VIT)**: 14 ($+140$ Max HP, $+0.7\text{ HP/s}$ regen).
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: 10 ($+15.0\%$ speed).
- **Intelligence (INT)**: 6 (Wild nature knowledge).
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: 10 ($+1.0\text{ MP/s}$ mana regen, $+10$ stability).
- **Cyber-Affinity (CYB)**: 4 (Low tech compatibility).

**Starting Derived Pools**:
- Health: $240\text{ HP}$
- Stamina: $70\text{ Stamina}$
- Mana: $80\text{ Mana}$
- Power: $40\text{ Power}$
- Humanity: $90$ (Beast blood influence)
- Stability: $90$ (High biological elasticity)

### 1.3 Starting Equipment Kit
- **Main Hand**: Grafted Chitin Claws (16 physical damage, 2.4 attacks/sec).
- **Off Hand**: Briar Whip (12 nature damage, pulls targets).
- **Armor**: Living Moss Wrap ($+6$ Armor, $+1\text{ HP/s}$ in daylight, $+15$ cold resist).
- **Consumables**: 3x Wild Jerky, 2x Chimeric Salves.

## 2. Signature Class Mechanic: Metamorphic Forms

Pressing `E` instantaneously toggles the Symbiote between two specialized beast forms with no resource cost and no cooldown:
- **Ursine Form (The Armored Bear)**:
  - $+50\%$ Armor value.
  - $+25\%$ Maximum Health.
  - $+30\%$ Knockback dealt to enemies.
  - $-15\%$ Movement Speed.
- **Lupine Form (The Agile Wolf)**:
  - $+30\%$ Movement Speed.
  - $+50\%$ Maximum Jump Height.
  - $+20\%$ Weapon Attack Speed.
  - $-15\%$ Armor value.

## 3. Class Skill Tree & Abilities

### 3.1 Active Skills (Bound to Hotkeys `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`)

#### Active Q — Chimeric Maul
- **Hotbar Slot**: `Q`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Stamina
- **Cooldown**: 3.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Delivers a ferocious claw swipe dealing $180\%$ physical damage in a wide arc. Restores 8 Stamina if the attack connects with at least one target.

#### Active E — Form Shift (Ursine / Lupine)
- **Hotbar Slot**: `E`
- **Resource Cost**: 0
- **Cooldown**: 1.0 second
- **Effect**: Toggles active metamorphosis form between Ursine and Lupine, adapting combat bonuses instantly.

#### Active R — Thorn Bramble Entanglement
- **Hotbar Slot**: `R`
- **Resource Cost**: 20 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 8.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Summons a 6-tile wide patch of razor briars on the ground for 5.0 seconds. Enemies walking through are rooted in place for 3.0 seconds and take continuous nature/bleed damage.

#### Active F — Apex Predator Unleashed
- **Hotbar Slot**: `F` (Ultimate)
- **Resource Cost**: 40 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 35.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Releases the inner chimeric apex predator for 10.0 seconds: grows massive chitinous wings granting full flight (`Space`), doubles claw attack range, and causes every attack to unleash sonic shockwaves.

### 3.2 Key Passive Specializations
1. *Feral Senses*: Highlights all enemies and prey animals within 30 tiles on the minimap.
2. *Grafted Fortitude*: Maximum Health increased by $+120$ flat HP.
3. *Stalker's Stride*: Movement speed in forests, caves, and swamps increased by $+20\%$.
4. *Chitin Spikes*: Reflects $15\%$ of incoming melee damage back onto the attacker.
5. *Photosynthesis*: While outdoors in direct sunlight, regenerate $1.0\text{ HP/s}$ and $1.0\text{ MP/s}$.
6. *Savage Rend*: Claw strikes apply Deep Wound, reducing target armor by $5\%$ per hit (stacks up to $25\%$).
7. *Primal Stamina*: Stamina recovery delay after swinging or running reduced by $50\%$.
8. *Rooted Stance*: Standing stationary for 1.0s grants $+25\%$ physical damage resistance.
9. *Pack Leader*: Tamed animals, pets, and summons gain $+30\%$ damage and health.
10. *Primal Ferocity*: Landing a critical strike grants $+10\%$ movement speed for 4.0 seconds.
11. *Adaptive Carapace*: Taking elemental damage reduces subsequent damage of that element by $20\%$ for 8.0 seconds.
