## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 60% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 50 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 43 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the runoff horror before anything else. As a corroded construct, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
