## Lore
The static horror is a lightning wraith native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near neodymium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
