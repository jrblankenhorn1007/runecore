## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 35% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -4%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 40 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 77 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 54 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Haunted Graveyards & Mire describe the bog zombie as deceptively patient for a mire beast. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
