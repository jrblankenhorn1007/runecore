## Lore
The voltaic wraith is a rogue ai construct native to Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
