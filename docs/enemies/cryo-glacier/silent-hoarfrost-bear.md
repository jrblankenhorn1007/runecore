## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 60% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -36%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
The hoarfrost bear is a cryo wraith native to Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cryo-Glacier has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
