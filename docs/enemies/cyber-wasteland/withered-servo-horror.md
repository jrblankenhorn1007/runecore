- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 45% and 42 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 94 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the servo horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
