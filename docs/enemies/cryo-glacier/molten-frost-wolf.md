## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 47% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -28%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 14 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 22 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the frost wolf as deceptively patient for a glacier construct. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 74% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
