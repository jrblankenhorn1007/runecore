## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 70% and 70 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -20%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 142 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 157 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The event-horizon sentinel did not always haunt The Infinite Void Rift; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its toxin weakness rewards elemental prep.
