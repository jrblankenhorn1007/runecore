## Lore
The storm wisp is a archon construct native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
