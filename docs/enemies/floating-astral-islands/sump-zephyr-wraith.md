## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the zephyr wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
