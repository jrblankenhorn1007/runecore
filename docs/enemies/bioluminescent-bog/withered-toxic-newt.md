## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 54% and 50 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 111 damage in a 6-tile radius.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The toxic newt did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 55% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
