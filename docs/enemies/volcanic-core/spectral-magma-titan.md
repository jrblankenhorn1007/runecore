## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 80% and 68 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -57%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 212 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 159 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 105 damage and applying a short knockback.
4. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 110 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the magma titan before anything else. As a lava wyrm, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Whirl is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
