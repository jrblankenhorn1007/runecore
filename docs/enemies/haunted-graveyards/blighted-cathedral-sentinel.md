## Lore
Fieldnotes from Haunted Graveyards & Mire describe the cathedral sentinel as deceptively patient for a cursed spirit. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 27% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
