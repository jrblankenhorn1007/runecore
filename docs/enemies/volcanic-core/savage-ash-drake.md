## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 79% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -45%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 69 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 95 damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, the ash drake has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
