## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, the marrow horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
