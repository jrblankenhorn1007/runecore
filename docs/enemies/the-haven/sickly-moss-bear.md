## Lore
Local guides in The Haven (Hub Valley) warn newcomers about the moss bear before anything else. As a territorial predator, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
