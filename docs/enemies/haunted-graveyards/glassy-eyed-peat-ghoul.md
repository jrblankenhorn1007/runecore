## Lore
The peat ghoul is a bog zombie native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Summon Broodlings chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
