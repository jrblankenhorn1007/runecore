## Lore
The rogue ai sentinel is a emp turret native to Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
