## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 60 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The void sentinel did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 90% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
