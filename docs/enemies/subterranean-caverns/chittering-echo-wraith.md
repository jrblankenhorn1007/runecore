## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 25 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 47 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the echo wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This blind predator breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
