The sinew golem is a genesis mutant native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
