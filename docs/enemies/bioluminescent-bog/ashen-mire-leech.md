## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 58% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 115 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
4. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Bioluminescent Fungal Bog warn newcomers about the mire leech before anything else. As a fungal horror, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
