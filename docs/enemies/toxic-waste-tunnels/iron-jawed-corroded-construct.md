## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 66% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 90 damage.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 121 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 113 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the corroded construct ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 22% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
