## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 6 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 11 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the wolf has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
