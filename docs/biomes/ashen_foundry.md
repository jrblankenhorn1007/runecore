# Ashen Foundry (Overworld Zone 2)

The Ashen Foundry is an expansive volcanic industrial wasteland located in the eastern lowlands. Formed where an active tectonic magma fissure erupted through a sprawling pre-cataclysm metallurgical complex, the biome is choked with black soot, burning slag rivers, automated blast vents, and rogue mining machines.

---

## 1. Biome Environment & Atmosphere

- **Biome Type**: Volcanic Industrial Surface & Caldera.
- **Ambient Temperature**: $+45^\circ\text{C}$ to $+65^\circ\text{C}$ (Extreme Overheating territory; standing near lava raises temperature above $+80^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - *Lava Rivers & Slag Pools*: Stepping into molten slag deals $50\text{ fire dmg/s}$ and sets character ablaze unless wearing Molten Slag Sabatons or using Fire Immunity potions.
  - *Sulfur Steam Vents*: Periodic high-pressure gas eruptions from terrain blocks that launch the player into the air and inflict choking poison.
  - *Extreme Thirst Drain*: Thirst meter depletes at $3.0\times$ normal rate due to blistering heat.
- **Weather States**:
  - *Ash Storm*: Dense black particulate fallout; reduces visibility; unshielded eyes suffer blurred vision unless wearing goggles or targeting visors.
  - *Ember Rain*: Falling molten cinders deal minor fire ticks to wooden structures and exposed flesh.

---

## 2. Natural Resources & Mineral Deposits

- **High-Temperature Ores**:
  - *Titanium Ore Veins*: Abundant in the cooled basalt rock faces.
  - *Raw Gold Ore*: Found embedded in quartz intrusions near magma flows.
  - *Solidified Magma Slag*: Mineable material used for crafting heat-resistant foundry armor and thermal maces.
  - *Obsidian Crystals*: Ultra-sharp volcanic glass shards used in razor daggers and arrowheads.
- **Combustible Materials**:
  - *Industrial Coke Coal*: High-temperature forge fuel.
  - *Sulfur Dust*: Required chemical component for pressing gunpowder and brewing explosive flasks.
  - *White Phosphorus*: Rare reagent for incendiary grenades.
- **Flora**:
  - *Fire Pepper Plants*: Scorching wild peppers used in spicy stews and heat-resistance recipes.
  - *Ash-Root*: Tough tuber that thrives in volcanic soil; source of natural burn salve.

## 3. Enemy Populations & Spawn Tables

### 3.1 Daytime & Surface Enemies (Levels 25–35)
- **Molten Slime** (Elemental): Bouncing fiery ooze that splits into 2 mini-slimes upon death; leaves burning fire trails.
- **Rogue Mining Automaton** (Machine): Heavy quadruped biped drill droid; charges forward spinning dual diamond drills; drops titanium ore and pneumatic pistons.
- **Flame-Trooper Cyborg** (Cyborg): Heavy armored scavenger wielding a continuous flamethrower arm and thermite canisters; drops fuel cells and steel plates.
- **Magma Basilisk** (Beast): Armored volcanic lizard that shoots streams of liquid slag from its jaws; drops heat-insulated scales and molten glands.

### 3.2 Nighttime Enemies (Levels 30–40)
- **Thermite Sentry Drone** (Machine): High-speed flying drone that dives directly at the player, self-destructing in a blinding white thermite detonation.
- **Slag Golem** (Elemental Machine): Massive humanoid constructed of cooled slag and glowing magma joints; immune to physical stagger; ground slams trigger volcanic fissures.
- **Ashen Reanimator** (Cyborg Mage): Cultist technician that summons flame wisps and reanimates destroyed mining droids.

## 4. Procedural Dungeons: The Molten Core Vaults (Campaign Tier 4)

Beneath the caldera floor lie the entry bulkheads to the **Molten Core Smeltery**:
- **Dungeon Architectural Style**: Heavy industrial steel catwalks suspended over boiling lakes of glowing lava, automated overhead ladle cranes, and giant hydraulic stampers.
- **Dungeon Hazards**: Conveyor belts moving the player toward furnace crushers, rotating flame-thrower turrets, and crumbling basalt bridges.
- **Campaign Tier 4 Boss Encounter: FORGE-MASTER VULCAN**:
  - *Phase 1*: Wields a colossal magma warhammer and crucible shield; strikes the central anvil to send horizontal waves of molten iron across platforms; hurls canisters of burning thermite.
  - *Phase 2 (30% HP)*: Discards outer heavy armor, igniting in full plasma flames; dashes across platforms leaving permanent fire walls; triggers ceiling slag collapses.
  - *Boss Unique Loot*: *Forge-Master Vulcan Hammer-Sword*, *Crucible Tower Shield*, and *Molten Slag Crown*.
