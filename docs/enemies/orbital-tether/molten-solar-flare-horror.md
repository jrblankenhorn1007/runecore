## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the solar flare horror as deceptively patient for a void sentinel. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Death Curse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
