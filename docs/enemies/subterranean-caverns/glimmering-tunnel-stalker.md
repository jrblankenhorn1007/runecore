## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 34% and 18 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 54 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 32 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 37 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the tunnel stalker before anything else. As a cave beast, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
