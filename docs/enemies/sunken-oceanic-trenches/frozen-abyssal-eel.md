## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 113 damage at range.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 158 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The abyssal eel did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
