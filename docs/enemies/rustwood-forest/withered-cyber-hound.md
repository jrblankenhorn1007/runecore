## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 18% and 2 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -31%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 13 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 7 damage.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the cyber-hound before anything else. As a feral predator, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
