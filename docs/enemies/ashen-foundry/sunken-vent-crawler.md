## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 38 damage in a 3-tile radius.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the vent crawler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 33% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
