## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 153 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 87 damage at range.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 129 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The hourglass golem did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
