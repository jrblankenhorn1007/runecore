## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 25% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 70 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the grave wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
