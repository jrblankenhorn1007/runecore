## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 35% and 26 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 49 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the rad-storm wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
