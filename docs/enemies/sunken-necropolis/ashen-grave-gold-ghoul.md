## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the grave-gold ghoul has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near bone slate deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
