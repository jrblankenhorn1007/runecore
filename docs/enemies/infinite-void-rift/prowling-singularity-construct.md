## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 70% and 69 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 113 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the singularity construct ranks among its defining hazards. This reality wraith ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
