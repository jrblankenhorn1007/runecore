## Lore
Local guides in Radioactive Crater Basin warn newcomers about the lead-skin horror before anything else. As a gamma horror, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
