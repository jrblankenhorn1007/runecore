## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 135 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 132 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The paradox horror did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
