## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 8 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Subterranean Caverns describe the deep spider as deceptively patient for a blind predator. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
