## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 36% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 57 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The radiant construct did not always haunt Crystal Spires & Geodes; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
