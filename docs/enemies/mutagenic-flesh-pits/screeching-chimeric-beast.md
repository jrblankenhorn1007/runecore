## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 63% and 50 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 68 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 152 damage.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 83 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, the chimeric beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
