## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 7% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -30%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 15 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 19 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the owl ranks among its defining hazards. This wild avian lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 14% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
