## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 68% and 51 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 110 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 109 shock damage to the first target struck.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 136 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The crag sentinel did not always haunt Magnetic Thunder-Crags; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Shock Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
