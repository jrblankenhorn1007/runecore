## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the thorn lynx has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
