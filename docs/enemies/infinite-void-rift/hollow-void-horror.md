## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 78% and 99 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 287 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 251 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
4. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 155 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in The Infinite Void Rift warn newcomers about the void horror before anything else. As a singularity construct, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Caustic Spray is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
