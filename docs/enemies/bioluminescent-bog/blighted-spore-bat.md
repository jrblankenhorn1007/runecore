## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 53% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 17 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 40 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, the spore bat has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Grapple chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
