- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 55% and 25 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 44 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 83 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
The voltaic serpent is a static horror native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
