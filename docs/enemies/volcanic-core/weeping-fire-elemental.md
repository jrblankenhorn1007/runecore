## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 74% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -46%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 43 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 89 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 52 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, the fire elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
