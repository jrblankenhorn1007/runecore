## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 55% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 66 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Toxin Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of toxin energy, dealing 45 toxin damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The bile crawler did not always haunt Mutagenic Flesh-Pits; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
