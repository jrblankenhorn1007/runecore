# Woods, Timbers, Barks & Botanical Saps Catalog

This document specifies the individual wood species, timber tiers, bark properties, botanical saps, and crafting applications across all surface and subterranean biomes in Untitled RPG (~50 botanical materials).

---

## 1. Wood Tiers & Felling Power Requirements

Trees require Woodcutting Axes of appropriate power to fell. Harder woods yield structural lumber with higher health points, superior fire or cold insulation, and resistance to enemy night raids.

| Tier | Wood / Timber Type | Required Axe Power | Primary Biome Source | Structural Block HP | Special Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Scrap Wood / Driftwood | $25\%$ Axe | Coastal Ruins, Shorelines | 80 HP | Porous, burns quickly. |
| **Tier 1** | Temperate Green Oak | $35\%$ Axe | The Haven, Rustwood Forest | 120 HP | General structural timber. |
| **Tier 2** | Whispering White Pine | $45\%$ Axe | Whispering Pine Taiga | 160 HP | High resin content; $+10$ Cold Resist. |
| **Tier 2** | Phosphor Willow | $50\%$ Axe | Bioluminescent Bog | 140 HP | Rot-resistant; faint blue glow. |
| **Tier 3** | Submerged Iron-Reed | $60\%$ Axe | Submerged Cooling Vaults | 180 HP | Water-hardened cellular structure. |
| **Tier 4** | Charred Cinder-Ash | $75\%$ Axe | Ashen Foundry | 260 HP | Naturally fireproof (immune to burning). |
| **Tier 5** | Glacial Frost-Bark | $90\%$ Axe | Cryo-Glacier Neon Peaks | 340 HP | Dense frozen grain; $+25$ Cold Resist. |
| **Tier 6** | Iron Canopy Burl-Wood | $105\%$ Chainsaw | Iron Canopy Jungle | 450 HP | Biomechanical wood reinforced with steel. |
| **Tier 7** | Petrified Astral Cedar | $125\%$ Plasma Axe | Hard-Light Citadel, Highlands | 600 HP | Stone-like density; conducts magic. |
