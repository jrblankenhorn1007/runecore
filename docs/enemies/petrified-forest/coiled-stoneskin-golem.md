## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 87 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
The stoneskin golem is a gorgon spawn native to Petrified Ancient Forest, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 36% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
