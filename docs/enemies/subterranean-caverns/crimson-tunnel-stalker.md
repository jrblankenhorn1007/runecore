## Lore
The tunnel stalker is a cave beast native to Subterranean Caverns, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
