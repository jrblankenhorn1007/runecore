## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 36% and 52 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 85 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 180 damage and applying a short knockback.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the metro horror ranks among its defining hazards. This feral mutant favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 44% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
