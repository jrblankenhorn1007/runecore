## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 10% and 19 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 49 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 57 damage.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
4. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 31 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Local guides in The Haven (Hub Valley) warn newcomers about the ram before anything else. As a scavenger vermin, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
