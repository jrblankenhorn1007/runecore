## Lore
The quicksand horror is a ghost/wraith native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 24% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
