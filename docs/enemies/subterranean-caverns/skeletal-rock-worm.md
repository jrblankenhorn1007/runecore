## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 19 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 39 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 45 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the rock worm before anything else. As a blind predator, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
