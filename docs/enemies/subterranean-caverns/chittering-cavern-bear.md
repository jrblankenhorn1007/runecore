## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 40 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 51 damage at range.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Subterranean Caverns describe the cavern bear as deceptively patient for a subterranean insect. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
