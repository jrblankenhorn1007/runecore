## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the barnacle horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
