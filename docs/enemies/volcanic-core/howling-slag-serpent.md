## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 67 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The slag serpent did not always haunt Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
