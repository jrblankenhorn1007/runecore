## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 61% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -30%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 57 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 51 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The slag beast is a magma construct native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
