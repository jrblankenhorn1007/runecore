## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the hard-light golem as deceptively patient for a solar wraith. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
