## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 35% and 53 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 162 damage in a 4-tile radius.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ancient Ruined Megacity describe the salvage scavenger as deceptively patient for a feral mutant. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near circuit scrap deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
