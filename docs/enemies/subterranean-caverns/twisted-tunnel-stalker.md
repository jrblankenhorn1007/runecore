## Lore
The tunnel stalker is a crystal crawler native to Subterranean Caverns, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
