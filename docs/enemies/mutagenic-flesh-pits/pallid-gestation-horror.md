2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 140 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Mutagenic Flesh-Pits dispute that the gestation horror ranks among its defining hazards. This genesis mutant hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its eldritch flesh scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
