# Tech Salvage & Electronic Components Catalog

This document specifies the salvageable technological artifacts, electronic circuits, wiring, motors, and microprocessors recovered from derelict ruins, rogue automata, crashed drop-pods, and cyber-laboratories in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Tech Salvage Mechanics
- **Procurement**: Scavenged from derelict labs, factory bunkers, downed satellites, robotic enemies, and scrap scrapheaps using wrench or crowbar disassembly tools.
- **Deconstruction**: Salvaged electronics can be broken down at the Electronics Bench into copper, gold, silicon, and solder wires.
- **Applications**: Mandatory components for Bionic Augmentations, automated turrets, generators, wiring grids, and energy weapons.
