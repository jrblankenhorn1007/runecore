- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 66% and 51 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 57 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
The lightning wraith is a magnetic construct native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
