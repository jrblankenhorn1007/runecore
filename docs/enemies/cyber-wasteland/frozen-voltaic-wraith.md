## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 55% and 43 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 124 damage at range.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 116 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the voltaic wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Phase Step chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
