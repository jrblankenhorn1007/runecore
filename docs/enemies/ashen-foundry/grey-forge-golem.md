## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 48% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -27%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 72 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 53 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The forge golem is a slag beast native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
