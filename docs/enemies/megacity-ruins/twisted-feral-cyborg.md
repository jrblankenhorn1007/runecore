## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 36% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -29%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 72 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 46 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The feral cyborg did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
