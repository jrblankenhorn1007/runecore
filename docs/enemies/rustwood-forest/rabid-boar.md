## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the boar ranks among its defining hazards. This cyborg hound favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its sound detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
