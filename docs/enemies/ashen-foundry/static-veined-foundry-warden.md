## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -39%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 32 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 33 damage.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 35 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The foundry warden is a forge golem native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
