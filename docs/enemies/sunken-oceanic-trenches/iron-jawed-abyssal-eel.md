## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the abyssal eel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 31% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
