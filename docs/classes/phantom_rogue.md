# Phantom (The Rogue / Nano-Infiltrator)

The Phantom is the silent stalker, assassin, and tech-infiltrator of Untitled RPG, utilizing optical cloaking, monomolecular blades, smoke screens, and teleportation blinks to strike lethal blows from behind before vanishing into shadows.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Playstyle

### 1.1 The Archetype
Embodying the agile thief and assassin augmented with nano-circuit camouflage and sensory scramblers, the Phantom operates at close quarters with daggers or at mid-range with toxic shurikens. They turn enemy vision against them, exploiting crowd control, blinding smokes, and backstabs.

- **Primary Combat Role**: Single-Target Burst DPS / Stealth Infiltrator / Critical Striker.
- **Weapon Specialization**: Dual Daggers, Monomolecular Blades, Claws, Throwing Knives, Shurikens, Suppressed Pistols.
- **Survival Niche**: Nearly silent footstep detection; ability to bypass enemy patrols; disarms dungeon traps automatically; harvests poisons and venom glands.

### 1.2 Baseline Attributes & Derived Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: 6 (Light carry capacity).
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: 16 ($+24.0\%$ speed, $+1.6\%$ crit, $+3.2\%$ movement speed).
- **Vitality (VIT)**: 8 ($+80$ Max HP).
- **Intelligence (INT)**: 8 (Nano-program execution).
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: 5 (Heightened danger awareness).
- **Cyber-Affinity (CYB)**: 7 ($+70$ Power Capacity).

**Starting Derived Pools**:

## 2. Signature Class Mechanic: Cloak & Ambush

When standing still in darkness or activating optical camouflage, the Phantom enters **Stealth**:
- **Invisibility**: Enemies lose line of sight and break active pursuit.
- **Ambush Strike**: The first attack breaking stealth is an automatic critical strike dealing $+200\%$ bonus Ambush Damage and stunning the target for 1.5 seconds.
- **Backstab Alignment**: Striking an enemy from their rear quadrant adds an additional $+40\%$ backstab damage multiplier.

## 3. Class Skill Tree & Abilities

### 3.1 Active Skills (Bound to Hotkeys `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`)

#### Active Q — Shadow Blink
- **Hotbar Slot**: `Q`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Stamina
- **Cooldown**: 6.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Instantly teleports 6 tiles towards the mouse crosshair, passing through enemy hitboxes and leaving a thick cloud of smoke that blinds all adjacent foes for 2.0 seconds.

#### Active E — Optical Camouflage
- **Hotbar Slot**: `E`
- **Resource Cost**: 20 Power or 25 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 14.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Activates total optical invisibility for 6.0 seconds. Moving or attacking does not break stealth for the first 1.5 seconds of activation.

#### Active R — Shuriken Flurry
- **Hotbar Slot**: `R`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Stamina
- **Cooldown**: 8.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Hurls 5 piercing monomolecular shurikens in a forward fan, penetrating all enemies in line and applying stacking Neurotoxin (slows movement by $5\%$ per stack and ticks acid damage).

#### Active F — Executioner's Dance
- **Hotbar Slot**: `F` (Ultimate)
- **Resource Cost**: 35 Stamina
- **Cooldown**: 30.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Teleports with blinding speed between up to 5 enemies within 10 tiles, slashing each for $200\%$ weapon damage with complete invulnerability frames during execution.

### 3.2 Key Passive Specializations
1. *Silent Footsteps*: Enemy detection radius reduced by $40\%$.
2. *Lethal Precision*: Base critical strike chance increased by $+5\%$.
3. *Backstab Protocol*: Attacks striking enemies from behind deal $+40\%$ bonus damage.
4. *Venomous Coat*: Thrown weapons and daggers apply stacking Neurotoxin.
5. *Acrobatic Momentum*: Dashing costs $30\%$ less Stamina; dash travel distance $+1$ tile.
6. *Phase Shift*: Successfully dodging through an attack with a dash restores 10 Stamina.
7. *Coup de Grace*: Deal $+50\%$ bonus damage to targets currently suffering from Stun, Freeze, or Blind.
8. *Holo-Decoy*: Teleporting leaves behind a holographic clone that taunts enemies for 2.5 seconds.
9. *Twin Blade Mastery*: Dual-wielding daggers increases weapon attack speed by $+20\%$.
10. *Emergency Smoke*: Taking damage exceeding $30\%$ Max HP automatically deploys a smoke bomb.
11. *Lethality Overflow*: Critical strikes refund 5 Stamina.

## 4. Level 20 Free Signature Augmentation

### *Nano-Wire Monomolecular Daggers*
- **Body Slot**: Hands
- **Augmentation Type**: Cybernetic Subdermal Weaponry
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 surgery credits, 0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep).
- **Core Functionality**:
  - Retractable monomolecular nano-wire blades extend from between the finger joints.
  - Attacks with these blades completely ignore all enemy physical armor.
  - Strikes executed from stealth deal an astronomical $400\%$ damage.
- **Cross-Class Exotic Surgery Trade-Off**:
  - Non-native installation penalty: $-25$ Humanity, $-10\%$ total physical armor, and prevents wielding two-handed weapons.
