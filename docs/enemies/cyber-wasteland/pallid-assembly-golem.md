## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the assembly golem as deceptively patient for a emp turret. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
