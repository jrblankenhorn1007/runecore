## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the quartz golem before anything else. As a blind predator, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
