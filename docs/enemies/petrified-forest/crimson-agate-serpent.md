## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 53% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 46 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the agate serpent before anything else. As a stone elemental, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
