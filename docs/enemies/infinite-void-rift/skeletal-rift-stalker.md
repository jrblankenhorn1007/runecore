## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 197 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 191 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The rift stalker is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
