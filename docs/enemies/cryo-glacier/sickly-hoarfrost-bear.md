## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 30 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 75 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the hoarfrost bear ranks among its defining hazards. This ice elemental guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 43% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
