## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the agate serpent has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 33% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
