## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the acid slug has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Death Curse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
