## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 50% and 51 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -9%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 160 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The screaming flesh mass is a chimeric beast native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 32% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
