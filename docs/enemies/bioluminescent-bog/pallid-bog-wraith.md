## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 40% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 64 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
The bog wraith did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 83% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
