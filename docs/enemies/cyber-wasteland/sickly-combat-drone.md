## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 57% and 49 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 62 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The combat drone is a combat drone native to Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
