## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 50% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -38%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 72 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 53 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The vent crawler did not always haunt Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
