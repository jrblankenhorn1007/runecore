## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 70% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in The Infinite Void Rift warn newcomers about the warp wisp before anything else. As a entropy spawn, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
