## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the thorn lynx ranks among its defining hazards. This territorial predator breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
