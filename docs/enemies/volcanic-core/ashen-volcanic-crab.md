## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 66% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -52%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 93 damage.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 127 damage in a 4-tile radius.
3. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 51 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the volcanic crab as deceptively patient for a ember wraith. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Death Curse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
