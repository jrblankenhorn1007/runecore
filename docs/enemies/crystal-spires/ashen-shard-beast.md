## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 44% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 28 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the shard beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
