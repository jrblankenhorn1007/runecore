## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 33% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 32 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 35 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 40 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
The blind crawler did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
