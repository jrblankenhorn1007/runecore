## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 37% and 43 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 95 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 113 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The zephyr wraith did not always haunt Floating Astral Sky Islands; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 16% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
