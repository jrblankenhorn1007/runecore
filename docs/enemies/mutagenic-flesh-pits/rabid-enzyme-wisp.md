## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 136 damage.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 105 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 93 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
The enzyme wisp is a eldritch spawn native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
