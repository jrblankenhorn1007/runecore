# Earrings, Sensory Studs & Focus Cuffs Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, sensory perception bonuses, audio-visual HUD enhancements, and spell critical amplifiers for earrings and ear studs across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~60 accessory definitions). Characters can equip 1 Earring/Stud in their accessory array.

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Accessory Category**: Earring / Sensory Stud Slot.
- **Affix Bias**: Emphasizes audio awareness (revealing off-screen enemy approach pings), stealth noise reduction, spell casting velocity, and precision critical multipliers.
