## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 30 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 22 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 15 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the prowler before anything else. As a cyborg hound, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
