## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 84% and 70 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 134 damage in a 4-tile radius.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 250 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the cosmic horror ranks among its defining hazards. This singularity construct hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Pounce is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
