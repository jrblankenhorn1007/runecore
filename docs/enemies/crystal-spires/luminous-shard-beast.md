## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 40% and 46 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -8%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 135 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 63 damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the shard beast as deceptively patient for a shard beast. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Weakpoint Overload is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
