## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 40% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 24 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The mire wretch is a ghost/wraith native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 16% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
