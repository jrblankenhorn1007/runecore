## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 23 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the icicle crawler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glacial ice deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
