## Lore
The lead-skin horror is a gamma horror native to Radioactive Crater Basin, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Radioactive Crater Basin has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
