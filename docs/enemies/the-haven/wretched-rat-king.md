3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 8 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The rat-king is a bramble beast native to The Haven (Hub Valley), lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Haven has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
