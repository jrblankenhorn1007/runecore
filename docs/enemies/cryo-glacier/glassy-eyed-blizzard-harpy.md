## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 63% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -36%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 49 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 18 damage at range.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the blizzard harpy as deceptively patient for a ice elemental. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
