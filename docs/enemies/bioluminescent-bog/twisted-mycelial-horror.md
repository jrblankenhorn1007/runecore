## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 52% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 65 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 76 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 33 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the mycelial horror ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic amphibian circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
