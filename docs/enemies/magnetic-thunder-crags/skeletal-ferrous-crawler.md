## Lore
The ferrous crawler is a thunder beast native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
