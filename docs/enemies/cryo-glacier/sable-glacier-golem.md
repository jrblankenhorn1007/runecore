## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the glacier golem has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
