## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 51% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -38%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 72 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 39 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the slag beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
