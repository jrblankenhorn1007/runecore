## Lore
The weeping widow spirit is a bog zombie native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
