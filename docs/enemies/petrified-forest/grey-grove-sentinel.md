## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the grove sentinel before anything else. As a stone elemental, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
