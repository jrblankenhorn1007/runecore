## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 69% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -50%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the obsidian guardian before anything else. As a magma elemental, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
