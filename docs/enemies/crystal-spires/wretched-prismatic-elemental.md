## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 37% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 88 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 60 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 59 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Crystal Spires & Geodes warn newcomers about the prismatic elemental before anything else. As a shard beast, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Physical Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
