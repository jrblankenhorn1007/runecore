## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 50% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -44%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 18 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 22 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the slag hulk has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Grapple is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
