## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 70% and 50 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 104 damage at range.
2. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 107 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 79 damage and applying a short knockback.
4. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the runoff horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 51% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
