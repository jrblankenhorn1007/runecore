## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 24% and 6 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 14 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 18 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
The ironbark treant is a cyborg hound native to Rustwood Forest, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
