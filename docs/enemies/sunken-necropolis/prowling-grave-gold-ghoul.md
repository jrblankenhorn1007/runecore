## Lore
The grave-gold ghoul is a grave wraith native to Sunken Necropolis, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
