## Lore
Fieldnotes from Magnetic Thunder-Crags describe the lightning wraith as deceptively patient for a lightning wraith. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 23% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
