## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 15% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 31 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 24 damage at range.

## Lore
The stone scuttler did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
