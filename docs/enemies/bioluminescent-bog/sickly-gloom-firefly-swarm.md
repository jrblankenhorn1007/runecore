- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 42% and 25 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 96 damage.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 88 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Bioluminescent Fungal Bog warn newcomers about the gloom firefly swarm before anything else. As a fungal horror, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
