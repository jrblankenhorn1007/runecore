## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 27% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 32 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 63 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 25 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the silver adder ranks among its defining hazards. This subterranean insect breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 73% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
