## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 58 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 190 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Chrono-Distortion Desert describe the chrono construct as deceptively patient for a chrono construct. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
