## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 51% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, the rot hound has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
