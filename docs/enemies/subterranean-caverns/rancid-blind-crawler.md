## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 28% and 37 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -5%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 87 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 92 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
4. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the blind crawler before anything else. As a blind predator, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near granite deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
