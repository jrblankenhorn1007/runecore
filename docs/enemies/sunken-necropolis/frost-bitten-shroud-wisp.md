## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 42 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 93 damage at range.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The shroud wisp did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 32% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
