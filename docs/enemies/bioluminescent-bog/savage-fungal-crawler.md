## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 68 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 79 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the fungal crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This spore beast lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 38% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
