## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 10% and 2 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 7 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 15 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 8 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the rifleman ranks among its defining hazards. This cyborg hound swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
