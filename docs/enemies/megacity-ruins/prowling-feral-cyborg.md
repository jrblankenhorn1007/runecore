## Lore
The feral cyborg is a salvage scavenger native to Ancient Ruined Megacity, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ancient Ruined Megacity has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
