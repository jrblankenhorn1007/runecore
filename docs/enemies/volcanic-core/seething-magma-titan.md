## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the magma titan ranks among its defining hazards. This ashen titan breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
