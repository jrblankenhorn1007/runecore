## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 30 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 71 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 36 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
The acid slug did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
