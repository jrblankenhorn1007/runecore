## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 22% and 2 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -25%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The timber wolf did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 17% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
