## Lore
Few who study Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps dispute that the ash wretch ranks among its defining hazards. This industrial drone breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its basalt scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
