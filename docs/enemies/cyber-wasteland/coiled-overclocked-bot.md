## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 68 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Local guides in Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers warn newcomers about the overclocked bot before anything else. As a combat drone, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
