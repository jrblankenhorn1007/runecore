## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 30% and 66 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 188 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 153 damage over time and pinning the target.
4. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 133 damage at range.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the prismatic elemental as deceptively patient for a geode crawler. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
