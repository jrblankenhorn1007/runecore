## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 31% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 75 damage.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 50 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the prismatic elemental as deceptively patient for a prismatic elemental. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
