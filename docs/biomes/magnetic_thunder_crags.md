# Biome Dossier: Magnetic Thunder-Crags & Storm Spires

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Magnetic Thunder-Crags & Storm Spires (Biome 20)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-20`, Tier 5 Elemental / High Altitude.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Towering jagged mountain spires composed of supercharged magnetic lodestone, situated directly beneath the storm clouds of the Floating Astral Islands ($X = -1000$ to $X = -2500$, Altitude $Y = +300$ to $Y = +700$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Continuous Atmospheric Lightning**: Violent lightning strikes hit high ground every 4–8 seconds. Players standing on exposed ridges without a Lightning Rod totem or Rubber Grounding Boots take 120 shock damage.
  - **Magnetic Polar Distortion**: Intense magnetic field pulls metal weapons and ammunition toward magnetized lodestone pillars; ballistic bullets curve unpredictably unless using non-ferrous ceramic or plasma rounds.
  - **Levitating Magnetite Boulders**: Floating islands of magnetic rock that bob vertically and horizontally on magnetic flux lines; mistiming jumps leads to lethal falls.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 5°C (Howling winds, heavy rain, ionized air).

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Magnetic Lodestone Blocks, Storm-Crag Basalt, Fulgurite Spires, Neodymium Ore Blocks, Electrified Copper Strata.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Natural Lodestone, Copper Ore, Basalt.
  - **Moderate**: Neodymium Magnets, Storm Agate, High-Purity Quartz.
  - **Rare**: Tachyon Shards, Superconducting Graphene, Hyper-Magnetic Cores.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Tesla-Grass**: Metallic grass blades that hum with static; shocks walking creatures for 5 damage/tick.
  - **Storm-Orchids**: Blue flowers that store lightning charges; used in high-voltage alchemy and energy capacitor cells.
  - **Conductive Lichen**: Scraped from rocks to craft electrical wire insulation.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Precursor Weather Control Spire "Aegis-Tempest"
- **Dungeon Structure**: Massive spire anchored into a central magnetic rift. Features massive rotating Tesla coils, high-voltage elevator shafts, magnetic catapult launch pads, and open storm-lashed balconies.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Aligning magnetic polarity switches (+ and -) to create repellent fields that catapult the player across vertical gaps to higher tower levels.
- **Dungeon Boss: Thor-9 "The Storm Titan Automaton" (Level 54 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: The circular crown of the weather spire exposed to open raging storm clouds, surrounded by 4 active Tesla pylons.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Wields a colossal magnetic warhammer; slams hammer down to send ripples of electric arcs across the floor, and throws the hammer like a boomerang that pulls player toward it.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Magnetically hovers into the air, drawing all 4 Tesla pylons together into a hyper-charged storm core; carpets the arena in continuous lightning strikes and fires high-speed railgun spikes.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Mjolnir Kinetic Driver (T5 Warhammer/Railgun), Superconducting Spine Wire (Nervous Augment), Neodymium Power Core, 3x Precursor Datacubes.
