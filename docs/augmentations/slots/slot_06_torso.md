# Slot 6: Torso Augmentations Catalog (25 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 25 torso augmentations (Internal titanium plating, nanite hives, runic ribcages, and chimeric carapaces) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Torso Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Torso Augmentation installed within the internal thoracic cavity.
- **Role**: Governs core damage reduction, structural knockback resistance, internal medical dispensaries, and secondary organ shielding.
