## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 61% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -31%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 40 damage.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 28 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 17 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The cinder hound did not always haunt Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
