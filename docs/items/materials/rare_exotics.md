# Rare & Exotic Materials Catalog

This document specifies the high-tier rare, exotic, anomalous, and precursor materials harvested in the dangerous depths and anomalous rifts of Untitled RPG. These materials form the pinnacle of late-game crafting, high-tier augmentations, and cosmic modifications.

---

## 1. Material Mechanics & Properties
- **Procurement**: Sourced from Tier 4–7 biomes, endgame procedural dungeon bosses, deep-core orbital wreckage, and Void anomalies.
- **Processing**: Cannot be smelted in standard stone furnaces; requires the Induction Foundry, Arcane Bio-Vat, or Molecular Assembler.
- **Inherent Traits**: Exotic materials carry passive crafting traits that automatically imbue weapons and armor with base affixes when used as primary fabrication components.
