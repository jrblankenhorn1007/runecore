## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 42% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 50 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 47 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Necropolis warn newcomers about the ossuary crawler before anything else. As a grave wraith, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
