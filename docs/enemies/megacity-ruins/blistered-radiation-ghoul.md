## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 32% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 77 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 82 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The radiation ghoul did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
