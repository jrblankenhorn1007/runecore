## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 28% and 33 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -12%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 78 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 76 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 75 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the quartz sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near gems deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
