## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 67 damage at range.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 80 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the ziggurat stalker has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
