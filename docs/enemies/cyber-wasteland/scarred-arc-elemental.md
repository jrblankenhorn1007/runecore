## Lore
Few who study Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers dispute that the arc elemental ranks among its defining hazards. This emp turret hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its quantum chips scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
