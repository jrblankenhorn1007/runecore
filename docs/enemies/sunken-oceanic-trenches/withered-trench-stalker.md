## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 20% and 68 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -36%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 251 damage at range.
4. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 196 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the trench stalker as deceptively patient for a aquatic predator. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Enrage is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
