## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 10% and 30 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 95 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
4. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 102 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The rat-king is a feral livestock native to The Haven (Hub Valley), lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Haven has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Corrosive Spit is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
