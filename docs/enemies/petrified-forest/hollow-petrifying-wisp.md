## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 51% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 85 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 78 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the petrifying wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This petrified guardian breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
