## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 33% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 43 damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Crystal Spires & Geodes describe the prismatic elemental as deceptively patient for a prismatic elemental. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
