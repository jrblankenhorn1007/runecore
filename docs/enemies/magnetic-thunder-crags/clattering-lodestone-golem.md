## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 59% and 40 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 47 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 110 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The lodestone golem did not always haunt Magnetic Thunder-Crags; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
