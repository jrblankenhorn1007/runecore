## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 26% and 34 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 108 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 73 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
4. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 53 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the blind crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This rock elemental charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 57% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
