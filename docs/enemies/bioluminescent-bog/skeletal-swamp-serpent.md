## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the swamp serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic amphibian breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
