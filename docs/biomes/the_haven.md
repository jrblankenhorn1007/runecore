# The Haven (Central Hub Settlement)

The Haven is the starting sanctuary, central town, and primary safe zone in Untitled RPG. Built within the crumbling stone and iron ruins of an ancient pre-cataclysm city, it serves as the player's permanent base of operations, farming hub, and gateway to subterranean procedural dungeons.

---

## 1. Biome Environment & Atmosphere

- **Biome Type**: Surface Sanctuary / Settlement Hub.
- **Ambient Temperature**: $+20^\circ\text{C}$ (Comfortable year-round; no freezing or heatstroke penalties).
- **Day/Night Dynamic**:
  - Daytime: Peaceful daylight, birdsong, clean water streams, safe from hostile spawns.
  - Nighttime: Dark skies with twinkling aurora; night beasts patrol beyond settlement perimeter walls.
- **Weather States**:
  - *Clear*: Mild sunshine, standard crop growth.
  - *Gentle Rain*: Naturally hydrates all outdoor crops; zero lightning danger.

---

## 2. Settlement Features & Natural Resources

### 2.1 Natural Geography
- **The Clear-Water Spring**: An infinite source of purified drinking water that flows through the center of town. Players can drink and fill canteens with zero parasite risk.
- **The Old Farmland**: Fertile, weed-free soil plots flanking the east side of town, ready for tilling with a gardening hoe.
- **The Pre-War Ruins**: Two-story stone and timber building frames that can be restored into functional NPC housing and player workshops.
- **The Vault Archway**: A massive ancient blast gate at the base of the central cliff leading down into the subterranean procedural dungeons.

### 2.2 Forageable & Harvestable Resources
- *Oak & Pine Trees*: Abundant timber for basic building tiles, platforms, and torches.
- *Surface Clay & Sand*: Harvested along the stream banks for bricks and glass windows.
- *Wild Carrot & Herb Patches*: Forageable wild food and starter alchemical healing herbs.
- *Surface Boulders*: Breaks down into rough stone, flint shards, and traces of copper ore.

---

## 3. Native Wildlife & NPCs

### 3.1 Non-Hostile Fauna
- *Wild White-Tailed Deer*: Roams the western meadow; yields raw venison and rawhide pelts when hunted.
- *River Trout & Frogs*: Found in the spring stream; harvestable with fishing rods or bug nets.
- *Honeybees*: Wild hives in oak trees; source of wax for candles and honey for cooking.

### 3.2 NPC Settlement Residents (Recruited into Built Housing)
1. **Dr. Raymond (Cyber-Surgeon)**: Moves in when an 8x5 room with an electric light and examination table is constructed. Installs and uninstalls bionic body augmentations.
2. **Weaver Selene (Arcane Biomancer)**: Binds chimeric beast tissues, crafts magic robes, and refines ancient crystals.
3. **Blacksmith Hargrove**: Sells iron ingots, forged blades, ammunition casings, and repairs broken tools.
4. **Merchant Silas**: Trades in general supplies, seeds, torches, batteries, and wire.
5. **Botanist Ivy**: Sells exotic crop seeds, nutrient soil fertilizers, and automated irrigation valves.
6. **Master Thorne (Trainer)**: Respecs allocated skill tree points for gold.

---

## 4. Procedural Dungeon Integration

- **The Descent Vault Entrance**:
  - Located in the bedrock cliff below the town center.
  - Interacting with the ancient console allows the player to select the unlocked subterranean dungeon tier (Tier 1 through Tier 8, and the Infinite Rift post-game).
  - Generates a fresh, procedurally assembled BSP dungeon instance each time the player enters.
- **Recovery Drone Landing Pad**:
  - Located beside the medical pod. When the player is defeated inside any dungeon, their consciousness is recalled to this pad, preserving all equipped gear while deploying a locator beacon to their dropped recovery drone.
