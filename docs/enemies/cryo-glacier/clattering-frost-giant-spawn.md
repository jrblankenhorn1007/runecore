## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the frost giant spawn has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
