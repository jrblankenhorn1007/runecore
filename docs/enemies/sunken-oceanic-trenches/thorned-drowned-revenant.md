## Lore
The drowned revenant is a deep leviathan native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
