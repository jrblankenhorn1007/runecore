# Slot 2: Eye Augmentations Catalog (22 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 22 eye augmentations (Thermal spectrum optics, ocular lasers, x-ray sensors, runic void eyes, and sniper scopes) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Eye Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: 1 Eye Augmentation installed at the Cyber-Clinic or Bio-Vat.
- **Vision Modes**: Eye augmentations provide specialized visual layers (thermal outlines, night-vision illumination, trap highlighting) and offensive ocular energy beams.
