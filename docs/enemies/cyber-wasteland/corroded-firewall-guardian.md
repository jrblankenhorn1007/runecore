## Lore
Few who study Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers dispute that the firewall guardian ranks among its defining hazards. This combat drone guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its quantum chips scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Shock Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
