2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 62 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the void seraph has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 73% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
