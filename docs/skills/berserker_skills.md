# Berserker Class Skill Tree: Frenzy, Blood Magic, Dual-Wield & Savage Fury

This document specifies all 52 class skills for the **Berserker (Barbarian Archetype)** in Untitled RPG. The Berserker thrives on low-health thresholds, ferocious dual-wield melee strings, self-mutilating blood pacts, and unstoppable rage.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Resource Dynamics
- **Core Resource**: Rage Pool ($RAG$, 0–100 scale). Builds rapidly as the Berserker lands hits and takes damage; slowly decays out of combat.
- **Low-Life Mechanics**: Berserker abilities scale aggressively in power as current health approaches 0%, gaining immense attack speed, lifesteal, and critical chance.
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Dual Greataxes, Hatchets, Claws, Flails, Spiked Knuckles.

---

## 2. Complete Berserker Skill Catalog (52 Skills)
