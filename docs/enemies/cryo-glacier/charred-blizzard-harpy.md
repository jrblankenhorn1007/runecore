## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 58% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 16 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 17 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The blizzard harpy did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
