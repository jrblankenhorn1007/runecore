## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the basilisk kin before anything else. As a gorgon spawn, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
