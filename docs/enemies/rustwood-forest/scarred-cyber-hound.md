## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 23% and 1 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -24%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 3 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Rustwood Forest warn newcomers about the cyber-hound before anything else. As a bandit raider, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
