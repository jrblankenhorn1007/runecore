## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 35% and 59 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 135 damage.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 86 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the precursor sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
