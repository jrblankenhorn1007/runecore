## Abilities

2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The ash wretch is a fire elemental native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 59% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
