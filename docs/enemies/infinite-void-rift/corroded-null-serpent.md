## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 136 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 139 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in The Infinite Void Rift warn newcomers about the null serpent before anything else. As a entropy spawn, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
