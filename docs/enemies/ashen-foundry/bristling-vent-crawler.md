## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 49% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -34%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 32 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 43 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the vent crawler as deceptively patient for a magma construct. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Caustic Spray is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
