## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the cryo servitor as deceptively patient for a ice elemental. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
