## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 35 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 40 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 66 damage in a 5-tile radius.

## Lore
Local guides in Haunted Graveyards & Mire warn newcomers about the cursed spirit before anything else. As a cursed spirit, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
