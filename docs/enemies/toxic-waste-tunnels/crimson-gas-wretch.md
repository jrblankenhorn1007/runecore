## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 71% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 78 damage in a 4-tile radius.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the gas wretch has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Summon Broodlings chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
