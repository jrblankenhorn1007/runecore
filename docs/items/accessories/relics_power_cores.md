# Pocket Relics, Battery Artifacts & Talismans Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, active cooldown powers, passive resource reserves, and implicit perks for pocket relics and battery artifacts across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~60 accessory definitions). Characters can equip 1 Pocket Relic in their utility accessory slot.

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Accessory Category**: Pocket Relic / Battery Core Slot.
- **Affix Bias**: Emphasizes auxiliary electrical power storage (+Power), continuous mana regeneration, portable light fields, and active utility buttons (triggering drones, flares, or energy overshields).
