## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 45% and 46 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 147 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 123 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Crystal Spires & Geodes, the gem spider has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 42% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
