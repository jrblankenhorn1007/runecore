## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 17% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -31%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 37 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 31 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 34 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the deep leviathan has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
