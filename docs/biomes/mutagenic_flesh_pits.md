# Biome Dossier: Mutagenic Flesh-Pits & Eldritch Womb

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Mutagenic Flesh-Pits & Eldritch Womb (Biome 16)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-16`, Tier 5 Organic Horror / Eldritch.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Horrifying organic bio-caverns located deep in the subterranean mantle ($Y = -600$ to $Y = -900$), where the world itself has mutated into living meat, eyes, and pulsating organs.
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Living Digestive Walls**: Terrain blocks squirt caustic digestive enzymes when mined or damaged, dealing 40 acid damage.
  - **Insanity & Psychic Whispers**: Standing near pulsating Eldritch Eye clusters induces Hallucination madness (-30% accuracy, causes illusory enemies to spawn).
  - **Suffocating Bile Pools**: Pools of amniotic fluid that pull players down and cause rapid bio-mutation infections.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 37°C (Internal body temperature of a colossal living organism).

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Pulsating Flesh Blocks, Calcified Bone Strata, Veined Muscle Walls, Adipose Tissue Blocks, Mucus Membrane Grates.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Bone Calcium Fragments, Calcified Chitin, Blood Crystals.
  - **Moderate**: Organic Iron Nodules, Amber Bile Nodes, Mutagenic Marrow.
  - **Rare**: Eldritch Eye Clusters, Corrupted Eldritch Flesh, Primal Genesis Enzymes.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Polyp Tendril Weeds**: Writhing parasitic polyps that reach out to grab ankles.
  - **Nerve-Root Vines**: Transmit bio-electrical pulses; used in cyber-organic nerve splicing.
  - **Bile-Spore Puffballs**: Explode into paralytic rot when brushed against.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Eldritch Gestation Chamber
- **Dungeon Structure**: Pulsating organic labyrinth inside the belly of a subterranean eldritch entity. Features beating heart chambers, peristaltic tunnel valves that open and close like sphincters, and amniotic nursery vats.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Severing biological arterial valves in a specific sequence to drain stomach acid chambers and expose the core nerve cluster.
- **Dungeon Boss: Shub-Niggurath the Flesh Matron (Level 54 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Massive circular organ chamber floating inside a sea of glowing mutagenic fluid.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Embedded in the back wall as a colossal organic queen; sweeps room with giant bone tentacles, vomits swarms of ravenous grubs, and fires blood laser beams from her central eye.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Tears herself free from the wall, emerging as a rampaging centipede-like horror; charges across platforms, infests floor with biting maws, and triggers "Cellular Rupture" where player must find safe amniotic pods to avoid being crushed by room contractions.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Flail of the Living Flesh (T5 Symbiote Whip), Chimeric Hive-Node (Head Augment), Genesis Bio-Catalyst, 3x Precursor Datacubes.
