## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 47% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 33 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
The bog zombie did not always haunt Haunted Graveyards & Mire; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 31% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
