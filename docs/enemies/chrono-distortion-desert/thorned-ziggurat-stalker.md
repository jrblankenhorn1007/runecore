## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 31% and 40 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 7 tiles to close the gap, dealing 38 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 43 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The ziggurat stalker is a precursor sentinel native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
