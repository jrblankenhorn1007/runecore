## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 55% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 83 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
The stone elemental did not always haunt Petrified Ancient Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
