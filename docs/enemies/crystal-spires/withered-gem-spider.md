## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 64 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Crystal Spires & Geodes dispute that the gem spider ranks among its defining hazards. This light wisp hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its gems scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
