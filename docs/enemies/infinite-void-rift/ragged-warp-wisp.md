## Abilities

2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 106 damage in a 5-tile radius.

## Lore
The warp wisp did not always haunt The Infinite Void Rift; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
