# Reanimator (The Necromancer / Cyber-Necromancer)

The Reanimator is the master of autonomous combat minions, cybernetic thrall fabrication, necrotic energy harvesting, and corpse detonation in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Playstyle

### 1.1 The Archetype
Fusing dark necromancy with automated cybernetic assembly, the Reanimator commands an entourage of constructed minions. They harvest Soul Fragments and metallic scrap from fallen enemies, using internal 3D-printing lattices to assemble combat drones and cyber-skeletal servitors on the fly.

- **Primary Combat Role**: Minion Summoner / Passive Area Control / Necrotic Dark DPS.
- **Weapon Specialization**: Reaping Scythes, Siphon Wands, Bone Crossbows, Necro-Canisters.
- **Survival Niche**: Converts monster corpses into scrap metal and crafting fuel; minions automate defense during settlement night raids; dark vision underground.

### 1.2 Baseline Attributes & Derived Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: 6 (Relies on minions for heavy lifting).
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: 7 (Standard precision).
- **Vitality (VIT)**: 8 ($+80$ Max HP).
- **Intelligence (INT)**: 15 ($+30.0\%$ minion damage scaling, $+75$ Max Mana).
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: 8 ($+0.8\text{ MP/s}$ mana regen).
- **Cyber-Affinity (CYB)**: 12 ($+120$ Power Capacity, drone assembly efficiency).

**Starting Derived Pools**:
- Health: $180\text{ HP}$
- Stamina: $50\text{ Stamina}$
- Mana: $110\text{ Mana}$
- Power: $90\text{ Power}$
- Humanity: $90$
- Stability: $90$

### 1.3 Starting Equipment Kit
- **Main Hand**: Scrap Reaping Scythe (15 physical/dark damage, 1.4 attacks/sec).
- **Off Hand**: Soul Siphon Wand (12 dark damage, siphons fragments).
- **Armor**: Servo-Jacket (+6 Armor, $+1$ Maximum Minion Capacity).
- **Consumables**: 3x Battery Cells, 2x Bio-Flasks.

---

## 2. Signature Class Mechanic: Soul Assembler

Slaying enemies grants a chance to generate glowing **Soul Fragments** on the ground:
- **Minion Fabrication**: The Reanimator consumes Soul Fragments to construct and maintain up to 4 autonomous minions:
  - *Flying Laser Drones*: Hover around the player and fire ranged pulse lasers.
  - *Skeletal Cyber-Thralls*: March forward in melee, engaging enemies with bone-saws.
- **Overmind Link**: Whenever an active minion attacks, the player restores 2 HP and 1 Mana.

## 3. Class Skill Tree & Abilities

### 3.1 Active Skills (Bound to Hotkeys `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`)

#### Active Q — Soul Harvest Dart
- **Hotbar Slot**: `Q`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 3.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Fires a necrotic energy dart at target enemy dealing 90 dark damage and generating 1 guaranteed Soul Fragment upon impact.

#### Active E — Fabricate Minion (Drone / Thrall)
- **Hotbar Slot**: `E`
- **Resource Cost**: 1 Soul Fragment
- **Cooldown**: 5.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Assembles a minion: Tapping `E` summons a Flying Laser Drone; holding `E` for 0.5s summons a Skeletal Cyber-Thrall (up to maximum minion cap).

#### Active R — Corpse Detonation
- **Hotbar Slot**: `R`
- **Resource Cost**: 20 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 6.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Targets a fallen enemy corpse or an active minion, detonating it in a violent acidic shrapnel burst that deals $260\%$ damage in a 3.5-tile radius and leaves an acid puddle for 5 seconds.

#### Active F — Swarm Overdrive
- **Hotbar Slot**: `F` (Ultimate)
- **Resource Cost**: 50 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 40.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Supercharges all active minions for 10.0 seconds: minions grow $50\%$ in size, gain $+100\%$ attack speed, and radiate a poisonous radioactive damage aura dealing $30\text{ damage/s}$ to all nearby foes.

---

### 3.2 Key Passive Specializations
1. *Harvest Protocol*: Defeating any enemy has a $35\%$ chance to automatically yield a Soul Fragment.
2. *Reinforced Chassis*: Minions gain $+40\%$ Maximum Health and $+15$ Armor.
3. *Drone Multi-Beam*: Flying laser drones fire 2 parallel beams instead of 1.
4. *Necrotic Siphon*: Whenever a minion strikes an enemy, the player restores 2 HP and 1 Mana.
5. *Dark Metallurgy*: Crafting and repairing gear costs $20\%$ less metal and scrap.
6. *Expanded Hangar*: Maximum minion capacity increased by $+2$ (Total 6 active minions).
7. *Acidic Fallout*: Corpse detonations leave an acid pool on the floor dealing damage for 5 seconds.
8. *Unholy Resonator*: Minion attack damage increased by $+1.5\%$ per point of INT.
9. *Self-Destruct Matrix*: Minions explode automatically upon death dealing $150\%$ damage to nearby enemies.
10. *Shadow Ward*: While at least 2 minions are alive, the player takes $15\%$ less damage from all sources.
11. *Master of the Grave*: Minions inherit $50\%$ of the player's critical strike chance and armor penetration.

## 4. Level 20 Free Signature Augmentation

### *Automated Minion Assembler Spine*
- **Body Slot**: Nervous System
- **Augmentation Type**: Cybernetic Automated Vertebral Rig
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 surgery credits, 0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep).
- **Core Functionality**:
  - Installs an automated micro-fabricator along the spinal column.
  - Whenever an active minion is destroyed, the spine automatically reconstructs a replacement instantly without consuming Soul Fragments (cooldown 12 seconds).
- **Cross-Class Exotic Surgery Trade-Off**:
  - Non-native installation penalty: $-30$ Humanity, 15 Power continuous upkeep, and $-20\%$ healing received.
