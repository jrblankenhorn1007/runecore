## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -30%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 53 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 128 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
The trench stalker did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
