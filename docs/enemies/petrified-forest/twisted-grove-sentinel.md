## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 45% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 23 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 24 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The grove sentinel did not always haunt Petrified Ancient Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near petrified wood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
