## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 59% and 53 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the tempest guardian before anything else. As a magnetic construct, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 21% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
