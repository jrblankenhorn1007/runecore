## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 23% and 50 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 139 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 133 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 128 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 60 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the alley stalker has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
