## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 35% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 87 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 73 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The hourglass golem did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
