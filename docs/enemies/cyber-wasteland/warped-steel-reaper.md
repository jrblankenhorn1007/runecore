## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 42 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The steel reaper is a emp turret native to Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
