## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 55 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Chrono-Distortion Desert warn newcomers about the glass elemental before anything else. As a sand horror, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
