## Abilities

2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the bog zombie ranks among its defining hazards. This cursed spirit patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near peat deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
