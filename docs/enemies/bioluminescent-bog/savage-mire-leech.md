## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 47% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 87 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the mire leech ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic amphibian lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 16% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
