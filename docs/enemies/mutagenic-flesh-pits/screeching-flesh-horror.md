## Lore
Fieldnotes from Mutagenic Flesh-Pits describe the flesh horror as deceptively patient for a genesis mutant. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
