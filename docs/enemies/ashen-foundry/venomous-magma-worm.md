## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 46% and 34 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -32%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 95 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 134 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 129 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
4. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 62 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the magma worm has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
