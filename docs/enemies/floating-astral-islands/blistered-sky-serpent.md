## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 64 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 38 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the sky serpent as deceptively patient for a void seraph. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 20% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
