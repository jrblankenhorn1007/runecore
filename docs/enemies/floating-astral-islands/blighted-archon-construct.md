## Lore
The archon construct did not always haunt Floating Astral Sky Islands; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 73% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
