# Slot 10: Hand Augmentations Catalog (22 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 22 hand augmentations (Micro-thruster palms, monomolecular nano-claws, alchemical touch fingertips, and grip clamps) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Hand Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Hand Augmentation installed across both wrists and hands.
- **Role**: Governs precision manual dexterity, weapon recoil stabilization, mid-air thrust hovering, unarmed lethal strikes, and harvesting yields.
