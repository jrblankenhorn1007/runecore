## Lore
Local guides in Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers warn newcomers about the arc elemental before anything else. As a servo horror, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
