## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 68% and 33 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -59%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 54 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 97 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the ash drake ranks among its defining hazards. This ember wraith charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
