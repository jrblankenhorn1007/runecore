## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 79 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 36 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The molten golem is a molten construct native to Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Volcanic Core has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
