## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 55% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 119 shock damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The tempest guardian did not always haunt Magnetic Thunder-Crags; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
