## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 34% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 61 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
The chrono construct is a glass elemental native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Piercing Shriek is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
