## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Necropolis warn newcomers about the ossuary crawler before anything else. As a undead revenant, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
