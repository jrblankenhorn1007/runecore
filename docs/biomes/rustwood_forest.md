# Rustwood Forest (Overworld Zone 1)

The Rustwood Forest is the temperate woodland region immediately flanking the Haven to the east and west. It is a dense, overgrown forest littered with the moss-covered, rusted hulks of ancient war walkers, artillery emplacements, and abandoned scavenger camps.

---

## 1. Biome Environment & Atmosphere

- **Biome Type**: Temperate Surface Woodland.
- **Ambient Temperature**: $+15^\circ\text{C}$ daytime, $+5^\circ\text{C}$ nighttime (Safe; mild cold at night).
- **Day/Night Cycle**:
  - Daytime: Dappled sunlight through oak canopies; woodland fauna and low-tier scavengers.
  - Nighttime: Deep darkness; nocturnal red-eyed Cyber-Hounds and Prowlers roam the forest floor.
- **Weather States**:
  - *Clear*: Standard visibility.
  - *Forest Fog*: Reduces enemy detection radius and player visibility to 15 tiles.
  - *Thunderstorm*: Heavy rain with occasional lightning strikes on metal tank wrecks; sparks create short-lived electric charge nodes.

---

## 2. Natural Resources & Mineral Deposits

- **Timber**:
  - *Ancient Oak Trees*: Hardwood timber yield for sturdy structural construction.
  - *Pine Trees*: Rich resin sap used for crafting torches and waterproof coatings.
- **Surface Ores & Mineral Veins**:
  - *Copper Ore Veins*: Exposed on rocky cliff walls.
  - *Surface Iron Nodules*: Buried along stream beds.
  - *Flint Deposits*: Harvested from river gravel.
- **Forageable Flora**:
  - *Wild Rust-Wheat*: Harvestable for grain and seeds.
  - *Soma Berry Bushes*: Bitter berries that restore minor health and cleanse poisons.
  - *Medicinal Healing Herb*: Fundamental alchemical reagent for crafting minor health potions.
  - *Forest Mushrooms*: Edible mushrooms found under shaded hollow logs.

---

## 3. Enemy Populations & Spawn Tables

### 3.1 Daytime Enemies (Levels 1–8)
- **Scrap Scavenger** (Melee): Human survivor wielding scrap machetes and shields. Patrols camps; drops scrap iron and cloth rags.
- **Scavenger Rifleman** (Ranged): Fires makeshift zip-guns from tree platforms; drops light ammo and copper pins.
- **Feral Wild Boar** (Beast): Charges with tusks when provoked; drops raw pork meat, animal fat, and boar hide.
- **Timber Wolf** (Beast): Hunts in packs of 2 to 3; fast leaping bite attacks; drops wolf pelts and sharp fangs.
- **Rogue Sentry Drone** (Machine): Low-flying recon drone armed with a weak taser spark; drops micro-batteries and copper wire.

### 3.2 Nighttime Enemies (Levels 5–12)
- **Rabid Cyber-Hound** (Cyborg Beast): Fast canine enhanced with metal jaws and glowing red eye implants; deals bleed damage and pursues relentlessly.
- **Night Prowler** (Mutant): Cloaked humanoid stalking from trees; leaps down with monomolecular shivs; drops shadow silk.
- **Corrupted Steam Servitor** (Machine): Malfunctioning maintenance droid venting scalding steam; high armor; drops steel springs and gears.

---

## 4. Procedural Dungeon Vaults in Rustwood Forest

Hidden among the roots of massive ancient trees and buried under collapsed tank carcasses lie entrances to the **Rustwood Forest Bunkers & Catacombs**:
- **Dungeon Architectural Style**: Brick-and-mortar stone vaults reinforced with rusted corrugated steel sheets and dripping steam valves.
- **Dungeon Hazards**: Swinging spiked log traps, collapsing floor grates, and tripwire fragmentation mines.
- **Dungeon Boss Mini-Encounters**:
  - *The Scavenger Overboss*: Heavily armored bandit captain wielding an explosive flak hand-cannon and riot shield.
  - *Matriarch Boar*: Gigantic scarred beast with iron plates lodged in its skull; charges destructively through stone support pillars.
