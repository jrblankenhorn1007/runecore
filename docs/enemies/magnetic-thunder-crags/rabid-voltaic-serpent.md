## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 56% and 39 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -6%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 85 damage.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 128 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 67 damage at range.

## Lore
Few who study Magnetic Thunder-Crags dispute that the voltaic serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This lightning wraith circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its neodymium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
