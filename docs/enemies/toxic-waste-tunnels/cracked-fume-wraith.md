## Lore
The fume wraith is a gas beast native to Toxic Waste Tunnels, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Toxic Waste Tunnels has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
