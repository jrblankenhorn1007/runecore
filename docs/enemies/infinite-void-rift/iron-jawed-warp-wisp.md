## Abilities

2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 58 damage at range.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 93 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the warp wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This singularity construct charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 41% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
