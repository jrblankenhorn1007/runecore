## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 52% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -7%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The fried circuit horror did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
