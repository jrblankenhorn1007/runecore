## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 41% and 26 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -7%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 34 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Necropolis describe the rot zombie as deceptively patient for a bone construct. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Weakpoint Overload is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
