## Lore
The fractured echo did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glass sand deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
