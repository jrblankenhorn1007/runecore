## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 48% and 8 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 25 damage at range.

## Lore
The kiln horror is a magma construct native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
