## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 62% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -39%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 16 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the cinder hound as deceptively patient for a fire elemental. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
