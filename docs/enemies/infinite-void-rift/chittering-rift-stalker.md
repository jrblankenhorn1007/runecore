## Lore
The rift stalker is a abyssal titan native to The Infinite Void Rift, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that The Infinite Void Rift has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
