## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 134 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 62 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 89 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ancient Ruined Megacity describe the salvage scavenger as deceptively patient for a radiation ghoul. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
