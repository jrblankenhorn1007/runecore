## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the gloom bat has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near granite deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
