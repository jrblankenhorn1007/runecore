## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 45% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -6%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 249 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 273 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 284 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 284 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Radioactive Crater Basin describe the geiger wretch as deceptively patient for a mutant ghoul. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
