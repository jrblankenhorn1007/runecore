## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 57% and 30 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 80 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 96 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The sludge ooze did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
