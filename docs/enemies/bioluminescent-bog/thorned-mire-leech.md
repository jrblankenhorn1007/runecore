## Lore
The mire leech is a toxic amphibian native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
