## Lore
The sky whale calf is a void seraph native to Floating Astral Sky Islands, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Floating Astral Sky Islands has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
