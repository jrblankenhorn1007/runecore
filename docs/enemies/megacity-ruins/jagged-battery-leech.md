## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 31% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -28%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 41 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 16 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The battery leech did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
