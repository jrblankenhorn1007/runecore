## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 23 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 14 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Few who study Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps dispute that the cinder hound ranks among its defining hazards. This fire elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its basalt scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
