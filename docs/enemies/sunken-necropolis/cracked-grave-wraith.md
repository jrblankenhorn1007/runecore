## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the grave wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
