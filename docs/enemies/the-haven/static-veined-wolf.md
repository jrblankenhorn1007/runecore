## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 17% and 1 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 8 damage at range.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 7 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the wolf as deceptively patient for a wild avian. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
