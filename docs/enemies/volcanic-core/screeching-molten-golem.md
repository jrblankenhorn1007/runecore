## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 72% and 34 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -42%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 58 damage in a 4-tile radius.
3. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 66 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches warn newcomers about the molten golem before anything else. As a ember wraith, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
