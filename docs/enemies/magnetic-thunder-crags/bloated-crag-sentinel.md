## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 57% and 44 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the crag sentinel before anything else. As a static horror, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
