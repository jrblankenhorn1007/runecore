## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 37% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 35 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 46 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the peat ghoul has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
