## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 48% and 52 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 92 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 68 damage.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 118 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
4. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 82 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Haunted Graveyards & Mire, the tombstone horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near peat deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
