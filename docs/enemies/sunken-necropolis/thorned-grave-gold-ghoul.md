## Lore
The grave-gold ghoul is a grave wraith native to Sunken Necropolis, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
