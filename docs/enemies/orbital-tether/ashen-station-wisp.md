## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 45% and 47 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 147 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 73 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the station wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This vacuum horror ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
