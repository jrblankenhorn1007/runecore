## Abilities

2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 35 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks warn newcomers about the hoarfrost bear before anything else. As a cryo wraith, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
