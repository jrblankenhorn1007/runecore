## Lore
The gestation horror is a genesis mutant native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
