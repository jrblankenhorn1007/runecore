## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 6% and 19 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 77 damage at range.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 72 damage.
4. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 59 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The boar is a cyborg hound native to Rustwood Forest, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
