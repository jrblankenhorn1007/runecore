## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 54 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the archon construct has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
