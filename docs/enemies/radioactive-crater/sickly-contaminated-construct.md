## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 79 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The contaminated construct did not always haunt Radioactive Crater Basin; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near uranium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
