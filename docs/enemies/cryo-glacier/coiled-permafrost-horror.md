## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 45 damage at range.

## Lore
Local guides in Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks warn newcomers about the permafrost horror before anything else. As a cryo wraith, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
