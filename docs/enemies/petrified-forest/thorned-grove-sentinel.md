## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 58% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 90 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 99 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the grove sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Suppressing Fire chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
