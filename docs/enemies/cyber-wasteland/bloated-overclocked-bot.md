## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 57% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -9%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 187 damage in a 6-tile radius.
4. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 125 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
The overclocked bot did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near quantum chips deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
