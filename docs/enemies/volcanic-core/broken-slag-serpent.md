## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -56%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 102 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 76 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the slag serpent as deceptively patient for a lava wyrm. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
