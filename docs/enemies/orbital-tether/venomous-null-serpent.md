## Lore
The null serpent did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
