## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 45% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -4%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 38 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 42 damage.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the sarcophagus horror ranks among its defining hazards. This cursed guardian breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
