## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 65% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (moderate, -6%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 144 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 93 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the combat drone as deceptively patient for a arc elemental. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
