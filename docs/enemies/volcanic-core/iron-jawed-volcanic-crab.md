## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 76% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -59%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 80 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 111 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 96 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the volcanic crab as deceptively patient for a lava wyrm. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
