## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 75% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 72 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 99 shock damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The storm-bound revenant is a magnetic construct native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 40% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
