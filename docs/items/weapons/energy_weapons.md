# Energy Weapons Catalog: Lasers, Arc Weapons, Plasma Projectors & Railguns

This document specifies the directed-energy weapons, continuous laser emitters, arc discharge conduits, high-thermal plasma blasters, and electromagnetic railguns in Untitled RPG. These weapons utilize Bionic Power or dedicated battery cells, completely bypassing mechanical recoil in exchange for thermal heat management.

---

## 1. Energy Weapon Dynamics & Heat Mechanics
- **Thermal Heat Gauge**: Continuous firing builds weapon heat ($0\% \to 100\%$). Reaching $100\%$ triggers thermal shutdown/venting, disabling fire for 3–5 seconds unless equipped with Cryo-Heatsink augments.
- **Power Siphon**: Consumes Bionic Power from the player's core battery or external energy cells instead of physical gunpowder ammunition.
- **Elemental Affinities**: Pure Shock/Lightning, Superheated Plasma (Thermal/Fire), Disintegration Hard-Light, or Void Flux.
