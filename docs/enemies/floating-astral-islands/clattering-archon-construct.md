## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the archon construct has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
