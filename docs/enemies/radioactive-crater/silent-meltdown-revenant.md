## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 40% and 51 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 139 damage.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 168 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the meltdown revenant has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
