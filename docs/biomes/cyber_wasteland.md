# Biome Dossier: Cyber-Wasteland & Automated Factory Bunkers

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Cyber-Wasteland & Automated Factory Bunkers (Biome 15)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-15`, Tier 5 Cybernetic / Industrial.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Scorched technological wastelands and subterranean automated manufacturing megaplexes ($X = 4000$ to $X = 6000$, Subterranean & Surface).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **High-Voltage Arc Fences & Laser Grids**: Automated defense systems that pulse deadly beams; stepping through laser tripwires triggers automated turret fire and 80 electric damage.
  - **Automated Heavy Hydraulic Presses**: Industrial stamping pistons that cycle every 2 seconds, crushing anything caught underneath for lethal physical damage.
  - **Electromagnetic Pulses (EMP)**: Intermittent facility power surges drain 50 Bionic Power and temporarily disable cybernetic limbs for 3 seconds without Grounding Augments.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 28°C (Industrial heat, smell of ozone and motor oil).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Reinforced Steel Hull Plates, Industrial Catwalk Grates, Concrete Foundation Blocks, Copper Busbars, Silicon Circuit Walls.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Scrap Electronics, Heavy Steel Beams, Copper Wire.
  - **Moderate**: Titanium Plates, Lithium Batteries, Neodymium Magnets.
  - **Rare**: Precursor Logic Gates, Quantum Microprocessors, Nanite Sovereign Cores.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Synthetic Fiber Grass**: Artificially engineered carbon-nanotube turf grown over rusted steel.
  - **Coolant Lichen**: Bioluminescent blue mold that grows on leaking freon coolant pipes.
  - **Bio-Battery Reeds**: Harvested for organic chemical electrolytes.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Assembly Complex Alpha-9
- **Dungeon Structure**: Sprawling multi-tier robotic manufacturing foundry with functioning conveyor belts, robotic assembly arms, molten metal dipping vats, and automated laser quality-control scanners.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Overriding master mainframe security terminals by hacking logic consoles, deactivating automated laser kill-grids and unlocking central factory elevator shafts.
- **Dungeon Boss: Unit-00 "The Factory Overlord" (Level 50 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Enormous industrial hangar with conveyor belts moving weapons and explosive barrels continuously across the floor.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Suspended from overhead gantry rails; fires quad-missile volleys, sweeps arena with heavy industrial mining lasers, and activates conveyor belts to pull player into sawblades.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Detaches from gantry, dropping down as a colossal 6-legged spider mech; discharges radial EMP shockwaves, summons waves of combat drones, and enters overdrive rotary cannon fire.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Overlord Rotary Gatling (T5 Minigun), Nanite Auto-Repair Core (Heart Augment), Quantum Mainframe Chip, 3x Precursor Datacubes.
