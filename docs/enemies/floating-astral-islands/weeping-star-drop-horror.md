## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the star-drop horror ranks among its defining hazards. This void seraph patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
