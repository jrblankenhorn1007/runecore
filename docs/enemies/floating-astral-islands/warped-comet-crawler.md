## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 27% and 86 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 265 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 208 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 137 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The comet crawler did not always haunt Floating Astral Sky Islands; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Void Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
