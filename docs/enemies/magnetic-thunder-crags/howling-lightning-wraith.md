## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 67% and 44 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -9%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 81 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 117 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the lightning wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 74% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
