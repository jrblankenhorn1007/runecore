# Biome Dossier: Petrified Ancient Forest & Stone Groves

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Petrified Ancient Forest & Stone Groves (Biome 18)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-18`, Tier 4 Ancient / Terrestrial.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Ancient prehistoric forest turned to solid stone by alchemical cataclysm, located along the eastern continental foothills ($X = 3000$ to $X = 4500$, Surface).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Petrifying Mist & Calcification**: Low-hanging grey vapor; breathing it without filtration or alchemical antidotes accumulates "Petrification" stacks. Reaching 100 stacks turns the player into stone for 4 seconds, increasing physical armor by 50% but preventing all movement.
  - **Falling Stone Branches**: Chopping or shattering petrified trees releases razor-sharp quartz stalactites and heavy stony boughs dealing 65 physical damage.
  - **Razor-Gravel Dunes**: Sharp flint and agate gravel that shreds unreinforced boots and causes bleed.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 14°C (Stagnant, dry, dead air).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Petrified Wood Logs, Silicified Bark Blocks, Agate Stone Strata, Flint Nodules, Grey Calcified Earth.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Petrified Wood Chunks, Flint, Grey Basalt.
  - **Moderate**: Moss Agate, Banded Onyx, Calcite Formations.
  - **Rare**: Fossilized Amber with Precursor Insects, Gorgon Bile Crystals, Crystalline Heartwood.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Stone-Bloom Blossoms**: Rigid petrified orchids whose powdered petals form the basis for petrification cure salves.
  - **Lichen of the Basilisks**: Yellow encrusted lichen growing on statue victims; used in stasis oils.
  - **Calcified Vines**: Inflexible stone vines that can be pulverized into heavy ceramic flux.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Sunken Grove of the Gorgon Queen
- **Dungeon Structure**: Sprawling ancient temple overgrown with petrified banyan trees and lined with realistic statues of terrified warriors from past civilizations. Features mirror-shield reflection hallways, shifting stone slab gates, and subterranean root catacombs.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Polishing weathered bronze mirrors to reflect the gaze of petrifying Gorgon statues back onto stone seal gates to shatter them.
- **Dungeon Boss: Queen Medusa the Petrifying Primordial (Level 44 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Grand hypostyle hall lined with hundreds of petrified victim statues and polished stone columns.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Slithers rapidly across the arena, firing poison-tipped arrows and unleashing wide-cone petrifying gazes (player must turn away or hide behind stone pillars to avoid instant calcification).
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Commands the victim statues in the room to come alive as brittle stone warriors; leaps onto temple rafters, raining down explosive venom flasks and shattering pillars with deafening sonic screeches.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Bow of the Stone-Gaze (T4 Ranged Weapon), Gorgon Eye Amulet (Accessory), Petrification Immunity Dermis (Skin Augment), 2x Precursor Datacubes.
