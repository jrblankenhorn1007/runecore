## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 48% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -42%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 24 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 18 damage.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 21 damage at range.

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the slag hulk before anything else. As a forge golem, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
