## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the ziggurat stalker has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 84% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
