## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 53% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -9%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 88 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 79 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the stone elemental before anything else. As a gorgon spawn, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
