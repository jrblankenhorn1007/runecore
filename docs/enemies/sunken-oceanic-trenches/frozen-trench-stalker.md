## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 27% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -36%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 114 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 71 damage.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The trench stalker is a pressure horror native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
