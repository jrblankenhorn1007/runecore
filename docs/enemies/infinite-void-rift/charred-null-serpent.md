## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 71% and 99 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 152 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 239 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
4. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 203 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The null serpent did not always haunt The Infinite Void Rift; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
