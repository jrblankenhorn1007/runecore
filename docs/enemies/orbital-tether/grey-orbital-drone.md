## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 29% and 97 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 229 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 280 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 252 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
4. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 204 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the orbital drone as deceptively patient for a void sentinel. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
