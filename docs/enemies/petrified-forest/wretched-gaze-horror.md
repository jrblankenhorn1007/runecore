## Abilities

3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
The gaze horror is a root horror native to Petrified Ancient Forest, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
