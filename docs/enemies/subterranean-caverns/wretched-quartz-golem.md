## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 17% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 19 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 21 damage.

## Lore
The quartz golem is a cave beast native to Subterranean Caverns, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Subterranean Caverns has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
