## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 75 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 112 physical damage to the first target struck.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 73 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The refraction wraith did not always haunt Crystal Spires & Geodes; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near gems deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
