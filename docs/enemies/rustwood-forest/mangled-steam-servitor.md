## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -28%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 28 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 35 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 23 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
The steam servitor did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
