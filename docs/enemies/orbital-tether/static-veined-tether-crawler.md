## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the tether crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This zero-g construct guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
