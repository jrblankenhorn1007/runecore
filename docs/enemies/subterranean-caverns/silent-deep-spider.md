## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 43 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 51 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the deep spider before anything else. As a subterranean insect, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 36% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
