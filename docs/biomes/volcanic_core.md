# Biome Dossier: Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches (Biome 09)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-09`, Tier 4 Abyssal/Subterranean.
- **Location & Depths**: Directly below the Ashen Foundry at the deepest bedrock mantle ($Y = -500$ to $Y = -800$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Lethal Lava Seas**: Stepping into liquid magma deals 80 fire damage per half-second and causes permanent burn for 10s. Requires Fireproof Boots or Lavawalker Bionics.
  - **Extreme Radiant Heat**: Ambient heat exceeds 90°C. Players without Thermal Heat Sinks, Coolant Injections, or Flame-Retardant Suits suffer Heatstroke (-50% stamina regen, continuous dehydration).
  - **Volcanic Eruptions**: Geothermal vents intermittently erupt towering columns of flame and explosive pumice blocks.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 85°C to 110°C (Extreme Heat).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Magma Basalt, Volcanic Pumice, Obsidian Glass Blocks, Liquid Lava, Hardened Ash Slag.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Obsidian Shards, Sulfur Crystals, Iron Ore.
  - **Moderate**: Titanium Veins, Fire-Ruby Geodes, Pyrite.
  - **Rare**: Ignis Magma Cores, Volcanic Diamond, Molten Gold.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Flame-Root Fern**: Growing directly inside cooling magma crusts; used in fire resistance potions.
  - **Ember-Spore Caps**: Glowing red mushrooms that emit heat; explode in fire when harvested carelessly.
  - **Ash-Lichen**: Harvested for high-temperature alchemical binding agents.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Caldera Vault of Vulcan
- **Dungeon Structure**: Ancient industrial Precursor geothermal refinery suspended directly over a roaring lake of magma. Features moving floating platforms, steam-vent propulsion elevators, and automated smelting vats.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Redirecting geothermal coolant pipes using pipe wrenches to cool molten walkways into solid obsidian bridges.
- **Dungeon Boss: Ignis the Smelting Titan (Level 36 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Octagonal metal platform surrounded by 4 pouring molten crucible waterfalls.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Swings a colossal molten tongs warhammer, stomps to send waves of fire across the arena, and summons magma salamanders.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Submerges into the central lava vat, emerging superheated and glowing white-hot (+100% attack speed, leaves a trail of burning fire, emits radial heat waves).
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Ignis Flame Cleaver (T4 Greataxe), Molten Core Bionic Heart, Thermal Heat Sink Augment, 2x Precursor Datacubes.
