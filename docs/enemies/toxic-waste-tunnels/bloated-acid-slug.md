## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 69% and 50 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -22%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 96 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 106 damage and applying a stagger.
4. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 126 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the acid slug ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
