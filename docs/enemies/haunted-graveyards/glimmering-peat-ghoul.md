## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 116 damage.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 104 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the peat ghoul ranks among its defining hazards. This grave construct favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
