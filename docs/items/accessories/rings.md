# Rings, Bands & Signets Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, attribute enhancements, unique proc chances, and implicit affixes for rings and finger bands across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~140 accessory definitions). Players can equip up to 2 rings simultaneously.

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 2 rings equipped simultaneously (Ring Slot 1 & Ring Slot 2).
- **Affix Scaling**: Rings roll specialized utility, attribute (+STR, +DEX, +INT, +VIT, +WIS, +CYB), elemental resistance, critical strike, and resource efficiency modifiers.
- **Rarity Budget**: Rare rings roll 2 affixes, Epic roll 3 affixes, Legendary roll 4 affixes + 1 Unique Perk, and Prismatic roll 5 affixes + 2 Unique Perks.
