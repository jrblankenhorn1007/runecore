## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 75% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -25%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 73 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 64 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Toxic Waste Tunnels, the corroded construct has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 85% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
