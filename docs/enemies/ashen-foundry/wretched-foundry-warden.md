## Lore
The foundry warden is a industrial drone native to Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ashen Foundry has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
