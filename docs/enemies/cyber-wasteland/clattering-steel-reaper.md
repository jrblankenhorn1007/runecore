## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 62% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -12%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 60 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers, the steel reaper has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 35% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
