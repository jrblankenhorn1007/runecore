## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 83 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 114 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the aetherial hawk has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its physical weakness rewards elemental prep.
