## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -11%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 225 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 291 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the vat-born spawn before anything else. As a chimeric beast, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
