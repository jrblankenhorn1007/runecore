## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 60% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 60 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the stone elemental ranks among its defining hazards. This stone elemental swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
