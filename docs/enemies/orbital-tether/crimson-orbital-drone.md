## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 114 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 104 damage at range.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The orbital drone did not always haunt Precursor Orbital Tether; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
