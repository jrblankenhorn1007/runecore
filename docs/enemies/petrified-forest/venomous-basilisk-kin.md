## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 74 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 76 physical damage to the first target struck.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the basilisk kin before anything else. As a petrified guardian, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 37% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
