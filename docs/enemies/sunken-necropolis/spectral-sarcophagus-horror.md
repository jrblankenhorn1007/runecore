## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 27% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 52 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The sarcophagus horror did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
