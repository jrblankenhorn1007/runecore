## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 42% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 55 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 38 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 72 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Bioluminescent Fungal Bog warn newcomers about the bog toad before anything else. As a spore beast, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
