## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 24 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 36 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the ram as deceptively patient for a feral livestock. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 17% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
