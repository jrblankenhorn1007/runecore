## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 69% and 58 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 164 damage.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 181 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the ferrous crawler before anything else. As a magnetic construct, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
