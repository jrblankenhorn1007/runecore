## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 16% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 13 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
The gloom bat did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Regenerative Pulse chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
