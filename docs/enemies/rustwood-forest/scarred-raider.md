## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 22% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -30%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The raider did not always haunt Rustwood Forest; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
