## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 64% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -42%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 42 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 40 frost damage to the first target struck.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the blizzard harpy as deceptively patient for a frozen revenant. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Frost Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
