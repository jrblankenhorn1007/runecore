# Monster Parts & Trophies Catalog

This document specifies organic, chimeric, and mutant trophies, organs, bones, and glands harvested from the fauna, mutants, and eldritch horrors of Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Monster Harvest Mechanics
- **Procurement**: Dropped by beasts, predatory wildlife, chimeric hybrids, and boss monsters upon death. Slicing with harvesting knives or hunting daggers increases yield by up to 100%.
- **Uses**: Primary reagents for Chimeric bio-grafts, alchemical potions, leather tanning, bone weapons, and trophy decorations.
