## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 62% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 45 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 29 damage at range.

## Lore
Few who study Toxic Waste Tunnels dispute that the chem leech ranks among its defining hazards. This toxic mutant lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its toxic slime scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
