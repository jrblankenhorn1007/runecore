## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 24% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -23%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 14 damage in a 6-tile radius.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 22 damage at range.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 9 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The trapper is a bandit raider native to Rustwood Forest, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
