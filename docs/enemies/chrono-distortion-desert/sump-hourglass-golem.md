## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 81 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 45 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Chrono-Distortion Desert describe the hourglass golem as deceptively patient for a precursor sentinel. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 30% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
