2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 93 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the flesh horror before anything else. As a enzyme ooze, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
