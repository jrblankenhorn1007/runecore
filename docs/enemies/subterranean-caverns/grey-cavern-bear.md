## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 35% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 34 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 3 projectiles, each dealing 46 damage at range.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 22 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the cavern bear has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
