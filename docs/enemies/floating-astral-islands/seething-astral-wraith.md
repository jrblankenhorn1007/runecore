## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the astral wraith ranks among its defining hazards. This astral wraith hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
