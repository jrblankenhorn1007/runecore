## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 59% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 109 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 63 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 82 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the arc hound before anything else. As a thunder beast, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Caustic Spray is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
