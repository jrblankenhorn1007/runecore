## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 114 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 77 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the glass wind wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
