## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 42 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -47%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 156 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 88 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the lava wyrm as deceptively patient for a magma elemental. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
