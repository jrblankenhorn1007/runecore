## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 24% and 5 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 11 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the sentry drone as deceptively patient for a feral predator. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 17% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
