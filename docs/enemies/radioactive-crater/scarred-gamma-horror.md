## Lore
Fieldnotes from Radioactive Crater Basin describe the gamma horror as deceptively patient for a fallout beast. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Toxin Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
