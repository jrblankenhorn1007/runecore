## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Infinite Void Rift, the antimatter golem has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 31% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
