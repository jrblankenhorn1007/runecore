## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 55% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 56 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 83 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the gas wretch before anything else. As a toxic mutant, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near toxic slime deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
