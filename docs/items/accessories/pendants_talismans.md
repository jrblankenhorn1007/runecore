# Pendants, Charms & Runic Talismans Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, elemental wards, proc effects, and implicit affixes for pendants, charms, and runic talismans across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~80 accessory definitions).

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Accessory Category**: Secondary Neck & Brooch Slot / Pouch Relic.
- **Affix Bias**: Talismans and charms emphasize defensive elemental wards, status cleanse procs, resource recovery rates (+HP/s, +MP/s, +Power/s), and harvesting luck.
