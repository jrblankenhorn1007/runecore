## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 21% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -20%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 19 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the mole horror before anything else. As a subterranean insect, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Corrosive Spit is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
