## Lore
The grave-gold ghoul is a soul parasite native to Sunken Necropolis, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Void Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
