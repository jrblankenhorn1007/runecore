## Abilities

## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the alley stalker ranks among its defining hazards. This salvage scavenger guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
