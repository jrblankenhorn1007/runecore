## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the pressure wraith as deceptively patient for a pressure horror. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
