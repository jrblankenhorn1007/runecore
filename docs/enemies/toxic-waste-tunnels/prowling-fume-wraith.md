## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 27 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 72 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 44 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Toxic Waste Tunnels describe the fume wraith as deceptively patient for a gas beast. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
