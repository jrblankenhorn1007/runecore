## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 37 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -40%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 124 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 63 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
4. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 59 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps, the molten crab has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 55% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
