## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the root horror ranks among its defining hazards. This stone elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its bio-magnetic sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
