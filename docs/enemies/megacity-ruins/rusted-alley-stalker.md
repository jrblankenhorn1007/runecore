## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 38% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 70 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 64 damage and applying a short knockback.
3. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.

## Lore
The alley stalker is a feral mutant native to Ancient Ruined Megacity, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ancient Ruined Megacity has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
