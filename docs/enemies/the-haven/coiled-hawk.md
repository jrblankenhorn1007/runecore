## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 9% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -21%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 22 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 39 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 20 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the hawk as deceptively patient for a wild avian. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Physical Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
