## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 61% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 153 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 140 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Few who study Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers dispute that the firewall guardian ranks among its defining hazards. This emp turret ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its quantum chips scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Grapple is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
