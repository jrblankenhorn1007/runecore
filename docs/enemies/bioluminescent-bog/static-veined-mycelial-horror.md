## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the mycelial horror ranks among its defining hazards. This glowing swarm lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 16% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
