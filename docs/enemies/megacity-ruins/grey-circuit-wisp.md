## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the circuit wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This security construct breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its sound detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
