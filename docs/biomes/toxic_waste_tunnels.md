# Biome Dossier: Toxic Waste Tunnels & Chemical Sewers

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Toxic Waste Tunnels & Chemical Sewers (Biome 10)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-10`, Tier 3 Industrial Subterranean.
- **Location & Depths**: Underground beneath the Ashen Foundry and industrial factory complexes ($Y = -200$ to $Y = -400$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Caustic Sludge Waters**: Liquid green chemical runoff deals 50 acid damage per second and rapidly corrodes equipped armor durability. Requires Acid-Proof Waders or Poly-Dermis Augments.
  - **Toxic Fumes & Corrosive Gas**: Ambient air is saturated with chlorine and fluoroantimonic acid gas; drains health unless wearing a Sealed Hazmat Mask or Toxin Scrubber lungs.
  - **Explosive Gas Pockets**: Firing energy weapons or throwing flames into yellow gas pockets causes devastating secondary chain explosions.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 24°C (Stifling, humid, chemical stench).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Corroded Concrete Blocks, Rusted Steel Grates, Slime-Coated Brick, Acid-Etched Tile, Green Sludge Liquid.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Scrap Metal, Rusted Iron Pipes, Sulfur Slag.
  - **Moderate**: Copper Wire Conduits, Lead Plates, Lithium Deposits.
  - **Rare**: Fluoroantimonic Acid Crystals, Uranium Flakes, Mutagenic Ooze.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Mutated Slime-Fungus**: Glows neon green; primary ingredient in acidic explosives.
  - **Filter-Reeds**: Grows in sludge pools; roots can be harvested to craft detoxifying filters.
  - **Bio-Hazard Spore Caps**: Release corrosive spores when stepped on.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Waste Treatment Facility Zeta
- **Dungeon Structure**: Massive multi-level Precursor sewage and chemical processing facility featuring giant drainage pipes, rotating impeller turbines, filtration tanks, and sluice gate valves.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Turning rusted pipe valves to drain acidic sludge levels, revealing submerged metal catwalks and hidden security vaults.
- **Dungeon Boss: The Amalgamated Bio-Sludge Matron (Level 32 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Huge circular drainage cistern with acid sludge slowly rising from below.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Flings massive globs of acidic bile, summons swarms of sludge blobs, and pounds ground to shake down toxic ceiling debris.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Floods the room with acid, forcing player onto elevated grating pipes. The boss becomes acidic-armored, firing high-velocity acid jets and sweeping tentacles.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Caustic Sludge Projector (T3 Heavy Weapon), Hazmat Breathing Matrix (Lung Augment), 2x Precursor Datacubes.
