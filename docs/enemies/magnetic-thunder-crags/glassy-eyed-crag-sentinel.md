## Lore
Few who study Magnetic Thunder-Crags dispute that the crag sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This thunder beast charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its neodymium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
