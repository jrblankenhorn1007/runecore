## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Petrified Ancient Forest, the petrifying wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
