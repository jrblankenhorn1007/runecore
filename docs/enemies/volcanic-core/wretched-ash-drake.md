## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 76% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -60%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 124 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 93 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the ash drake ranks among its defining hazards. This molten construct patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near obsidian deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
