## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 61% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -37%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 50 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 23 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the kiln horror as deceptively patient for a magma construct. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 27% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
