## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 52% and 35 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -27%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 117 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 114 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
4. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 56 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the magma worm before anything else. As a industrial drone, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Ground Slam chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
