## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 23% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -22%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 87 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 91 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Chrono-Distortion Desert describe the time-locked revenant as deceptively patient for a glass elemental. It breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
