## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 15% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -21%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 22 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 23 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Rustwood Forest, the poacher has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
