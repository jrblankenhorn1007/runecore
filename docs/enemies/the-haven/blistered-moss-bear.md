## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 6 damage.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Few who study The Haven (Hub Valley) dispute that the moss bear ranks among its defining hazards. This scavenger vermin hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its oak timber scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
