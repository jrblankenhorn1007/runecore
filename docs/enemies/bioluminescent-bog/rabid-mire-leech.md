3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 63 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Bioluminescent Fungal Bog describe the mire leech as deceptively patient for a fungal horror. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Bleeding Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
