## Lore
The orbital drone is a solar wraith native to Precursor Orbital Tether, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Precursor Orbital Tether has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
