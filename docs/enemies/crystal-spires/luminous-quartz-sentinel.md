## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 40% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -9%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Local guides in Crystal Spires & Geodes warn newcomers about the quartz sentinel before anything else. As a prismatic elemental, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 50% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
