## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 93 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 103 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Magnetic Thunder-Crags warn newcomers about the tempest guardian before anything else. As a static horror, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
