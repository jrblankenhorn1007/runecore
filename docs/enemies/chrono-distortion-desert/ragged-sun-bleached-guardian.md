## Lore
The sun-bleached guardian is a chrono construct native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
