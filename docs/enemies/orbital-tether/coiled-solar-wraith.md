3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 139 damage at range.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Precursor Orbital Tether, the solar wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Summon Broodlings is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
