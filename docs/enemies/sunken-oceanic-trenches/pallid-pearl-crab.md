## Lore
The pearl crab is a drowned revenant native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
