## Lore
The screaming flesh mass did not always haunt Mutagenic Flesh-Pits; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.
