- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 38% and 12 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 16 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 38 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ancient Ruined Megacity describe the circuit wisp as deceptively patient for a rogue drone. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Corrosive Spit chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
