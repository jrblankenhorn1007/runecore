## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 28% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 20 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the echo wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 29% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
