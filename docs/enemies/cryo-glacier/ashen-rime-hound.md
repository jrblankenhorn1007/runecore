## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 45% and 15 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 57 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 65 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 65 damage.

## Lore
The rime hound did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near glacial ice deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
