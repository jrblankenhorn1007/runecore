## Lore
The drowned revenant is a drowned revenant native to Sunken Oceanic Trenches, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Oceanic Trenches has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
