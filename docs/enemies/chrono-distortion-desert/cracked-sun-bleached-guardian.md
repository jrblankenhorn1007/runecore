## Abilities

2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 315 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 311 damage.
4. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 251 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Chrono-Distortion Desert, the sun-bleached guardian has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its echolocation detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
