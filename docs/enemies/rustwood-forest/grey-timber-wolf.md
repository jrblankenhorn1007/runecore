## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the timber wolf ranks among its defining hazards. This cyborg hound hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near ironwood deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
