## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 73% and 68 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -48%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 144 damage.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 103 fire damage to the first target struck.
4. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the fire elemental ranks among its defining hazards. This ember wraith hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
