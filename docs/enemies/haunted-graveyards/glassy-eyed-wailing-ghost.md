## Lore
Local guides in Haunted Graveyards & Mire warn newcomers about the wailing ghost before anything else. As a cursed spirit, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
