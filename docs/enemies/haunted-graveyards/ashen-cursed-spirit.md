## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 41% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 83 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the cursed spirit ranks among its defining hazards. This cursed spirit breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
