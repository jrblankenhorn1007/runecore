## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 25% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -29%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The glass elemental is a chrono construct native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
