## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 8% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 10 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in The Haven (Hub Valley) warn newcomers about the owl before anything else. As a wild avian, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Phase Step chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
