## Lore
Local guides in Haunted Graveyards & Mire warn newcomers about the mourning wraith before anything else. As a cursed spirit, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
