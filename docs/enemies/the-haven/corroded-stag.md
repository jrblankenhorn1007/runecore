## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 8% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 53 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
The stag did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near oak timber deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
