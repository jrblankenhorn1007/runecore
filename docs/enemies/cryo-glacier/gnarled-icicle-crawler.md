## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 63% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -32%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 14 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the icicle crawler as deceptively patient for a ice elemental. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Grapple is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
