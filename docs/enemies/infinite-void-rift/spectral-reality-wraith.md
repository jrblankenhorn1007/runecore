2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 140 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 148 damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Infinite Void Rift, the reality wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its ground vibration detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
