# Warden (The Paladin / Hard-Light Templar)

The Warden is the righteous guardian, holy smiter, and hard-light defender of Untitled RPG, utilizing projected geometric forcefields, reflective barriers, and blinding solar lances to banish corruption.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Playstyle

### 1.1 The Archetype
Fusing chivalric paladin codes with ancient hard-light projection matrices, the Warden creates impenetrable barriers on the battlefield. Wielding holy blades and energy emitter shields, they deflect hostile projectiles back into enemy ranks and summon concentrated pillars of solar light to eradicate mechanical fiends and undead monstrosities.

- **Primary Combat Role**: Defensive Bruiser / Barrier Support / Retaliatory Reflector.
- **Weapon Specialization**: Radiant Broadswords, Hard-Light Shields, Solar Halberds, Consecrated Maces.
- **Survival Niche**: Shields friendly NPCs from night raid damage; provides light aura in dark caverns; high resistance to heat and radiation; inspires settlement morale.

### 1.2 Baseline Attributes & Derived Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: 12 ($+18.0\%$ melee damage, $+24\text{ kg}$ carry weight).
- **Vitality (VIT)**: 12 ($+120$ Max HP, $+0.6\text{ HP/s}$ regen).
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: 7 ($+10.5\%$ speed).
- **Intelligence (INT)**: 10 ($+20.0\%$ spell scaling).
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: 8 ($+0.8\text{ MP/s}$ mana regen).
- **Cyber-Affinity (CYB)**: 8 ($+80$ Power Capacity).

**Starting Derived Pools**:
- Health: $220\text{ HP}$
- Stamina: $60\text{ Stamina}$
- Mana: $70\text{ Mana}$
- Power: $70\text{ Power}$
- Humanity: $100$
- Stability: $100$

### 1.3 Starting Equipment Kit
- **Main Hand**: Radiant Broadsword (18 physical/holy damage, 1.8 attacks/sec).

- **Consumables**: 3x Holy Healing Salves, 2x Light Beacons.

## 2. Signature Class Mechanic: Aegis Retaliation

The Warden wields active projectile redirection. Holding Right Mouse Button raises the hard-light shield:
- **Perfect Parry**: Timing a block within 0.20s of an incoming attack completely negates damage, stuns melee attackers for 1.5 seconds, and reflects hostile projectiles back toward the shooter at double velocity.
- **Righteous Charge**: Successfully parrying charges the Warden's weapon, causing the next attack to deal an automatic $+100\%$ bonus holy damage.

## 3. Class Skill Tree & Abilities

### 3.1 Active Skills (Bound to Hotkeys `Q`, `E`, `R`, `F`)

#### Active Q — Radiant Smite
- **Hotbar Slot**: `Q`
- **Resource Cost**: 18 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 4.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Infuses weapon with searing solar energy, striking forward for $220\%$ weapon holy damage and blinding hit targets for 2.5 seconds (enemies miss $50\%$ of their attacks).

#### Active E — Bastion Shield Bash
- **Hotbar Slot**: `E`
- **Resource Cost**: 15 Stamina
- **Cooldown**: 6.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Dashes forward 4 tiles with shield raised, deflecting all incoming missiles and slamming into enemies for $130\%$ damage, knocking them back 5 tiles with a 1.5-second stun.

#### Active R — Consecrated Ground
- **Hotbar Slot**: `R`
- **Resource Cost**: 30 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 15.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Consecrates an 8-tile zone on the floor for 8.0 seconds. Allies inside gain $+20$ Armor and heal for $12\text{ HP/s}$; undead, demonic, and corrupted machines inside take $25\text{ holy damage/s}$.

#### Active F — Dawn Ray Judgment
- **Hotbar Slot**: `F` (Ultimate)
- **Resource Cost**: 50 Mana
- **Cooldown**: 35.0 seconds
- **Effect**: Channels a brilliant vertical pillar of sunlight from above for 3.0 seconds, incinerating all targets within a 3-tile wide column for continuous ticks of $140\%$ holy/energy damage.

### 3.2 Key Passive Specializations
1. *Vanguard Resolve*: Armor increased by $+12\%$ while wielding any shield.
2. *Righteous Retribution*: Parrying an attack releases a burst of light dealing 45 holy damage to all adjacent foes.
3. *Unwavering Faith*: Durations of status effects and debuffs suffered reduced by $30\%$.
4. *Shield Wall*: Projectiles striking the shield have a $40\%$ chance to ricochet directly back at the attacker.
5. *Bulwark Aura*: Standing within 6 tiles of friendly NPCs or pets grants them $+20\%$ damage mitigation.
6. *Hard-Light Blades*: All melee weapon attacks deal an additional $+15\%$ holy/energy damage.
7. *Beacon of Hope*: Healing potions heal an additional $+25\%$ health.
8. *Zealot's Stride*: Heavy armor movement speed penalties reduced by $100\%$.
9. *Holy Vengeance*: When health drops below $25\%$, an explosion of light blinds all nearby enemies for 4.0 seconds (90s internal cooldown).
10. *Sanctified Steel*: Melee hits against corrupted, undead, or robotic foes deal $+35\%$ bonus damage.
11. *Divine Bastion*: Maximum Health increased by $+100$ and Maximum Mana by $+60$.

## 4. Level 20 Free Signature Augmentation

### *Hard-Light Aegis Projector*
- **Body Slot**: Left Arm
- **Augmentation Type**: Magitech Hard-Light Matrix
- **Native Class Cost**: Free (0 surgery credits, 0 Humanity penalty, 0 Power upkeep).
- **Core Functionality**:
  - Replaces the left forearm with an advanced hard-light emitter lens.
  - Can be activated to project an invulnerable 6-second dome shield (cooldown 45s).
  - Blocks $100\%$ of all incoming hostile projectiles and blasts, while allowing allied attacks to pass through freely.
- **Cross-Class Exotic Surgery Trade-Off**:
  - Non-native installation penalty: $-25$ Humanity, 15 Power drain per block, and $-15\%$ dodge roll distance.
