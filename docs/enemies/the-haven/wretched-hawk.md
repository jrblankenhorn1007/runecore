- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 19% and 2 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 14 damage at range.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 9 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 5 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Haven (Hub Valley) describe the hawk as deceptively patient for a feral livestock. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 89% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
