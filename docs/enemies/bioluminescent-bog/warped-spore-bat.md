## Lore
The spore bat is a fungal horror native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
