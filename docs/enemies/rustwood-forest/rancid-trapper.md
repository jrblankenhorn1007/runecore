## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 22% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -21%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 8 damage at range.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 12 damage in a 6-tile radius.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the trapper as deceptively patient for a feral predator. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
