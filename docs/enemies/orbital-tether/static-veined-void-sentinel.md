## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 93 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 137 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
The void sentinel is a precursor guardian native to Precursor Orbital Tether, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Precursor Orbital Tether has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
