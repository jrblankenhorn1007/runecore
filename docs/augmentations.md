# Body Augmentations & The Dual-Track Economy Specification

This document provides the complete technical and design specification for the 11-slot body augmentation system in Untitled RPG. Characters can replace their mortal flesh with advanced cybernetics (bionics) or ancient magitech and chimeric beast grafts.

---

## 1. The Dual-Track Biological & Technological Economy

Every augmentation alters the player's anatomical baseline. Players can specialize as pure bionic cyborgs, pure ancient magitech channelers, or balance both through hybrid builds.

```
[Full Tech / Bionic] <============== [Pure Mortal] ==============> [Full Magitech / Chimeric]
 High Power Capacity                                                   High Mana Regeneration
 Heavy Armor & Forcefields                                             High Spell Resonance
 Low Humanity (Weak Healing)                                           Instability / Cellular Rejection
```

### 1.1 The Bionic Power Grid & Humanity System
- **Power Capacity & Generation**:
  - Base Power Capacity: $50$ (increased by $+10$ per point in Cyber-Affinity and by Core/Heart augmentations).
  - Power is generated by the **Heart / Core** slot (e.g. Micro-Fission Arc Core generates $15\text{ Power/s}$).
  - **Passive Power Reservation**: Certain bionics reserve power permanently (e.g. Subdermal Plating reserves $15$ Power).
  - **Active Power Drain**: Skills, laser cannons, and jet boots consume active power units per second or per activation.
  - **Power Depletion (0 Power)**: When power is depleted, energy shields collapse, active cyber-weapons fail to fire, and movement speed is reduced by $30\%$.
- **Humanity & Sanity (Mortal Baseline: 100)**:
  - Installing bionic parts permanently subtracts Humanity (e.g. Hydraulic Arms $-15$, Targeting Optics $-10$).
  - **Threshold 1 (Humanity < 50)**: "Biological Detachment" — External magical healing and potions restore $50\%$ less health.
  - **Threshold 2 (Humanity < 20)**: "Cyber-Psychosis / Overclock" — Magic spells deal $75\%$ less damage; in exchange, the character gains permanent $+15\%$ weapon attack speed, $+10\%$ movement speed, and total immunity to Fear, Blindness, and Mind-Control debuffs.

---

### 1.2 The Magitech Resonance & Chimeric Stability System
- **Magical Stability (Mortal Baseline: 100)**:
  - Chimeric grafts (living beast parts like the Chimeric Bear Arm or Drake Pyro Glands) and ancient magical crystals require continuous mana resonance.
  - **Passive Mana Upkeep**: Living chimeric tissue draws a continuous mana upkeep to stay metabolically stable (e.g. Bear Arm drains $0.8\text{ Mana/s}$).
  - **Cellular Rejection**: If current Mana drops to $0$ while wearing unstable chimeric grafts:
    - The graft's stat bonuses deactivate.
    - The player suffers "Cellular Rejection" damage ($2\%$ of Max Health as internal bleed every second) until Mana is restored above $10\%$.
- **Power Crystals & Magitech Synergy**:
  - Rare items like *Ancient Astral Power Crystals* or *Aether Micro-Reactors* can be socketed into the Heart/Core slot. They produce both electrical Power and magical Mana, allowing hybrid characters to run cyber-weapons alongside magical limbs without triggering rejection.

## 2. The 11 Body Augmentation Slots

Every character body consists of 11 distinct modification slots:

```
                      [ 1. Head ]
                      [ 2. Eyes ]
             [ 3. Nervous ] ── [ 4. Lungs ]
             [ 5. Heart/Core ]
             [ 6. Torso   ] ── [ 7. Skin/Dermis ]
[ 8. L-Arm ]                                [ 9. R-Arm ]
[ 10. Hands ]                               [ 10. Hands ]
                      [ 11. Legs ]
```

1. **Head**: Neural co-processors, telepathic focus crowns, targeting micro-arrays.
2. **Eyes**: Night-vision optics, thermal scanners, ocular lasers, runic void eyes.
3. **Nervous System**: Superconducting cyber-wires, mana meridians, reflex accelerators.
4. **Lungs**: Gas scrubbers, hazard rebreathers, dragon-drake pyro breath glands.
5. **Heart / Core**: Micro-fission reactors, ancient elemental crystals, behemoth chimera cores.
6. **Torso**: Titanium-carbide ribs, nanite dispensaries, runic spell plates.
7. **Skin / Dermis**: Subdermal alloy mesh, chameleon scales, thermal insulation weave.
8. **Left Arm**: Heavy utility tools, deflector shields, chimeric bear arms, grappling launchers.
9. **Right Arm**: Weapon actuators, laser cannons, arcane focus conduits, hydraulic smashers.
10. **Hands**: Micro-thruster palms (mid-air hovering), monomolecular claws, alchemical touch.
11. **Legs**: Rocket jump boots, mantis leap tendons, hydraulic shock dampers.

## 4. Surgical Installation & The Cyber-Clinic

Augmentations cannot simply be swapped in the field; they require clinical surgery:
- **The Cyber-Clinic**: Run by **Dr. Raymond** in the Hub. Specializes in installing and removing bionics for a fee in scrap/credits.
- **The Bio-Vat & Arcane Loom**: Run by **Weaver Selene** in the Hub. Specializes in binding chimeric beast tissues and infusing ancient magic crystals into the nervous system.
- **Surgery Cost**: Each surgery requires credits, specialized medical items (e.g. *Surgical Nanite Gel*, *Sterile Plasma Scalpels*), and 5 seconds of in-game rest time.
