- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 51% and 41 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 132 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 75 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the tumor wraith before anything else. As a enzyme ooze, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
