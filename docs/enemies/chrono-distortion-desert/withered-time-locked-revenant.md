## Lore
The time-locked revenant did not always haunt Chrono-Distortion Desert; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 73% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
