## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 51% and 9 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Fire Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of fire energy, dealing 32 damage to all targets within 3 tiles.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 21 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps dispute that the kiln horror ranks among its defining hazards. This fire elemental circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its basalt scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Fire Nova chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
