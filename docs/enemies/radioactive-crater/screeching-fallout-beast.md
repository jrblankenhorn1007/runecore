## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 76 damage.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 107 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 115 damage at range.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the fallout beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its heat signature detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
