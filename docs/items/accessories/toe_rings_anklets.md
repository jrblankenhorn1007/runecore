# Toe Rings, Grounding Bands & Speed Anklets Catalog

This document specifies the individual base stat roll ranges, footing friction multipliers, sprint velocity bonuses, and ground hazard resistances for toe rings and lower-extremity jewelry across all 8 progression tiers in Untitled RPG (~50 accessory definitions). Characters can equip 1 Toe Ring or Anklet in their foot accessory slot.

---

## 1. Mechanics & Stat Rolling Parameters
- **Accessory Category**: Foot & Toe Jewelry Slot (Toe Ring / Anklet).
- **Affix Bias**: Emphasizes ground contact mechanics: friction on ice, mud walking compensation, sprinting speed bursts, jump takeoff velocity, and floor trap damage mitigation (spikes, acid pools, molten slag).
