# Biome Dossier: Floating Astral Sky Islands

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Floating Astral Sky Islands (Biome 12)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-12`, Tier 5 Celestial / Aerial.
- **Location & Altitude**: High in the stratosphere above The Haven and Mountain Peaks ($Y = +600$ to $Y = +1200$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Lethal Bottomless Fall**: Stepping off island edges results in a 1,000-block fall back to the surface. Requires Glider Wings, Jet Boots, Harpoons, or Feather-Fall Draughts.
  - **Low Gravity & Atmospheric Thinning**: Gravity is reduced by 40% (higher jumps, longer hang-time); hypoxia causes stamina regeneration to slow by 25% without an Oxygen Recycler.
  - **High-Velocity Jet Streams**: Violent wind currents that blow players horizontally off narrow floating bridges.
- **Ambient Temperature**: -15°C to 0°C (Sub-zero thin air).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: White Marble Blocks, Astral Cloudstone, Sunstone Pillars, Solidified Hard-Light Platforms, Golden Soil.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Cloudstone Flakes, White Marble Slabs, Quartz Crystals.
  - **Moderate**: Aether-Infused Starmetal, Silver Ingot Veins, Sun-Diamond.
  - **Rare**: Elysian Sun-Drops, Astral Quicksilver, Celestial Gold.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Star-Lily Blossoms**: Glow with pure white starlight; used in supreme mana draughts.
  - **Sky-Vine Trellises**: Golden vines that hang between islands; can be climbed to traverse gaps.
  - **Solar Orchids**: Absorb raw cosmic rays; used in permanent attribute mutagens.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Sunken Palace of the Archons
- **Dungeon Structure**: Magnificent white marble cathedral floating amidst golden cumulus clouds. Features vertical wind-launch jump pads, shifting hard-light bridges, celestial choir halls, and solar battery spires.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Aligning solar mirror prisms to redirect light beams into crystal receiver doors, activating ancient anti-gravity bridges.
- **Dungeon Boss: Seraphiel the Dawn Sovereign (Level 48 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Open-air circular marble amphitheater overlooking the edge of the world.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Flits between pillars with 6 golden wings, firing volleys of light arrows, summoning cyclone gusts to blow player off the arena, and calling down solar pillars.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Descends with a colossal blazing sun-blade; dashes across arena leaving trails of holy fire, and triggers "Solar Eclipse" where player must take cover beneath hard-light barriers to survive a screen-wide nova.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Seraphiel's Dawn-Bow (T5 Legendary Bow), Wings of the Archon (Glider/Wing Augment), Elysian Sun-Drop, 2x Precursor Datacubes.
