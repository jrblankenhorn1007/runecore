## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 55% and 45 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the basilisk kin ranks among its defining hazards. This stone elemental charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Regenerative Pulse is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
