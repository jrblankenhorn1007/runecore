## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 57% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 80 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Magnetic Thunder-Crags describe the charged wisp as deceptively patient for a static horror. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
