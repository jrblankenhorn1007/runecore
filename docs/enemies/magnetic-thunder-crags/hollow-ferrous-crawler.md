## Lore
Few who study Magnetic Thunder-Crags dispute that the ferrous crawler ranks among its defining hazards. This lightning wraith charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its neodymium scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near neodymium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
