## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 79 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 70 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The tombstone horror did not always haunt Haunted Graveyards & Mire; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Grapple chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
