## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 53% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Local guides in Petrified Ancient Forest warn newcomers about the grove sentinel before anything else. As a basilisk kin, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Defensive Shell is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
