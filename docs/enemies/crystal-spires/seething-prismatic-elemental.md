## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 50 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 33 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 58 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Local guides in Crystal Spires & Geodes warn newcomers about the prismatic elemental before anything else. As a shard beast, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Whirl is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
