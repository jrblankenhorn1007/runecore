## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Infinite Void Rift describe the null serpent as deceptively patient for a entropy spawn. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
