## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 32% and 36 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -18%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 69 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
The feral cyborg is a security construct native to Ancient Ruined Megacity, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Ancient Ruined Megacity has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
