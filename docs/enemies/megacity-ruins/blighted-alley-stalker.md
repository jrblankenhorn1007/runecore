## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 26% and 27 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 56 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 8 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 56 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
The alley stalker did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Phase Step is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
