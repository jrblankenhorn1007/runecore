## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 68% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 38 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 63 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The corroded construct did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 24% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
