## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 25% and 3 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities

2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the steam servitor as deceptively patient for a beast. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
