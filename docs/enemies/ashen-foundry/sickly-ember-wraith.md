## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 48 damage at range.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 34 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Fire Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of fire energy, dealing 26 fire damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The ember wraith did not always haunt Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
