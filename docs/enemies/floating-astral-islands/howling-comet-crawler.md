## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 40% and 86 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 215 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 164 damage in a 3-tile radius.
4. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 4 tiles to close the gap, dealing 314 damage and applying a short knockback.

## Lore
The comet crawler did not always haunt Floating Astral Sky Islands; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near cloudstone deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
