## Abilities

2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 63 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 63 damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Bioluminescent Fungal Bog describe the bog toad as deceptively patient for a bog mutant. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
