## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the drowned revenant ranks among its defining hazards. This deep leviathan patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.
