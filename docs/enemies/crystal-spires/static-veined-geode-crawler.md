## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 37% and 68 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 231 damage at range.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 145 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
4. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 120 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The geode crawler is a light wisp native to Crystal Spires & Geodes, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Crystal Spires has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
