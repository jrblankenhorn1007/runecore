## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 132 damage.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 86 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches dispute that the fire elemental ranks among its defining hazards. This molten construct hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its obsidian scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 38% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
