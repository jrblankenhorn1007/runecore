## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 24% and 16 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 39 damage.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 45 damage at range.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
The mole horror did not always haunt Subterranean Caverns; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rending Tear chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
