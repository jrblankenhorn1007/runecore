## Abilities
1. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 5 projectiles, each dealing 57 damage at range.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 74 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The arc elemental did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Volley chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
