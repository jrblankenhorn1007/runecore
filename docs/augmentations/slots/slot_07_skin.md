# Slot 7: Skin & Dermis Augmentations Catalog (22 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 22 skin and dermis augmentations (Subdermal alloy meshes, chameleonic scales, thermoregulation skins, and conductive coatings) for Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Mechanics of the Skin / Dermis Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Skin Augmentation installed across the full body epidermis.
- **Role**: Governs environmental surface interactions, camouflage, trap spike mitigation, and contact elemental retaliation.
