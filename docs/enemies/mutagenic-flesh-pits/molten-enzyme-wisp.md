2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Local guides in Mutagenic Flesh-Pits warn newcomers about the enzyme wisp before anything else. As a chimeric beast, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 28% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
