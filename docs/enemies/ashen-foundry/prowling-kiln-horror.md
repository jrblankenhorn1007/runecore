## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 35 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the kiln horror as deceptively patient for a slag beast. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
