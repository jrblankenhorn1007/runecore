## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 18% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -29%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 5 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 50 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the sunken sentinel has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Phase Step chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
