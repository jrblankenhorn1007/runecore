## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 51% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -19%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 71 damage and applying a stagger.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 86 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
The mire leech is a spore beast native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 17% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
