## Lore
The gamma horror is a gamma horror native to Radioactive Crater Basin, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Radioactive Crater Basin has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
