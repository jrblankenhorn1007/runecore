## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 33 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -58%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 70 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 54 damage.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 107 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches describe the obsidian guardian as deceptively patient for a lava wyrm. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 19% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
