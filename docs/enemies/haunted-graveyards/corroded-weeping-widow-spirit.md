## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 33% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 6 tiles to close the gap, dealing 64 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 96 damage at range.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 57 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the weeping widow spirit ranks among its defining hazards. This mire beast patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
