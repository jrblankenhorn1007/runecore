## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 58% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -42%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 51 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 34 damage.
3. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 45 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the icicle crawler as deceptively patient for a glacier construct. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 65% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
