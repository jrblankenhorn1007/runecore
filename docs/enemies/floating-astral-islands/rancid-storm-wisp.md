## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Floating Astral Sky Islands, the storm wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
