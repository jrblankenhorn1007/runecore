## Abilities

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Floating Astral Sky Islands describe the sky whale calf as deceptively patient for a storm elemental. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
