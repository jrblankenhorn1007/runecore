- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 78% and 102 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 362 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
4. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 203 damage at range.

## Lore
Local guides in The Infinite Void Rift warn newcomers about the dark matter wraith before anything else. As a reality wraith, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.
