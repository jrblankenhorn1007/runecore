## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the aurora wisp has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
