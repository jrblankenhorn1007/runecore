## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 49% and 31 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 52 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 38 damage in a 4-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The cathedral sentinel is a cursed spirit native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 43% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
