## Abilities

2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 2 projectiles, each dealing 105 damage at range.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The overclocked bot did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its toxin weakness rewards elemental prep.
