## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 44% and 13 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 45 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Haunted Graveyards & Mire describe the wailing ghost as deceptively patient for a mire beast. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
