## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the gamma horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
