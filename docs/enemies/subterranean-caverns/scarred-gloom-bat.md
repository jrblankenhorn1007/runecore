## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 26% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 8 tiles to close the gap, dealing 65 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 43 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 28 damage.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Subterranean Caverns describe the gloom bat as deceptively patient for a rock elemental. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
