## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 48% and 22 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 36 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
3. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 46 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Haunted Graveyards & Mire warn newcomers about the peat ghoul before anything else. As a mire beast, it charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Piercing Shriek is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
