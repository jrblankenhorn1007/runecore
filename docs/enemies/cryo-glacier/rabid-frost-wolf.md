## Lore
Few who study Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks dispute that the frost wolf ranks among its defining hazards. This frozen revenant favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its glacial ice scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
