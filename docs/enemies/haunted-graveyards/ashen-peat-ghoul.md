## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 35% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 52 damage.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 52 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The peat ghoul did not always haunt Haunted Graveyards & Mire; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rending Tear is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
