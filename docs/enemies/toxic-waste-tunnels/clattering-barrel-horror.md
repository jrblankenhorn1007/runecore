## Abilities
1. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 6 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
2. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 56 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 53 damage in a 4-tile radius.

## Lore
Local guides in Toxic Waste Tunnels warn newcomers about the barrel horror before anything else. As a waste crawler, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
