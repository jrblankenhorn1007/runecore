# Biome Dossier: Subterranean Caverns & The Underworld

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for **Subterranean Caverns & The Underworld (Biome 07)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-07`, Tier 2 Subterranean.
- **Location & Depths**: Directly beneath The Haven and Rustwood Forest ($Y = -50$ to $Y = -250$).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Pitch Darkness**: Ambient light level is 0%; player and surroundings are completely invisible without torches, lanterns, miner helmets, or glowing bionics.
  - **Unstable Cave-Ins**: Mining key support blocks or detonating explosives can trigger falling rock stalactite hazards dealing 60 physical crush damage.
  - **Fissure Chasms**: Deep vertical drops into spikes or subterranean rivers; falling without a grappling hook or double jump causes lethal fall damage.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 12°C to 16°C (Temperate, no thermal suit required).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Granite Stone, Basalt Blocks, Limestone, Calcite Crystals, Dripstone Stalactites.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Copper Ore, Iron Ore, Coal Veins.
  - **Moderate**: Silver Ore, Gold Ore, Quartz Geodes.
  - **Rare**: Amethyst Geodes, Topaz Crystals, Fossilized Amber.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Luminescent Cave Shrooms**: Emits cyan light; used in night-vision potions.
  - **Cave-Bat Guano Mounds**: Sourced for high-nitrogen fertilizer.
  - **Pale Spore Moss**: Grows on wet cave walls; used in wound salves.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: The Sunken Catacombs & Abandoned Mine-Shafts
- **Dungeon Structure**: Multi-level wooden mine support networks, abandoned railway tracks with pushable ore carts, crumbling crypt crypts, and vertical elevator shafts.
- **Puzzles & Traps**: Pressure-plate boulder traps, dart traps hidden in mossy walls, collapsing timber walkways over spike pits.
- **Dungeon Boss: The Broodmother Arachnida (Level 18 Mini-Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Enormous web-covered limestone cavern with egg sacs lining the ceiling.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Fires web nets, spawns spiderlings, scuttles across ceiling to drop acid bile.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Drops down in an enraged frenzy, piercing with massive bladed front legs and spraying neurotoxin.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Broodmother Silk Robes, Barbed Fang Dagger, Chitinous Leg Greaves, 1x Precursor Datacube.
