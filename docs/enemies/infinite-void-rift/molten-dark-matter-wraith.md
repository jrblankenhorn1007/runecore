## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 82% and 32 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Infinite Void Rift, the dark matter wraith has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Defensive Shell chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
