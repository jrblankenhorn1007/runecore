## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 27% and 24 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -14%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 73 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 64 physical damage to the first target struck.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 67 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Subterranean Caverns, the stone scuttler has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Static Discharge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
