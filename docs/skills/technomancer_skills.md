# Technomancer Class Skill Tree: Lightning, Forcefields, Drones & Nanite Swarms

This document specifies all 52 class skills for the **Technomancer (Mage Archetype)** in Untitled RPG. The Technomancer merges esoteric elemental spellcraft with cybernetic circuit logic, plasma projectors, forcefield physics, and autonomous robotic swarms.

---

## 1. Class Identity & Resource Dynamics
- **Core Resource**: Overcharge & Flux Capacitor ($OVC$, 0–100 scale). Generated by channeling arc lightning, discharging laser grids, and collecting dropped scrap flux coils.
- **Resource Coupling**: Technomancers convert Mana into Bionic Power and vice-versa through real-time capacitor shunts.
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Arc Wands, Plasma Scepters, Energy Rifles, Tesla Staffs, Tech Gauntlets.
