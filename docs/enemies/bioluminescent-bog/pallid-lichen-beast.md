## Lore
The lichen beast is a fungal horror native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
