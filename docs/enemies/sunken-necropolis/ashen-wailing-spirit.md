## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 25% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -8%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 28 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 29 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 60 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
The wailing spirit did not always haunt Sunken Necropolis; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 29% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
