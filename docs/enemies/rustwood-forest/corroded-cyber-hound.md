## Lore
The cyber-hound is a feral predator native to Rustwood Forest, lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
