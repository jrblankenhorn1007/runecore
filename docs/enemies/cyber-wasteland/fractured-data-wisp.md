## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 56% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 94 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers describe the data wisp as deceptively patient for a arc elemental. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Piercing Shriek is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
