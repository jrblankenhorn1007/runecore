## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Oceanic Trenches, the barnacle horror has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its spore-trace scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
