# Slot 11: Leg Augmentations Catalog (24 Augmentations)

This document specifies all 24 leg augmentations (Rocket jump boots, mantis spring tendons, hydraulic shock greaves, mag-boot clamps, and hover skids) for Untitled RPG.

## 1. Mechanics of the Leg Slot
- **Slot Capacity**: Exactly 1 Leg Augmentation installed across both lower extremities.
- **Role**: Governs jumping mechanics, continuous flight, wall climbing, dash travel distance, fall damage mitigation, and ground impact shockwaves.
