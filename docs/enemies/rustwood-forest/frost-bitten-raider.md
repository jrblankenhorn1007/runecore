## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 9% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -32%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 27 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 32 damage in a 3-tile radius.
3. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 21 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Rustwood Forest describe the raider as deceptively patient for a bandit raider. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Whirl chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
