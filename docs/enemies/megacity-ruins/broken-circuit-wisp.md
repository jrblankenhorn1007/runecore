## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 39% and 13 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -22%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 15 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 38 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the circuit wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This radiation ghoul charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Caustic Spray is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
