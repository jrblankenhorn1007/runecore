## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 35% and 25 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 75 void damage to the first target struck.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 56 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the peat ghoul ranks among its defining hazards. This ghost/wraith hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Void Lash is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
