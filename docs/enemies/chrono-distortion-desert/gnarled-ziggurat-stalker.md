## Lore
Fieldnotes from Chrono-Distortion Desert describe the ziggurat stalker as deceptively patient for a glass elemental. It swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
