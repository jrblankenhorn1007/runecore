## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 56% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (moderate, -8%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 71 damage.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 85 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 48 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
The sludge hound did not always haunt Toxic Waste Tunnels; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Corrosive Spit is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
