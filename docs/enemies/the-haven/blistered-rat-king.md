- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 6% and 2 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 12 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 7 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the rat-king has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near oak timber deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
