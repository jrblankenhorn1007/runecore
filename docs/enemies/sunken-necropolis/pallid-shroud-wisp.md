## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 16 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 18 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.

## Lore
The shroud wisp is a bone construct native to Sunken Necropolis, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
