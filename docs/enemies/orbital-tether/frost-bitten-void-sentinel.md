## Lore
Few who study Precursor Orbital Tether dispute that the void sentinel ranks among its defining hazards. This solar wraith favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its hyper-alloy scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Void Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
