## Lore
The basilisk kin is a basilisk kin native to Petrified Ancient Forest, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Petrified Ancient Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
