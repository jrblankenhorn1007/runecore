## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 79% and 53 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Toxin damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 108 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.

## Lore
The oblivion wretch did not always haunt The Infinite Void Rift; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Rally Cry chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
