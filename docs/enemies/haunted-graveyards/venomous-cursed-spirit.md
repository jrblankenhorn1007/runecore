## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 36% and 23 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -2%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 58 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the cursed spirit ranks among its defining hazards. This ghost/wraith hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
