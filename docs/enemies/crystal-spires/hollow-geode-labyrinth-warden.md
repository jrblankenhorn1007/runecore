## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 29% and 95 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 171 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 262 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 296 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
4. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
The diamond horror is a crystal golem native to Crystal Spires & Geodes, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Crystal Spires has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Grapple chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
