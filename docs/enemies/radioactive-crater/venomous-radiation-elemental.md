## Abilities

2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 165 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 155 damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Radioactive Crater Basin, the radiation elemental has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
