## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 74% and 50 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -13%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Magnetic Thunder-Crags, the thunder beast has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near neodymium deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
