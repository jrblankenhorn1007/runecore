## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 19% and 29 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -38%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 102 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
3. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 75 damage to all targets within 5 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Sunken Oceanic Trenches dispute that the coral golem ranks among its defining hazards. This coral construct circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and its coral scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
