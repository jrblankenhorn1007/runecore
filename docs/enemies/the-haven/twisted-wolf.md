## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 20% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -25%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 33 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 17 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of The Haven (Hub Valley), the wolf has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its void weakness rewards elemental prep.
