## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 9% and 18 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -17%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 51 damage in a 4-tile radius.
4. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 81 damage in a 6-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
The steam servitor is a rogue drone native to Rustwood Forest, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Rustwood Forest has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
