## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 30% and 50 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 188 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Void Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of void energy, dealing 118 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Precursor Orbital Tether describe the orbital drone as deceptively patient for a zero-g construct. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
