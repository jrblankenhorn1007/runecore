## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 113 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Mutagenic Flesh-Pits describe the flesh horror as deceptively patient for a enzyme ooze. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Commonly found near eldritch flesh deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
