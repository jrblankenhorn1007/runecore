# Scrolls, Teleport Runes & Emergency Transponders Catalog

This document specifies one-time-use scrolls, teleportation runes, emergency recall transponders, and summoning seals in Untitled RPG (~50 utility consumable definitions).

---

## 1. Mechanics & Activation Parameters
- **Activation Time**: Channeling cast of 1.0 to 2.5 seconds. Taking damage during channel interrupts activation.
- **Escape Mechanics**: Provides emergency retreat from deep procedural dungeon vaults, instant town recall, and combat reinforcements.
- **Stack Size**: Maximum 10 items per inventory slot.
