## Lore
The bog toad is a toxic amphibian native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
