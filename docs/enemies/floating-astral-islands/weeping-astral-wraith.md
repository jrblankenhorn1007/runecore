## Abilities
1. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 60 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
Local guides in Floating Astral Sky Islands warn newcomers about the astral wraith before anything else. As a astral wraith, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 21% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
