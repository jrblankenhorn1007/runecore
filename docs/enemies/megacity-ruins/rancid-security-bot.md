## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 40% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -26%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 22 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
Few who study Ancient Ruined Megacity dispute that the security bot ranks among its defining hazards. This feral mutant charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and its circuit scrap scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
