## Abilities
1. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 7 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.

## Lore
The hard-light golem is a void sentinel native to Precursor Orbital Tether, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Precursor Orbital Tether has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Summon Broodlings chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
