## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 110 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 106 damage at range.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 111 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the rot zombie ranks among its defining hazards. This soul parasite favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Volley is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
