# Biome Dossier: Ancient Ruined Megacity & Sub-Metro

This document specifies the environmental conditions, hazards, geology, fauna, dungeon encounters, and loot tables for the **Ancient Ruined Megacity & Sub-Metro (Biome 06)** in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Profile & Environmental Hazards
- **Biome ID & Tier**: `BIO-06`, Tier 3 Urban / Industrial Ruins.
- **Location & Boundaries**: Massive decaying skyscraper canyons and collapsed elevated highways situated between Rustwood Forest and the Cyber-Wasteland ($X = 1500$ to $X = 3000$, Surface & Sub-Metro).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - **Structural Collapse & Falling Rebar**: Damaging cracked concrete or detonating explosives near weathered building facades triggers falling concrete slabs dealing 70 physical crush damage.
  - **Exposed High-Voltage Conduits**: Severed transformer lines hanging from collapsed ceilings arc intermittently, dealing 50 shock damage and disabling energy shields.
  - **Automated Air-Defense Zones**: Scanning spotlights across rooftops; stepping into searchlights calls automated gunship drone strikes within 3 seconds.
- **Ambient Temperature**: 15°C to 20°C (Temperate, ozone-tinged urban wind).

---

## 2. Geological Composition & Harvestable Resources
- **Terrain Blocks**: Reinforced Concrete Blocks, Rusted Rebar Pillars, Asphalt Highway Slabs, Shattered Plate Glass, Corrugated Sheet Metal.
- **Ores & Minerals**:
  - **Abundant**: Scrap Metal, Rusted Iron Rebar, Copper Wire Bundles.
  - **Moderate**: Lead Slabs, Lithium Batteries, Silicon Wafers.
  - **Rare**: Precursor Microchips, Gold-Plated Terminals, High-Density Capacitors.
- **Flora & Fungi**:
  - **Asphalt Dandelions**: Hardy weeds growing through cracked pavements; used in minor stamina tonics.
  - **Subway Wall Mold**: Fluorescent orange mold flourishing on damp metro tunnels; yields bioluminescent reagents.
  - **Creeping Ivy**: Overgrows ruined high-rises; climbable like ropes to access vertical skyscraper tiers.

## 4. Procedural Dungeon: Central Transit Hub & Corporate Spire
- **Dungeon Structure**: Multi-story subterranean subway junction connecting to the lobby of a soaring Precursor corporate headquarters. Features stalled subway cars, moving turnstile barricades, elevator maintenance shafts, and high-rise office cubicle labyrinths.
- **Puzzles & Mechanisms**: Restoring power to metro electrical substations by inserting charged lithium batteries, opening security gates and clearing train tunnels.
- **Dungeon Boss: The City Overseer AI "Metron-9" (Level 30 Dungeon Boss)**
  - **Arena**: Grand spherical central transit concourse with suspended bullet train cars forming vertical platforms.
  - **Phase 1 (100%–50% HP)**: Controls a massive ceiling-mounted spider turret chassis; fires sweeping laser grids across tracks, summons waves of riot drones, and drops shipping containers on players.
  - **Phase 2 (50%–0% HP)**: Detaches from the ceiling and pilots a high-speed express train engine around the perimeter ring; fires high-caliber autocannons through windows while charging across the central platform.
  - **Guaranteed Drops**: Metron-9 Rail Carbine (T3 Ballistic/Energy Weapon), Subdermal GPS Telemetry Matrix (Head Augment), High-Density Capacitor Core, 2x Precursor Datacubes.
