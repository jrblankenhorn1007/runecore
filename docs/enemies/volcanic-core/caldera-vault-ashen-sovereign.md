## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 63% and 95 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -59%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 343 damage in a 5-tile radius.
3. **Whirl** — Spins rapidly, dealing 193 damage to all targets in melee range over 1.5 seconds.
4. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Volcanic Core & Magma Trenches, the inferno hound has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
