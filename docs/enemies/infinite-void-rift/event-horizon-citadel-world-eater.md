## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 85% and 138 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
3. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 260 damage over time and pinning the target.
4. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 425 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from The Infinite Void Rift describe the void horror as deceptively patient for a reality wraith. It favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 52% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
