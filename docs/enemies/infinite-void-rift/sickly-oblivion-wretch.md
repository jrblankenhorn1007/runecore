## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 150 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 103 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 82 damage in a 5-tile radius.

## Lore
Few who study The Infinite Void Rift dispute that the oblivion wretch ranks among its defining hazards. This singularity construct lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its dark matter scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 38% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
