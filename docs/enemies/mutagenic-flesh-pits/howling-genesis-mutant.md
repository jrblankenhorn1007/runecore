## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 59% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -16%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 124 damage in a 5-tile radius.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 114 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
3. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 274 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
4. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The genesis mutant did not always haunt Mutagenic Flesh-Pits; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Static Discharge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
