## Abilities
1. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 46 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
2. **Toxin Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of toxin energy, dealing 69 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
The bile crawler is a enzyme ooze native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Toxin Nova is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
