## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 47% and 37 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Physical Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of physical energy, dealing 61 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Few who study Petrified Ancient Forest dispute that the agate serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This petrified guardian lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its petrified wood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its 89% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
