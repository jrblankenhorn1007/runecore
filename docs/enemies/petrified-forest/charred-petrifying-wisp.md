## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 46% and 68 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.
2. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 210 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
3. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
4. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 179 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Petrified Ancient Forest describe the petrifying wisp as deceptively patient for a gorgon spawn. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its shock weakness rewards elemental prep.
