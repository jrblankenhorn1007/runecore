## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 30% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -33%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 76 frost damage to the first target struck.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
3. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.

## Lore
The kraken spawn did not always haunt Sunken Oceanic Trenches; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Frost Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
