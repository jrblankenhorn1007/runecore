## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 11% and 6 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -10%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 10 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 17 physical damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The ram did not always haunt The Haven (Hub Valley); older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Grapple chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
