## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 54% and 18 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 38 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 24 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps describe the slag beast as deceptively patient for a slag beast. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its sound detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
