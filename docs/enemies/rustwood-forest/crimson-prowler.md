## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 9% and 4 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -21%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 12 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Few who study Rustwood Forest dispute that the prowler ranks among its defining hazards. This feral predator breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its ironwood scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Rally Cry is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
