## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 64% and 7 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (very high, -42%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 21 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 21 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.

## Lore
The glacier golem did not always haunt Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
