## Lore
The sand horror is a sand horror native to Chrono-Distortion Desert, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Chrono-Distortion Desert has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its frost weakness rewards elemental prep.
