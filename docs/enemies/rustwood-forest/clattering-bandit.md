## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 14% and 3 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (very high, -35%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 15 damage over time and pinning the target.
2. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 13 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Rustwood Forest, the bandit has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Its 37% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
