## Lore
Few who study Bioluminescent Fungal Bog dispute that the spore bat ranks among its defining hazards. This fungal horror lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its glowing shrooms scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
