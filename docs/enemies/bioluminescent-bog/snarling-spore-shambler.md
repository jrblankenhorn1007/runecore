## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 51% and 28 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -15%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Summon Broodlings** — Calls forth 2-3 lesser broodlings to reinforce the encounter.
3. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Bioluminescent Fungal Bog describe the spore shambler as deceptively patient for a spore beast. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
