## Lore
The hoarfrost bear is a ice elemental native to Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Cryo-Glacier has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
