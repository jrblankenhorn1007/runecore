## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 24% and 17 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -26%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 42 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 36 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Sunken Oceanic Trenches describe the barnacle horror as deceptively patient for a coral construct. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
