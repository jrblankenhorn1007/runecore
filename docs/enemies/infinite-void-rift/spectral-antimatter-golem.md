## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Void resistance of 73% and 54 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 4 tiles.
2. **Phase Step** — Briefly vanishes and reappears within 4 tiles of the target, avoiding all damage for 1 second.
3. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 78 void damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The antimatter golem did not always haunt The Infinite Void Rift; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Piercing Shriek chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
