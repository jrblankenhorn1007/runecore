## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 35% and 18 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Regenerative Pulse** — Channels for 2 seconds to restore 10% of maximum HP; interrupted by a stagger.
2. **Corrosive Spit** — Launches a glob of corrosive matter dealing 31 damage and reducing target armor by 10% for 5 seconds.
3. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 58 damage.

## Lore
Local guides in Subterranean Caverns warn newcomers about the gloom bat before anything else. As a subterranean insect, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
