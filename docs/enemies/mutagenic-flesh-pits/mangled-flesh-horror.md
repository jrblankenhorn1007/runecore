## Lore
The flesh horror is a chimeric beast native to Mutagenic Flesh-Pits, charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Mutagenic Flesh-Pits has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.
