## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 64% and 14 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -34%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Frost Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of frost energy, dealing 35 frost damage to the first target struck.
2. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
3. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 38 damage to all targets within 4 tiles.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks, the snow stalker has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It charges the instant it detects prey, favoring raw aggression over tactics, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Frost Lash chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
