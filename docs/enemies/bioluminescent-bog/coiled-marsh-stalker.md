## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Toxin resistance of 47% and 11 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 27 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.
2. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 23 damage and applying a stagger.

## Lore
The marsh stalker is a toxic amphibian native to Bioluminescent Fungal Bog, breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Bioluminescent Fungal Bog has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Burrow Strike is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
