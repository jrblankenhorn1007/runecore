## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the glacier golem as deceptively patient for a frost beast. It guards a fixed chokepoint or nest and will not pursue far beyond it, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.
