## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 29% and 21 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -16%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 58 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 40 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Volley** — Fires a spread of 4 projectiles, each dealing 30 damage at range.

## Lore
The security bot did not always haunt Ancient Ruined Megacity; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its 88% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
