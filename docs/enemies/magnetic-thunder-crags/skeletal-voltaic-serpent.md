## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 65% and 49 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -19%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Pounce** — Leaps up to 5 tiles to close the gap, dealing 78 damage and applying a short knockback.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Weakpoint Overload** — Exposes a glowing weakpoint for 3 seconds that takes +50% critical damage.

## Lore
The voltaic serpent is a storm elemental native to Magnetic Thunder-Crags, circles its target, probing for an opening before committing to an attack. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Magnetic Thunder-Crags has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Pounce chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
