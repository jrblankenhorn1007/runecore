# Cryo-Glacier & Neon Peaks (Overworld Zone 3)

The Cryo-Glacier and Neon Peaks constitute a perilous sub-zero alpine mountain range on the eastern horizon. Dominated by sheer glacial ice cliffs, howling blizzards, and the frozen wreckage of derelict orbital satellites that crashed decades ago, it is one of the most hostile surface biomes in Untitled RPG.

---

## 1. Biome Environment & Atmosphere

- **Biome Type**: Alpine Glacial Mountain Range & Sub-Zero Tundra.
- **Ambient Temperature**: $-15^\circ\text{C}$ to $-35^\circ\text{C}$ daytime, dropping below $-50^\circ\text{C}$ during blizzards (Extreme Freezing territory).
- **Environmental Hazards**:
  - *Hypothermia & Freezing*: Without insulated fur armor, heated bionic dermis, or adjacent campfires, the player's core temperature drops below $-10^\circ\text{C}$, slowing movement speed by $30\%$, increasing weapon spread by $+50\%$, and dealing $3\text{ cold dmg/s}$.
  - *Slick Glacial Ice*: Ground blocks possess near-zero friction; sprinting causes long sliding inertia unless wearing spiked snow shoes or heavy sabatons.
  - *Leaking Liquid Nitrogen Lines*: Ruptured satellite conduits spray freezing gas that instantly flash-freezes the player for 2.5 seconds.
- **Weather States**:
  - *High-Altitude Blizzard*: Visibility drops to 6 tiles; wind forces push character backward when walking against the gale; ambient temperature drops by $-20^\circ\text{C}$.
  - *Aurora Borealis (Clear Night)*: Luminous blue and green night skies that shed light, eliminating darkness penalties and boosting mana regeneration by $+20\%$.

---

## 2. Natural Resources & Mineral Deposits

- **Cryogenic Minerals & Ores**:
  - *Raw Cobalt Ore*: Dense blue metallic veins embedded within sheer ice walls.
  - *Heavy Tungsten Ore*: Extremely heavy, dense ore found in deep mountain fissures.
  - *Glacial Ice Chunks*: Harvested for liquid nitrogen coolant and alchemical cryo-potions.
  - *Pure Quartz Geodes*: Crystalline formations used for optical lenses and computer microprocessors.
  - *Satellite Cyber-Salvage*: Downed satellite pods contain high-yield gold traces, advanced microchips, and superconducting wire.
- **Alpine Flora**:
  - *Mountain Peppermint*: Pungent herb that thrives in snow; brewed into hot teas that cure freezing.
  - *Frost-Moss*: Bioluminescent blue lichen that glows softly in snowdrifts; used in chilling weapon coatings.
  - *Glacial Edelweiss*: Rare flower with pure cell preservation properties; ingredient in immortality mutagens.

## 3. Enemy Populations & Spawn Tables

### 3.1 Daytime Enemies (Levels 35–45)
- **Cryo-Stalker Yeti** (Beast): Massive fur-covered biped that leaps great distances; ground slams crack ice floors and stun player; drops thick yeti pelts and bear-grade meat.
- **Frostbite Wolf Pack** (Beast): White-furred cyborg wolves equipped with sub-zero chilling fangs; bites slow player movement by $30\%$.
- **Automated Cryo-Sentry** (Machine): Downed satellite defense turret armed with a continuous freezing beam; drops cobalt plates and coolant tubes.
- **Ice Shard Golem** (Elemental): Humanoid assembled from sheer glacial ice; taking heavy physical damage shatters shards outward in a defensive burst.

### 3.2 Nighttime Enemies (Levels 40–50)
- **Cryo-Sniper Commando** (Cyborg): Camouflaged sniper positioned on high peaks firing supersonic frostbite slugs from concealed blinds; drops heavy ammo and laser scopes.
- **Frozen Husk** (Undead Cyborg): Stasis patient reanimated by malfunctioning neural implants; relentless pursuit; immune to frost damage.
- **Glacial Phantom** (Specter): Incorporeal spirit that phases through ice walls, draining player body heat directly on touch.

## 4. Procedural Dungeons: The Cryo-Stasis Depths (Campaign Tier 5)

Deep inside the glacial core of the mountain lies the entry airlock to the **Cryo-Stasis Depths**:
- **Dungeon Architectural Style**: Sleek white-and-blue neon scientific corridors entirely encased in transparent sheets of ice, flickering fluorescent panels, and rows of frosted cryogenic sleep pods.
- **Dungeon Hazards**: Pressure-sealed blast doors requiring power overrides, frozen catwalks over bottomless abysses, and vents blasting liquid nitrogen at regular intervals.
- **Campaign Tier 5 Boss Encounter: ABSOLUTE ZERO COMMANDER**:
  - *Phase 1*: High-ranking military commander in a powered anti-matter cryogenic exo-suit with dual arm-mounted frost lasers; fires sweeping beams that freeze ground tiles into slick zero-friction ice; deploys deployable ice wall barriers.
  - *Phase 2 (50% HP)*: Overclocks internal cryo-core, triggering a localized indoor blizzard reducing room visibility; blinks rapidly around the arena leaving delayed-blast frost time-bombs; fires piercing freeze railgun slugs.
  - *Boss Unique Loot*: *Absolute Zero Commander's Frostbrand*, *Absolute Zero Commander's Exo-Suit*, and *Liquid Nitrogen Saber*.
