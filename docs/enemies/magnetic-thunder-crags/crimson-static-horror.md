## Lore
The static horror did not always haunt Magnetic Thunder-Crags; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its sight detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
