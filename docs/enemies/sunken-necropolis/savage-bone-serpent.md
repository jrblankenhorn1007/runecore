## Abilities
1. **Defensive Shell** — Hunkers down, gaining +40% damage reduction for 4 seconds at the cost of movement speed.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 69 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 65 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.

## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Sunken Necropolis, the bone serpent has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
