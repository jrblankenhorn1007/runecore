## Lore
The mire leech did not always haunt Bioluminescent Fungal Bog; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

Commonly found near glowing shrooms deposits; expect additional patrols nearby when engaging in daylight or high-alert states.
