- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 60% and 41 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.
2. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
3. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Magnetic Thunder-Crags describe the voltaic serpent as deceptively patient for a storm elemental. It lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Its 22% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
