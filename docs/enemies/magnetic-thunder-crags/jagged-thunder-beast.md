## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 58% and 44 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (high, -17%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 104 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 147 damage in a 3-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.
3. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Magnetic Thunder-Crags describe the thunder beast as deceptively patient for a lightning wraith. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Ground Slam is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
