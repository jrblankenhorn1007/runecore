## Lore
Born from the harsh conditions of Ancient Ruined Megacity, the feral cyborg has adapted to thrive where softer creatures perish. It ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and local trackers mark its territory with warning cairns built from the bones of those who ignored the signs.
