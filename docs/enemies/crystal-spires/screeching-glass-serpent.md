## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 29% and 38 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (moderate, -14%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Physical Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of physical energy, dealing 91 physical damage to the first target struck.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 117 damage over time and pinning the target.
3. **Ground Slam** — Rears up and slams the ground, dealing 65 damage in a 5-tile radius and staggering nearby targets.

## Lore
Few who study Crystal Spires & Geodes dispute that the glass serpent ranks among its defining hazards. This light wisp patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and its gems scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Best approached with ranged openers to whittle its HP before committing to melee; its fire weakness rewards elemental prep.
