## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Physical resistance of 35% and 10 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (high, -18%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Burrow Strike** — Submerges and resurfaces beneath the target, dealing 19 damage and applying a stagger.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 22 damage.

## Lore
Few who study Subterranean Caverns dispute that the mole horror ranks among its defining hazards. This rock elemental hunts in coordinated packs, flanking isolated targets, and its granite scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Burrow Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
