## Lore
The crow-familiar swarm is a mire beast native to Haunted Graveyards & Mire, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Haunted Graveyards has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its 39% stagger resistance means light weapons will struggle to interrupt its combo; heavier weapons are recommended.
