## Lore
Few who study Mutagenic Flesh-Pits dispute that the marrow horror ranks among its defining hazards. This chimeric beast swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its eldritch flesh scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Charge chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
