## Abilities
1. **Void Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of void energy, dealing 29 void damage to the first target struck.
2. **Suppressing Fire** — Lays down sustained fire at range, dealing 20 damage over time and pinning the target.

## Lore
Few who study Haunted Graveyards & Mire dispute that the quicksand horror ranks among its defining hazards. This grave construct ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its peat scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Suppressing Fire is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
