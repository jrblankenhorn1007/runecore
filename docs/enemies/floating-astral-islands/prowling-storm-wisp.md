## Lore
Few who study Floating Astral Sky Islands dispute that the storm wisp ranks among its defining hazards. This astral wraith lies dormant until disturbed, then attacks with full aggression, and its cloudstone scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Its scent detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
