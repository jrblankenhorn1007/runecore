## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 51% and 8 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire damage (high, -33%) and low stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Caustic Spray** — Expels a cone of caustic fluid, dealing 16 toxin damage per second for 3 seconds.
2. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 25 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Fieldnotes from Cryo-Glacier & Frost Peaks describe the rime hound as deceptively patient for a frozen revenant. It patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, using the terrain itself as a weapon against anyone careless enough to wander within its range.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Caustic Spray chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
