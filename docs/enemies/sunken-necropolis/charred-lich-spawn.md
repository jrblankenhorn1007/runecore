## Lore
Few who study Sunken Necropolis dispute that the lich spawn ranks among its defining hazards. This soul parasite breaks off and flees at low health to seek reinforcements, and its bone slate scarring is prized by hunters bold enough to bring back proof of the kill.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
