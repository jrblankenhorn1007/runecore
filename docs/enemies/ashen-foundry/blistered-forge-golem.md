## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 52% and 10 points of armor mitigation.

## Abilities
1. **Piercing Shriek** — Emits a disorienting shriek, reducing target accuracy and stagger resistance within 3 tiles.
2. **Static Discharge** — Releases a shock pulse dealing 15 shock damage and briefly disabling player abilities on hit.

## Lore
The forge golem did not always haunt Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
