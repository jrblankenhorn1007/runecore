## Lore
The ossuary crawler is a undead revenant native to Sunken Necropolis, patrols a fixed territory and rarely strays from its den. Survivors describe its presence as a sign that Sunken Necropolis has claimed another patrol, and scavengers who venture too deep rarely return with a full account of the encounter.

## Encounter Notes
Its electrical field sense detection makes stealth approaches unreliable; expect an aggressive opener when discovered.
