## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Frost resistance of 21% and 37 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Shock damage (very high, -40%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Enrage** — Below 30% HP, gains +25% attack speed and +15% movement speed for the rest of the encounter.
2. **Rending Tear** — Tears at the target ignoring 25% of armor and dealing 79 damage.
3. **Frost Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of frost energy, dealing 51 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.

## Lore
Local guides in Sunken Oceanic Trenches warn newcomers about the drowned revenant before anything else. As a deep leviathan, it swarms in numbers, overwhelming isolated targets through attrition, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Enrage chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
