## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Shock resistance of 55% and 82 points of armor mitigation; nearly immune to conventional small-arms fire.
- **Weaknesses:** Void damage (moderate, -13%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Shock Nova** — Releases an omnidirectional burst of shock energy, dealing 215 damage to all targets within 6 tiles.
2. **Charge** — Rushes the target in a straight line, dealing 264 physical damage and briefly staggering on hit.
3. **Death Curse** — On death, detonates dealing 188 damage in a 3-tile radius.
4. **Shock Lash** — Lashes out with a burst of shock energy, dealing 256 shock damage to the first target struck.

## Lore
The rogue ai sentinel did not always haunt Cyber-Wasteland Bunkers; older records suggest it was driven here by pressures from deeper zones. Now fully acclimated, it favors ranged harassment and repositions to avoid melee range, and expedition logs treat any sighting as cause for immediate caution.

## Encounter Notes
Solo players should use terrain to break line of sight when its Charge is on cooldown, then punish the recovery window.
