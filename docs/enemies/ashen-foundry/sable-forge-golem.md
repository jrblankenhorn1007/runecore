## Defenses & Weaknesses
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance of 60% and 20 points of armor mitigation.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost damage (very high, -45%) and moderate stagger threshold when isolated from allies.

## Abilities
1. **Bleeding Strike** — A precise strike that deals 36 damage and applies a stacking bleed over 6 seconds.
2. **Rally Cry** — Boosts nearby allied enemies' damage by 15% for 6 seconds.
3. **Grapple** — Latches onto the target, dealing 49 damage per second and preventing movement for 2 seconds.

## Lore
Local guides in Ashen Foundry & Slag Heaps warn newcomers about the forge golem before anything else. As a industrial drone, it ambushes stragglers from cover before retreating to regroup, and its resistance to common weaponry has made it a recurring entry in every survivor's field journal.

## Encounter Notes
Groups should focus it down quickly to avoid its Bleeding Strike chaining into a wider engagement with nearby patrols.
